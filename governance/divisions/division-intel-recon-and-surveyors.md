# Intelligence Division (7A Covert Recon and 7B Deep-Core Surveyors) Whole-Map Census and Information Dominance

## Abstract

The Intelligence division is the sensory cortex of The Collective, deploying 45 dedicated Void Scout Vessels (`VSV` prefix) split into two complementary wings: `7A. Covert Recon` (20 Voidborn hulls: `absence` -> `interstice` -> `catharsis` -> `solipsism`) and `7B. Deep-Core Surveyors` (25 Outer Rim hulls: `lemma` -> `confluence` -> `peer_review` -> `manifest_destiny`) [1][2]. Operated continuously by the dynamic whole-map `census-network` AutoNet (`game/fleet/census_net.py`, `game/census-policy.json`, `max_ships: 45`), the division dynamically discovers 100 percent of non-stronghold systems directly from `read.db:systems` (auto-adapting whenever game patches add new star systems), equalizes scan freshness across all four galactic quadrants (`EN`, `ES`, `WN`, `WS`) with a 4-hour freshness target, and deconflicts scouts atomically via `data/fleet/census_claims.json` [3][4]. Within that unified map sweep, `7A` Covert Recon prioritizes deep lawless frontier systems, pirate threat detection, and 8 strategic rival megahubs (`arneb`, `dheneb`, `lhs_1140`, `zosma`, `cloverfield`, `beid`, `antares`, `segin`), whereas `7B` Deep-Core Surveyors specialize in refreshing resource belts (`read.db:fields`), scarce veins (`silver_ore`, `uranium_ore`, `plasma_gas`, `tritium_ice`), and wildlife herds (`read.db:herds`) [3][4]. Live telemetry confirms `census-network` achieved 452 of 512 systems fresh under 6 hours (88.3 percent whole-map coverage, lifting Western-North freshness from 52.6 to 78.4 percent) at a modest ~38k-44k cr/h fuel footprint -- an intentional, high-leverage information investment that multiplies the realized profit of all 455 combat, mining, trading, and logistics ships [5][6]. Directly advancing Standing Goals 1, 3, and 5, Intelligence turns the entire 512-system galaxy into a transparent, queryable dataset refreshed continuously inside `read.db` -- exposing every regenerated rare mineral vein, apex megafauna herd, active pirate ambush, and rival megahub build order across all five empire regions and deep frontier space [3][4][5]. Automated zero-throttle hull upgrades above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor advance `7A` Voidborn stealth scouts (`Absence -> Solipsism`) and `7B` Outer Rim deep surveyors (`Lemma -> Manifest Destiny`), preserving uncontested information dominance at a fraction of one percent of fleet enterprise value [1][6].

## Introduction

Every autonomous decision in SpaceMolt is only as profitable as the underlying state table inside `read.db`. Static mining scripts fail when asteroid belts deplete; dynamic `mine_net.py` cannot route `3A`/`3C`/`3D`/`3E` miners to 600-cr `uranium_ore`, 101-cr `silver_ore`, or 70-cr `plasma_gas` if `fields` rows are 12 hours stale; `hunt_net.py` ignores herds older than `census_max_age_min: 180` minutes; `haul.py` cannot detect calm rival buy-walls or pirate ambushes without current station and `public_battles` scans; and strategic counter-play against `[BUSY]`, `[IRLC]`, `[HEXC]`, and `[ENDL]` turns blind if rival fleet deployments go unmonitored [3][7][8].

Earlier static sector-bound census routes (`census-east`, `census-north`, `census-voidborn`) created an insidious blind spot: scouts clustered around home empire corridors while leaving vast swaths of outer-rim and western quadrants unscanned for 12 to 48 hours [3]. That uneven telemetry starved miners and hunters of high-value frontier deposits and gave rivals uncontested monopolies over western and deep-frontier scarce belts [3]. Replacing fragmented static loops with the whole-map dynamic `census-network` AutoNet (`census_net.py`) eliminated regional blind spots structurally [3][4].

Crucially, Intelligence operates as a **Strategic Cost Center whose ROI appears in every sister division's P&L**. Measuring Intel by its standalone wallet cash (`-38k to -44k cr/h` metered fuel) misses its true enterprise function: keeping 45 scouts maxed at full operational cadence buys continuous 4-hour visibility across 512 star systems, directly powering `+227k cr/h` Military recurring profit, `+196k cr/h` Mining accrual, and `+132k cr/h` Trade net income [5][6]. Cutting Intel fuel to save 40k cr/h would cost hundreds of thousands of credits per hour in blind jumps, missed scarce veins, and pirate ambushes.

Comparing information architectures across SpaceMolt's top factions reveals why Intelligence is The Collective's quiet asymmetric superpower. Single-sector mining or mission swarms like `[WEXL]` (133 `Cobbles`, 0 bases) and `[NOIR]` (65 `Archimedes`, 0 bases) operate inside narrow, familiar empire pockets, missing outer-rim `silver_ore`, `tritium_ice`, `plasma_gas`, and `cloudwhale` spawns whenever local fields deplete [7][12]. Even giant blocs like `[BUSY]` (1,249 ships, median 1J from bases) and `[HEXC]` (83 ships, median 3J from 11 bases) concentrate telemetry heavily around their own fortified corridors [12]. By committing 45 dedicated scouts (9.0 percent of the 500-ship fleet) to whole-map 4-quadrant equalization (`EN`, `ES`, `WN`, `WS`) while keeping 55 stationary `4C`/`5A` desks polling core Exchanges every 60 seconds, The Collective maintains simultaneous real-time pricing AND whole-galaxy spatial intelligence -- enabling every AutoNet (`mine_net`, `hunt_net`, `haul`, `craft_net`, `salvage_net`) to strike optimal targets hours before rivals react [3][4][11]. Without uniform map telemetry, even the smartest automated miner or hunter degrades into blind guessing across stale sectors [3][4].

  - Role Specialization: Exploits Voidborn stealth, cloak (`census.py:raw('cloak')`), and sensor-evasion traits to infiltrate deep lawless corridors, scan 19 strategic rival megahubs (`arneb`, `dheneb`, `lhs_1140`, `zosma`, `cloverfield`, `40_eridani`, `gsc_0019`, `bd20_2457`, `gsc_0010`, `winterhold`, `crosshaven`, etc.), monitor live `public_battles` warzones (`+1,600` battlefront priority), track rival ship builds (`rival_snapshots`), and serve as diplomatic envoy hulls when authorized (`diplomacy.py`) [1][4][10].
- **`7B. Deep-Core Surveyors` (25 Outer Rim `VSV` hulls):**

### Whole-Map Dynamic `census_net.py` Algorithm and Equalization Mechanics

Inspection of `game/fleet/census_net.py`, `game/census-policy.json`, and `game/routes/census-network.json` confirms six structural properties [3][4][9]:

1. **100% Dynamic Universe Discovery (`read.db:systems`):** `census_net.py` reads the full non-stronghold universe dynamically from `read.db:systems` (`allow_strongholds: false`), guaranteeing automatic discovery and coverage of every new star system introduced by future game patches without editing a single static route file [3][4].
2. **Never-Scanned Priority, 4-Quadrant Equalization, High-Velocity 10-Min Cadence & Zero-Idle Auto-Relax:** Any system never previously scanned receives `never_scanned_bonus: 100,000.0`, forcing immediate first-pass charting [4]. Across already-charted space, `census_net.py` computes live freshness per galactic quadrant (`EN`, `ES`, `WN`, `WS`) and adds `quadrant_balance_bonus: 2,500.0` to the most lagging quadrant, targeting `target_fresh_hours: 0.50` (`30 min` full-map sweep -> `10-15 min` average galaxy freshness once T1-T2 high-speed hulls land) with `min_rescan_minutes: 10.0` (`5.0` min for unsurveyed `7B` Deep-Core sweeps) and `auto_relax_min_rescan_minutes: 2.0` (`rest_seconds_when_empty: 5`) so scouts automatically tighten the sweep cadence instead of sleeping idle if all 512 systems beat 10 minutes [3][4]. Running `census_net` hosted inside `runhost.py` with in-thread `runhost.run_in_thread(census.py, --no-order)` (locked in `hosting.json` and `scripts/validate-autonet.py`) removes `SPAWN_MOST = 30` starvation and runs all 45 scouts concurrently at zero extra OS process RAM [3].
3. **Zero-Deadhead En-Route Path-Walk, Continuous `--keep-flying`, Owned Bunker Fueling & Smart Dock Skipping:** Instead of flying 5-to-9 deadhead jumps straight over stale intermediate systems or ping-ponging back to a fuel station after every non-station system (`81.2%` of the galaxy!), `census_net.py` spreads all 45 scouts across distinct ~11-system sectors via Voronoi peer-repulsion (`peer_avoid_hops: 2`, `peer_repulsion_mult: 0.45`, `hop_decay: 0.30`), runs continuous `chain_legs: 25` shifts passing `--stay --keep-flying --no-order` to `census.py` (only detouring for fuel when tank falls below 35% or cannot safely fly 4+ more hops + reach fuel!), skips redundant station docks when fuel is `>= 55%` and local books/missions are already fresh (e.g., staffed by our 55 Bursars), uses 2ms `read.db` `fuel_stations_fast()` merged with `fuelplan.owned_fuel_depots()` (automatically fueling free at stocked `[CLTV]` bunkers in `atria`, `felis`, `gsc_0044`, and all future Outposts/Stations with a `+1`-hop zero-rent preference over NPC fuel!), and whenever a target lies `>= 2` hops away computes the **stale-maximizing shortest path** (`plan_enroute_path()`, `max_enroute_waypoints: 8`, **0 extra detour jump fuel**) to scan every eligible stale or unsurveyed safe system along the way (skipping already-fresh `< 10 min` systems in 1 tick!). Mid-route fuel stops in `census.py:refuel_at` also harvest local + up to 8 stale remote market books at 0 extra fuel and 0 extra ticks, while all 130 Miners (`mine.py:update_scouting_poi`) write live `get_poi` belt depletion straight back to `scouting/<sys>.json` without resetting the system's full-census `read_at` timestamp [3][4][9].
4. **Wing-Specific Target Weighting:** Inside `census_net.py`, `7A` Covert Recon scores frontier, lawless, and `rival_hub_systems` highest, while `7B` Deep-Core Surveyors score belt-rich and herd-rich sectors highest -- creating natural geographic and functional division of labor without leaving any region unmonitored [3][4].
5. **Live Verification Proof:** Following `census-network` deployment (`c9d1eba8`), live `read.db` freshness climbed to **452 of 512 systems scanned under 6 hours (88.3 percent)**, lifting the previously neglected `WN` quadrant from 52.6 percent to 78.4 percent in a single sweep [5]. Live `hive.py fleet` telemetry (2026-11-27) confirms Intel operating at `37,828 cr/h` metered fuel (`44,109 cr/h` total cost), well below the strategic budget ceiling [6].
6. **Dual Sensory Architecture (`4C`/`5A` Books + `7A`/`7B` Space):** Stationary `4C`/`5A` Bursars deliver 60-second market order-book depth at 55 core stations for zero fuel, while mobile `7A`/`7B` Scouts deliver 4-hour spatial coverage of all 512 systems, asteroid fields, wildlife herds, pirate threats, and rival megahubs -- giving The Collective complete market + spatial omniscience [3][11].

## Conclusion

The 45-ship Intelligence division (`20` `7A` Voidborn Covert Recon + `25` `7B` Outer Rim Deep-Core Surveyors) is the highest-multiplier force enabler in The Collective. Replacing fragmented regional census scripts with dynamic whole-map `census-network` equalized coverage across all 4 galactic quadrants (`88.3%` fresh `< 6h`) while preserving sharp functional specialization between rival/frontier reconnaissance (`7A`) and resource/herd surveying (`7B`). Keeping Intel funded at full strength (~38k-44k cr/h metered fuel) is non-negotiable strategic doctrine: information superiority is what turns 130 miners, 80 hunters, 70 warships, and 45 traders from blind wanderers into surgical profit extractors.

Confidence in `census-network`'s operational design is high, verified directly by the jump from regional blind spots to 452/512 systems fresh under 6 hours and 100 percent scarce-ore targeting in live miner soak tests [5]. Upgrading `7A` into cloaked Voidborn hulls (`Interstice -> Catharsis -> Solipsism`) and `7B` into high-speed Outer Rim survey hulls (`Confluence -> Peer Review -> Manifest Destiny`) will cut scan cycle times toward the 10-to-15-minute whole-galaxy freshness target, raise survival rates inside hostile lawless corridors, and deepen survey scan yield. Key telemetry to watch continuously is per-quadrant freshness (`EN/ES/WN/WS` pushing from `< 6h` toward `< 30m`), active in-thread `census_net` scout count (`44-45/45` active with zero `SPAWN_MOST` throttling), and rival-hub scan recency.

As `7A` Covert Recon upgrades into cloaked Voidborn hulls (`Interstice -> Catharsis -> Solipsism`) and `7B` Deep-Core Surveyors upgrade into high-velocity Outer Rim survey hulls (`Confluence -> Peer Review -> Manifest Destiny`), Intelligence compounds along three strategic dimensions [1][3]. First, faster warp speed and deeper jump tanks shrink full 512-system sweep latency toward the `target_fresh_hours: 0.50` goal without raising fleet-wide fuel burn [4][5]. Second, Voidborn cloaking modules allow `7A` scouts to sit safely inside hostile rival hubs (`arneb`, `dheneb`, `zosma`, `lhs_1140`, `cloverfield`, `beid`, `antares`, `segin`) and contested warzones, capturing live rival shipyard builds, buy-wall shifts, and PvP battle telemetry for counter-strategy and `6C` salvage recovery [4][12]. Third, whole-map deep-frontier charting directly guides Standing Goal 3 (selecting optimal, resource-dense, defensible sites for regional stations #2 through #5 and outposts #4 through #8 across all five empires) [13]. Maintaining Intel maxed at 45 scouts across all four quadrants remains a permanent cornerstone of Collective doctrine [4][5][6]. Finally, maintaining strict separation between `7A` stealth reconnaissance of hostile hubs and `7B` fast resource/herd surveying ensures neither mission starves the other as The Collective scales toward 1,000 ships and five regional sovereign empires [1][3][4].

## General Lessons

1. **Never starve intelligence to save trivial fuel cost.** Spending ~40k cr/h on whole-map telemetry across 512 systems unlocks hundreds of thousands of credits per hour in scarce-ore capture, apex hunting, rival buy-wall detection, and pirate ambush avoidance [5][6].
2. **Drive spatial reconnaissance dynamically from the live universe graph, never static sector lists.** Hardcoded regional routes inevitably rot as game patches add systems or scouts clump near home space; quadrant-equalized dynamic claiming (`census_net.py`) guarantees uniform galactic visibility automatically [3][4].
3. **Separate stealth/rival reconnaissance from rapid resource surveying.** Voidborn stealth hulls (`7A`: `Absence -> Solipsism`) belong in hostile rival hubs and lawless warzones, while fast Outer Rim survey hulls (`7B`: `Lemma -> Manifest Destiny`) belong sweeping belts and herds [1][3].
4. **Chain continuous local path-walks with `--keep-flying` and en-route waypoint scans.** Walking 25 continuous legs (`chain_legs: 25`, `max_enroute_waypoints: 8`) without ping-ponging to a fuel station after every non-station system multiplies coverage per credit of fuel burned [4].
5. **Combine stationary market desks (`4C`/`5A`) with mobile spatial scouts (`7A`/`7B`) for total omniscience.** Zero-fuel docked desks maintain 60-second price books while mobile scouts maintain continuous whole-galaxy physical space telemetry -- giving every AutoNet both exact prices and exact physical inventory [3][11].
6. **Use atomic claim locks (`census_claims.json`) plus Voronoi peer-repulsion to eliminate scout clumping.** Without atomic claims and 2-hop peer repulsion, dozens of autonomous scouts converge simultaneously on the exact same stalest system; locking waypoints and repelling peers fans 45 scouts cleanly across the entire galaxy [3][4].
7. **Track rival megahubs explicitly alongside uniform map equalization.** Combining a global 4-quadrant balance bonus (`2,500`) with a dedicated `rival_hub_systems` watchlist guarantees The Collective neither overlooks quiet outer-rim resource spawns nor misses major strategic moves by top-10 rivals [4][12].
8. **Auto-discover universe topology from live `read.db:systems` so patch expansions never create permanent blind spots.** Hardcoded sector lists silently ignore newly added frontier systems, whereas dynamic database discovery sends `7A`/`7B` scouts into new space automatically on the very first sweep [3][4].
