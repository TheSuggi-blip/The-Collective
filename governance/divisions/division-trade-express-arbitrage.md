# Express Arbitrage Wing (4A) Operations, Multi-Hub Spreads, and Anti-Trap Safeguards

## Abstract

Wing `4A. Express Arbitrage` is The Collective's 12-ship high-speed commercial trading spearhead, manned exclusively by Outer Rim Merchant Courier Vessels (`MCV` prefix) designed to capture spatial price spreads across all 5 empire regions and calm rival buy-walls [1][2]. Progressing along the fastest Outer Rim courier-trader ladder (`runner` -> `dust_devil` -> `syndicate` -> `macguffin`), `4A` mirrors the highest-velocity trading hulls fielded by top rivals `[IRLC]` (47 `Syndicate` couriers) and `[HIVE]` (54 `Runner` express shuttles) [1][3]. Operated autonomously by `haul.py` under `haul-network-trade` and `game/haul-policy.json`, `4A` is mechanically locked out of stationary Bursar desks (`bursar-network` excludes `4A.`) and zero-fuel crafting (`craft-network` excludes `4A.`), keeping 100 percent of its 12 express hulls flying high-margin multi-hub arbitrage and high-value surplus liquidation [4][5]. Every trade edge passes through strict anti-manipulation filters powered by `market_guard.py`: net-external volume checking (`load_cltv_buy_qty`), `is_mirage_arbitrage_trap()` blocking fake high-ask/high-bid wash traps, real top-of-book bid depth validation, and optimal multi-hauler job matching (`assign_optimal_arbitrage`) that prevents two couriers from racing for the same spread [6][7]. With live Trade net profit hitting `+132,467 cr/h` (`292,380 cr/h` gross income in the latest hour), `4A` converts galactic information advantages created by `4C` Bursars and `7A/7B` Intel into immediate, compounding cash flow [8]. Advancing Standing Goals 1, 4, and 5, Wing `4A` monetizes real-time regional price dislocations across all five empires and calm frontier citadels without burning fuel on blind speculation [1][6]. Upgrading automatically with zero artificial throttle above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor (`Runner -> Dust Devil -> Syndicate -> MacGuffin`), all 12 Outer Rim couriers convert high-value modules, exotic Hunter drops, rare minerals, and manufactured components into immediate IFRS 15 Section 1 external cash profit while eliminating wash trades, mirage traps, and spread-collision races mechanically [1][6][7].

## Introduction

In SpaceMolt's decentralized multi-station economy, prices diverge sharply across systems because individual stations maintain isolated local order books and players face real jump-fuel and transit-time costs. A module, biological drop, refined component, or rare ore listed at 120 cr in one core station often trades against a standing 240 cr buy-wall 4 to 8 jumps away at a major manufacturing hub or rival citadel (`dheneb`, `arneb`, `haven`, `sol`, `nexus_prime`) [3][9]. Rival telemetry proves how lucrative fast courier trading is at scale: `[IRLC]` operates 47 T3 `Syndicate` express traders alongside 32 `Frankenhauler` freighters to generate `+4.22M cr/h` in faction wealth, while `[HIVE]` runs 54 `Runner` shuttles to extract `+685k cr/h` with zero combat ships [3].

Express arbitrage, however, is notoriously dangerous for naive automated traders because order books in an open multiplayer market contain deliberate traps. Three classic exploits wipe out careless trade bots: (1) **mirage arbitrage traps**, where a manipulator posts both an inflated 800-cr sell order and a thin 1,200-cr buy order at a distant station, cancelling the buy order mid-flight so the bot buys overpriced junk at 800 cr; (2) **CLTV self-wash loops**, where a fleet courier buys goods and sells them directly into one of its own Bursar's open buy orders, burning 5 percent exchange tax and jump fuel for zero net gain; and (3) **spread-collision races**, where four couriers detect the exact same 20-unit bid simultaneously and three arrive after the first fills the book [6][7].

Wing `4A. Express Arbitrage` (12 Outer Rim `MCV` ships, 26.7 percent of Trade) solves both speed and safety simultaneously. By isolating 12 fast Outer Rim hulls on `haul-network-trade`, equipping them with high-velocity courier hulls (`Runner -> Dust Devil -> Syndicate -> MacGuffin`), and guarding every arbitrage edge with `market_guard.py`'s Cash-Weighted Median fair-price oracle, `load_cltv_buy_qty` net external depth, and `assign_optimal_arbitrage()` atomic claims, `4A` harvests real external spreads cleanly around the clock [1][6][7].

The unit economics of express courier trading explain why 12 fast Outer Rim vessels generate disproportionate returns per credit of invested hull capital. Unlike bulk ore haulers that depend on massive cargo holds to move 5-cr commodities, an express trader carrying 40 units of high-value modules, `superconductor` electronics, rare biologicals (`irradiated_marrow`, `caustic_sac`), or `silver_ore`/`uranium_ore` captures 50 to 500 cr of pure spread per unit -- realizing 2,000 to 20,000 cr net profit on a single fast multi-jump dash [6][9]. Rival benchmarks confirm the exact hull meta: `[IRLC]` fields 47 T3 `Syndicate` couriers as the speed layer of its `+4.22M cr/h` trade machine, `[HIVE]` flies 54 T1 `Runner` couriers to harvest regional spreads cheaply, and `[NOIR]` pairs multi-hub station trading with rapid exchange turnover [3]. Giving The Collective's 12 `4A` pilots Outer Rim birth bonuses and a dedicated `Runner -> Dust Devil -> Syndicate -> MacGuffin` progression ladder ensures we match and surpass the fastest rival couriers in the galaxy [1][2].

## Findings

### Roster, Express Hull Ladder, and Mechanical Wing Isolation

Exact verification of `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/routes/*.json` establishes `4A`'s architecture [1][2][4]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 12 Outer Rim `MCV` hulls (`4A. Express Arbitrage`), leveraging Outer Rim transit-speed and smuggling/evasion advantages [1][2].
- **Hull Progression Ladder:** `runner` (T1, `<= 52k`) -> `dust_devil` (T2, `<= 240k`) -> `syndicate` (T3, `<= 900k`) -> `macguffin` (T4, `<= 2.8M`) [1]. Upgrades run automatically via `upgrades.py` whenever treasury exceeds 2,000,000 cr and live shipyards list stock [1].
- **Strict Dispatcher Separation:** `bursar-network.json` restricts docked desk duty to `wing_prefix: ["4C.", "5A."]`, and `craft-network.json` explicitly sets `exclude_wing_prefix: ["4A.", "4B.", "4C.", "5A.", "6B.", "6C."]`, locking all 12 `4A` couriers strictly into mobile commercial trading (`haul-network-trade`) and fitting (`fit-network`) [4][5].

### Optimal Matching, Anti-Manipulation Defenses, and Calm Buy-Wall Access

`haul.py` executes `4A` trading through four rigorous algorithmic safeguards [6][7]:

1. **Global Optimal Multi-Ship Assignment (`assign_optimal_arbitrage`):** Rather than greedy per-ship picking that lets a far ship steal a close ship's best edge, `haul.py` loads all active `4A`/`4B` peer states (`load_available_haulers`), computes all feasible `(origin_ask, dest_bid)` edges across fresh `read.db:books` rows, scores net profit after 5 percent market fees (`unit_cost = ask_p * 1.05`) and jump fuel, and solves global peer-to-edge assignment atomically [6]. Atomic keys distinguish thin bids (`arb-bid:<base>:<item>`, 1 hauler max) from deep external buy-walls (`>= 500` qty and `>= bw_min_bid`, keyed per `(origin, dest, item)` as `arb-bw:` so multiple haulers can drain parallel origins into a massive buy-wall without deadlock) [6].
2. **Mirage Trap & Lowball Liquidation Guards:** Every candidate arbitrage edge checks `market_guard.is_mirage_arbitrage_trap(item, ask_p, bid_p, fair_price)` against the rolling LULD TWAP fair-price oracle (`price_oracle.json`), rejecting inflated bait-ask traps where an ask sits far above real market fair value even if a spoofed distant bid appears higher [6][7]. Likewise, surplus liquidation (`>= 20 cr` fair items) refuses any scam bid below 70 percent of oracle fair price [6].
3. **Net-External `load_cltv_buy_qty` Wiring:** Both `assign_optimal_arbitrage()` and `pick_surplus_liquidation()` subtract strictly The Collective's own open buy quantities (`load_cltv_buy_qty`) rather than blinding the whole `(base, item)` pair [6][7]. This simultaneously prevents self-wash trading into our own Bursars AND preserves full visibility of giant external rival buy-walls (such as `[BUSY]`'s 468,000-unit `silver_ore` wall at 101 cr) even when a CLTV Bursar has a small bid parked at the same hub [7].
4. **Calm Lawless Buy-Wall Penetration:** While ordinary arbitrage defaults to safe hubs, `allow_calm_buywall_dest: true` authorizes `4A` express couriers to dash into calm, pirate-free lawless buy-walls whenever external bid price (`>= 50 cr`) and depth (`>= 200` units) justify the sprint -- capturing rival faction citadels like `dheneb` (`[HEXC]`) and `arneb` (`[BUSY]`) safely [6][9].

## General Lessons

1. **Speed and cargo volume are distinct trading specializations.** Mixing fast small-hold couriers (`4A`) and slow massive-hold freighters (`4B`) under a single hull choice compromises both: couriers win thin high-value spreads by arriving first, whereas freighters win deep commodity buy-walls by amortizing jump fuel across thousands of units [1][6].
2. **Every arbitrage engine requires explicit anti-trap defenses.** In an adversarial market, the widest apparent spread on the screen is frequently a bait-ask mirage trap or a self-wash loop. Checking `is_mirage_arbitrage_trap()` against a multi-station LULD fair-price oracle and subtracting own open buy volume (`load_cltv_buy_qty`) turns arbitrage from a trap magnet into reliable compounding income [6][7].
3. **Solve multi-agent trade dispatch globally, not greedily.** Atomic `assign_optimal_arbitrage()` matching with distinct keys for thin bids (`arb-bid:`) versus deep buy-walls (`arb-bw:`) eliminates spread-collision races while still allowing parallel harvesting of massive 500+-unit rival buy-walls [6].
4. **Information infrastructure compounds trading returns.** `4A` couriers do not generate spreads alone; they monetize the 60-second order-book telemetry produced by `4C` Bursars and rival-hub recon produced by `7A` Scouts [10][11].
5. **Enforce wing isolation mechanically in route `requires`.** Excluding `4A.` from `bursar-network` and `craft-network` guarantees that expensive upgraded `Dust Devil` and `Syndicate` couriers never stall on zero-speed stationary desk duty [4][5].
6. **Price exact exchange fees (`5%`) and jump fuel into every arbitrage edge before takeoff.** Naive spread calculators comparing raw `bid > ask` lose money steadily on 4-percent spreads after 5-percent market fees (`unit_cost = ask_p * 1.05`) and transit fuel; enforcing strict post-fee, post-fuel net profit thresholds protects compounding cash flow [6].
7. **Target calm rival buy-walls selectively to harvest opponent wealth directly.** Allowing fast `4A` couriers to dump high-value goods (`>= 50 cr`, `>= 200` qty) straight into pirate-free rival megahubs (`[BUSY]` at `arneb`, `[HEXC]` at `dheneb`) turns rival industrial demand into a direct cash ATM for The Collective [6][9].
