# Ice Harvesters Wing (3E) Operations, Fuel Sovereignty, and Cryogenic Economics

## Abstract

Wing `3E. Ice Harvesters` is The Collective's 24-ship cryogenic extraction wing, staffed by Solarian Enterprise Utility Vessels (`EUV` prefix) dedicated to harvesting `water_ice`, `methane_ice`, `tritium_ice`, `nitrogen_ice`, `ammonia_ice`, and exotic frozen compounds across 160+ icy belts [1][2]. Operating under `mine-network` (`game/fleet/mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json`), `3E` follows the dedicated Solarian cryogenic hull ladder (`glacius` -> `hoarfrost` -> `cryogenic_survey` -> `cryo_industrial`), fitted exclusively with ice harvesters via `fit-network` [1][3]. Strategically, `3E` performs two vital enterprise functions simultaneously: it guarantees **fuel and hospitality sovereignty** -- supplying the raw `water_ice`, `methane_ice`, and `tritium_ice` refined into `fuel_cell`, `purified_water`, and station consumables for our 500-ship fleet (burning ~200,000-255,000 cr/h in metered fuel) and Collective Resort Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free facilities -- and it harvests scarce frontier ices (`tritium_ice`, `methane_ice`) that trade at high external margins [4][5][6]. Live field telemetry reveals strong lawless concentration across every valuable ice tier: 84.6 percent of `water_ice` belts (132 lawless / 24 policed), 77.8 percent of `methane_ice` belts (7/2), and 100 percent of `tritium_ice` belts (5/0) lie in lawless space [4]. Powered by soft lawless weighting (1.35x lawless vs 0.75x safe-zone), fuel-optimal 6-to-8-jump radii, adaptive 1-to-3 ship belt caps, and `bursar-policy.json` fuel-trickle refining (`fuel_trickle_target: 3000`), `3E` turns ice harvesting into both a direct profit source and an internal operating-cost shield. Advancing Standing Goals 1, 3, and 4, Wing `3E` directly attacks The Collective's largest recurring cash outflow (~200k-255k cr/h metered fuel across 500 ships) while simultaneously harvesting scarce lawless `tritium_ice` and `methane_ice` for commercial Bursar liquidation [4][6][7]. All 24 Solarian cryogenic hulls upgrade automatically with zero per-pass throttle above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor (`Glacius -> Hoarfrost -> Cryogenic Survey -> Cryo Industrial`), reporting transparent intersegment production credit inside IFRS 8 Section 5 while feeding `5A` refineries and `6A`/`6C` bunker tankers [1][12][15].

## Introduction

Fuel is the single largest recurring cash expense of any active 500-ship SpaceMolt fleet. Live telemetry (`hive.py fleet`, 2026-11-27) measures fleet-wide metered fuel expenditure between 200,435 cr/h (24-hour average) and 254,844 cr/h (1-hour active expansion average) across 448+ active vessels -- more than 4.8 million credits per day if purchased entirely at NPC retail prices [6]. Furthermore, operating owned sovereign stations (`Collective Resort Ridgecrest` and future 5-empire regional stations plus 8 outposts) consumes continuous `purified_water`, `fuel_cell`, and hospitality beverages (`station_seltzer`, `solarian_biotic`, `nebula_stellar_vintage`) [5][7].

Wing `3E. Ice Harvesters` (24 ships, 18.5 percent of Mining) attacks this enterprise cost structure at the root while capturing scarce cryogenic margins. Just like gas siphoning, ice extraction requires specialized ice-harvester modules (`ice_harvester_*`) and rewards purpose-built cryogenic hulls (`glacius`, `hoarfrost`, `cryogenic_survey`, `cryo_industrial`) [1][2]. Ordinary mining lasers cannot extract ice belts, creating a natural supply moat around `methane_ice`, `tritium_ice`, and high-volume `water_ice` [2][8]. Rivals such as `[IRLC]` and `[HEXC]` co-locate sovereign bases directly inside rich ice corridors so their refineries convert harvested ice into cheap internal fuel cells and station upkeep while selling surplus fuel and exotic cryogenic isotopes to passing players [9].

For The Collective, balancing `3E` requires solving a classic dual-product trade-off: never letting common `nitrogen_ice` or `water_ice` clog lockboxes into an unsellable glut (hence `bursar-policy.json` excludes `water_ice` and `nitrogen_ice` from external cash buys while `mine_net.py` applies saturation decay), while ensuring `tritium_ice`, `methane_ice`, and fuel-refining `water_ice` deficits always flow smoothly into Collective Resort Ridgecrest and our 5-empire deposit hubs [7][8][10].

Viewing fuel through an industrial value-investor lens reveals why `3E. Ice Harvesters` is a structural moat rather than just another mining sub-wing. Every active competitor in SpaceMolt pays fuel on every jump; factions running large mobile fleets without integrated cryogenic refining must purchase retail `fuel_cell` supplies continuously from NPC stations or player Exchanges, surrendering both spread and supply security during regional conflicts [6][9]. Even `[HIVE]`, despite running `+2,152 crafts/h` at docked hubs, lacks sovereign cryogenic stations to support a 500-to-1,000-ship multi-region grand fleet, whereas `[IRLC]` and `[HEXC]` anchor their 8-to-11-base empires directly onto self-sustaining fuel and industrial corridors [9]. Furthermore, The Collective's flagship Collective Resort Ridgecrest (`13` active facilities) and future `6B` Passenger & Resort Liners require uninterrupted `purified_water` and hospitality beverages derived directly from cryogenic ice [5][7]. Dedicating 24 Solarian hulls exclusively to `3E` secures both fleet mobility and resort margins. Balancing sovereign fuel autonomy with scarce `tritium_ice` capture turns cryogenic extraction into a lasting competitive moat [4][6].

## Findings

### Roster, Cryogenic Hull Progression, and Tool Specialization

Exact verification of `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/fleet/mine_net.py` confirms `3E`'s structural blueprint [1][2][8]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 24 Solarian `EUV` hulls (`3E. Ice Harvesters`), representing 18.5 percent of the 130-ship Mining division [1][2].
- **Solarian Cryogenic Ladder:** `glacius` (T1) -> `hoarfrost` (T2) -> `cryogenic_survey` (T3) -> `cryo_industrial` (T4) [1]. All 24 hulls auto-commission through `upgrades.py` whenever treasury exceeds 2,000,000 cr and live shipyards list stock [1].
- **Module & Wing Locking:** `fit-network` equips `3E` with ice harvesters and defensive shields/capacitors mapped via `read.db:items.slot` [3]. Inside `mine_net.py`, `pilot_tools()` detects fitted ice harvesters and limits belt scoring exclusively to `resource_tool_kind(res) == "ice"`, while `_wing_mine_multiplier("3E.", res, tools)` prioritizes high-value cryogenic targets (`tritium_ice`, `methane_ice`, `water_ice` deficits) cleanly without colliding with ore (`3A/3B/3C`) or gas (`3D`) wings [8].

### Empirical Geography, Range, Caps, and Anti-Glut Controls

Live `read.db:fields` inspection (2026-11-27) combined with `game/mine-policy.json` reveals the exact spatial and economic mechanics of `3E` [4][8][10]:

1. **Lawless Ice Dominance:** Lawless space contains 100 percent of known `tritium_ice` fields (5 lawless / 0 policed), 77.8 percent of `methane_ice` fields (7/2), and 84.6 percent of `water_ice` fields (132/24) [4]. Replacing `policed_only: true` with soft lawless preference (**1.35x lawless bonus** vs **0.75x safe-zone penalty**) plus pirate-sighting avoidance (`pirate_avoid_hours: 6.0`, `pirate_battle_avoid_hours: 2.0`) opens 144+ rich lawless ice fields directly to `3E` while preserving opportunistic policed access [8][10].
2. **Fuel-Optimal Tiered Radii:** Standard abundant ices (`water_ice`, `nitrogen_ice`) are bounded strictly to **6 jumps** (`max_station_jumps: 5`) so ice harvesters never burn more fuel hauling ice than the refined fuel cells generate [8][10]. High-value scarce ices (`tritium_ice`, `methane_ice` `>= 60 cr/u` or open `needs.tsv` fuel/station deficits) unlock **8 jumps**, with **10 jumps** reserved strictly if any exotic ice crosses `>= 100 cr/u` real external fair value [8][10].
3. **Adaptive Belt Crowding & Depletion Floors:** Rich `>= 10,000`-unit `water_ice` belts support up to **3 concurrent `3E` ships** (`max_per_belt: 3`, `units_per_ship_run: 400`), mid-size `1,000-9,999`-unit belts cap at **2 ships**, and scarce `< 3%` `tritium_ice` or `methane_ice` pockets hard-cap at **1 ship** [8][10]. Ordinary ice belts respect the **5.0 percent** regeneration floor, while scarce high-value ices or open fuel deficits drop to **`scarce_min_left_pct: 0.2%`** [8][10].
4. **Anti-Glut & Fuel-Chain Integration:** `mine_net.py` applies `skip_full_reserves: true` and `hard_glut_skip_units: 200000` so `3E` never hoards useless `nitrogen_ice` [8][10]. Simultaneously, `bursar-policy.json` excludes `water_ice` and `nitrogen_ice` from external buy orders while enabling `craft.fuel_trickle_target: 3000` so docked `5A` Refiners and `4C` Bursars continuously refine harvested ice into `fuel_cell` and `purified_water` at zero transit fuel [7].

### Cross-Division Synergy

`3E` sits at the root of the entire fleet's energy and hospitality logistics tree:
- **Intel (`7B` Deep-Core Surveyors) -> `3E`:** Whole-map 4-hour census scans monitor all 5 `tritium_ice` and 9 `methane_ice` sites across the galaxy so `3E` strikes immediately upon belt regeneration [11].
- **`3E` -> Craft (`5A` Station Refiners & `5B` Field Workshops):** Harvested `water_ice`, `methane_ice`, and `tritium_ice` convert directly into `fuel_cell`, `purified_water`, reactor fuel, and resort hospitality drinks (`station_seltzer`, `solarian_biotic`, `void_espresso`) at Ridgecrest's rent-free refineries [5][7][12].
- **`3E` -> Logistics (`6A` Bulk Haulers & `6C` Fuel Tankers):** Refined `fuel_cell` output is hauled by `6A` and `6C` directly into owned outpost fuel bunkers (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`, `ridgecrest`), cutting the 200k-255k cr/h fleet fuel bill structurally [6][13].
- **`3E` -> Trade (`4C` Bursars):** Surplus `methane_ice` and `tritium_ice` above internal crafting needs sell into external multi-hub demand above `safe_sell_floor` (72 percent of oracle fair price) [7][14].

## Conclusion

Wing `3E. Ice Harvesters` is the sovereign energy anchor of The Collective's 500-ship fleet. By pairing 24 Solarian cryogenic hulls (`Glacius -> Hoarfrost -> Cryogenic Survey -> Cryo Industrial`) with lawless ice access (100 percent of `tritium_ice`, 78 percent of `methane_ice`, 85 percent of `water_ice`), tight 6-jump standard fuel discipline, and direct handoffs into zero-fuel `5A` refineries and `6A/6C` bunker logistics, `3E` attacks the fleet's single largest recurring expense (fuel) while generating cash from scarce frontier ices.

Confidence in `3E`'s operational design is high: live field distributions confirm abundant nearby lawless ice near owned hubs, `bursar-policy.json` aligns buy exclusions and `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` directly with `3E` output, and IFRS 8 handover parity credits `3E` transparently inside Section 5 Mining production [4][7][15]. The decisive operational milestone ahead is scaling `3E` hulls into T2 `Hoarfrost` and T3 `Cryogenic Survey` alongside Collective Resort Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free facilities so internally refined `fuel_cell` production covers a rising share of the fleet's 200k+ cr/h fuel burn. If live refinery telemetry shows `water_ice` or `methane_ice` bottlenecking `fuel_cell` or `purified_water` output during 5-station expansion, raising `needs.tsv` priority on those ices will immediately focus all 24 `3E` hulls on the shortfall without manual intervention.

Over the next progression phase (`Glacius -> Hoarfrost -> Cryogenic Survey -> Cryo Industrial`), `3E`'s economic leverage compounds alongside Collective Resort Ridgecrest and our planned five-station, eight-outpost territorial footprint (Standing Goal 3) [1][5]. Each hull tier jump raises cryogenic extraction rate and cargo capacity, allowing 24 ships to cover both growing internal fuel/hospitality demand for 500 (and eventually 1,000) vessels AND high-margin external sales of scarce `tritium_ice` and `methane_ice` [1][4][8]. Because `mine_net.py` caps ordinary `water_ice` strictly at 6 jumps (`max_station_jumps: 5`) while allowing 8 jumps for scarce cryogenic deposits (`>= 60 cr/u` or open fuel deficits), `3E` maintains strictly positive net energy return on every sortie [8][10]. Key falsifiable indicators confirming `3E` success are declining external retail fuel spend as a percentage of total fleet jumps, `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` staying green across Ridgecrest and core hubs, zero hospitality water stockouts at owned stations, and consistent capture of all 5 lawless `tritium_ice` fields [4][6][7]. Anchored by 24 empire-native Solarian cryogenic hulls, tight 6-jump net-energy limits on `water_ice`, and direct refining handoffs into Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free facilities, Wing `3E` converts ice belts directly into lower fleet-wide fuel burn and higher compounding enterprise profit [1][5][7][8]. Pairing `3E` cryogenic output directly with `6A`/`6C` own-cell bunker logistics guarantees uninterrupted frontier mobility across all five empire corridors [7][13].

## General Lessons

1. **Internalize the feedstock of your largest recurring operating cost.** When fuel accounts for over 75 percent of recurring cash burn (~200k-255k cr/h), owning a dedicated ice-to-fuel-cell supply chain (`3E -> 5A -> 6A/6C`) creates a durable structural cost advantage over rivals buying retail fuel [6][7].
2. **Enforce strict net-energy discipline on fuel-feedstock harvesters.** Flying 10 jumps to mine cheap `water_ice` can consume more fuel in transit than the cargo yields when refined. Capping ordinary ice at 6 jumps (`max_station_jumps: 5`) guarantees positive net energy return on every sortie [8][10].
3. **Treat dual-use commodities differently from pure cash crops.** `water_ice` is both low-price on open markets and mission-critical internally for fuel and station hospitality. Using `needs.tsv` and `craft-demand.json` tiers lets `3E` keep internal bins full without dumping low-margin `water_ice` onto saturated exchanges [8][10].
4. **Open lawless access safely where scarcity concentrates.** All 5 known `tritium_ice` belts and nearly 80 percent of `methane_ice` belts lie outside empire space; combining soft lawless preference with 6-hour pirate-sighting and 2-hour battle avoidance captures high-margin cryogenic yields safely [4][10].
5. **Pair mining output directly with automated station refining.** Setting `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` on docked `5A`/`4C` desks ensures harvested ice converts smoothly into fungible fuel cells at zero extra transit cost [7].
6. **Align buy-wall exclusions with internal cryogenic output to stop treasury leakage.** Keeping `water_ice` and `nitrogen_ice` explicitly inside `bursar-policy.json:exclude_buy_items` prevents 55 stationary Bursars from spending cash purchasing common ices that 24 `3E` harvesters supply internally at fuel cost [7].
7. **Balance energy security against warehouse clutter via saturation decay.** Allowing `3E` to fill internal `fuel_cell` and `purified_water` deficits at top priority while letting `skip_full_reserves` and `hard_glut_skip_units` redirect spare capacity into scarce `tritium_ice` and `methane_ice` captures both cost savings and cash profit [8][10].
8. **Keep cryogenic tools strictly isolated on dedicated Solarian ice hulls.** Mixing ice harvesters with ore lasers dilutes extraction speed per tick; dedicated `3E` hulls maximize yield across both abundant `water_ice` and scarce `tritium_ice` [1][8].
