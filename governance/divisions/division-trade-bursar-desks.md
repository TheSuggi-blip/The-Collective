# Pure Bursar Desks Wing (4C) Operations, Market-Making, and Zero-Fuel Order-Book Telemetry

## Abstract

Wing `4C. Pure Bursar Desks` is The Collective's 25-ship stationary commercial trading and market-making network, manned by 22 Nebula and 3 Outer Rim Merchant Courier Vessels (`MCV` prefix) flying zero-fuel docked `congregation` hulls across major Empire and calm neutral Exchanges [1][2]. Working hand-in-hand with `5A. Station Refiners & Bursars` (30 ships) on `bursar-network` (`max_ships: 55`, governed by `game/fleet/bursar.py` and `game/bursar-policy.json`), `4C` ensures that 55+ core Exchange stations across all 5 empire regions stay permanently staffed at zero fuel burn [3][4]. Every 60 seconds (`poll_interval_s: 60`), each docked Bursar executes four high-leverage commercial duties simultaneously: (1) refreshing live local market order books (`read.db:books`) and mission boards (`read.db:missions`) with 60-second precision; (2) posting anti-manipulation guarded wholesale buy orders (`max_escrow_per_station: 250,000 cr`, capped at 90 percent of fair value) for open `needs.tsv` bottlenecks and 34 priority industrial/hospitality items; (3) liquidating unreserved personal and faction lockbox surplus (`sell_faction_storage: true`, up to 48 sell orders per station) without ever self-undercutting or selling below `safe_sell_floor`; and (4) executing zero-fuel opportunistic station crafting (`craft.enabled: true`, `fuel_trickle_target: 3000`) when margins exceed 15 percent [4][5]. Together with `5A`, `4C` provides both the sensory nervous system of the Trade division and the zero-fuel cash conversion engine driving `+132,467 cr/h` live Trade net profit [6]. Advancing Standing Goals 1 and 4, Wing `4C` converts zero-fuel Exchange permanence into an uncopyable informational and liquidity moat across all five empires: 55 permanent desks (`25` `4C` + `30` `5A`) feed live 60-second order books straight into `market_guard.py`'s Cash-Weighted Median oracle, guiding all 130 miners, 80 hunters, 40 crafters, and 20 mobile traders to true external cash prices while eliminating self-wash trades, spoof dumps, and redundant bulk-ore buys [4][5][8].

## Introduction

In SpaceMolt, every station Exchange operates as an isolated regional market whose live bid/ask book can only be read by a ship docked at that exact station [5]. Flying mobile scouts or couriers back and forth just to inspect order books burns hundreds of thousands of credits per hour in jump fuel and leaves market intelligence hours stale by the time an arbitrage hauler arrives. Top trading blocs overcome this visibility wall by parking dedicated low-cost desk vessels permanently inside major Exchanges: `[BUSY]` parks 130 `Floor Price` market desks across galactic hubs, `[IRLC]` stations 36 `Ledger` commercial desks, and `[NOIR]` runs docked multi-hub station traders and crafters without even owning a sovereign base [7].

Wing `4C. Pure Bursar Desks` (25 ships: 22 Nebula, 3 Outer Rim) represents The Collective's dedicated pure-exchange desk force, sharing the 55-station `bursar-network` footprint directly with `5A. Station Refiners & Bursars` (30 ships) [1][3]. Because docked Bursars never burn jump fuel once seated at their assigned station, hull tier and speed matter far less than market skill, order-slot capacity, and reliable low-cost permanence (`congregation` T1 desk hulls) [1][2].

Operating 55 simultaneous autonomous market-makers introduces subtle economic hazards if poorly guarded. Without fleet-wide coordination, two Bursars can claim the same station and undercut each other to zero; a single Bursar can burn millions buying glutted `iron_ore` or `copper_ore` that our own miners extract for free; a naive instant-sell rule can dump 100-cr rare ores into 2-cr scam bids; or an aggressive buy order can cross a sister Bursar's sell order in a taxable wash trade [4][5]. `bursar.py` and `market_guard.py` neutralize all four hazards mechanically, turning `4C` into a disciplined, zero-fuel multi-hub market-making desk [5][8].

In financial microstructure terms, `4C. Pure Bursar Desks` functions as The Collective's distributed galactic prime brokerage and automated market-maker. Every 5 percent exchange spread captured by posting patient wholesale buy bids at `<= 90%` of fair value and selling accumulated stock at top-of-book ask (`undercut_cr: 1`) above `safe_sell_floor` compounds working capital at zero fuel cost [4][8]. Comparing rival Exchange footprints underscores the scale advantage: `[BUSY]` commits 130 `Floor Price` vessels to stationary market coverage (`+9.42M cr/h` faction wealth growth), `[IRLC]` operates 36 `Ledger` desks (`+4.22M cr/h`), and `[STLR]` uses concentrated station exchange trading alongside 200 mobile ships (`+907k cr/h`) [7]. By spreading all 55 permanent station seats strictly 1-per-station (`priority_max_per_station: 1`, `max_per_station: 1`) across 25 pure `4C` desks and 30 `5A` refinery desks -- covering all 33 Empire NPC hubs, Collective Resort Ridgecrest, and the top 21 deepest calm rival Exchanges (`arneb` `[BUSY]`, `lhs_1140` `[IRLC]`, `dheneb`/`zosma` `[HEXC]`, `40_eridani`/`gsc_0019`/`bd20_2457`/`gsc_0010` `[BUSY]`, `winterhold`, `cloverfield`, `crosshaven`, etc.) -- The Collective maximizes geographic cash conversion and 60-second price coverage without wasting a single seat double-staffing or sitting on empty zero-depth neutral stations [3][4].

## Findings

### Roster, Station Coverage, and Atomic Desk Claiming

Exact verification of `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `game/routes/bursar-network.json`, and `game/bursar-policy.json` establishes `4C`'s footprint [1][3][4]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 25 `MCV` hulls (`4C. Pure Bursar Desks`: 22 Nebula, 3 Outer Rim) locked alongside 30 `5A` ships (`wing_prefix: ["4C.", "5A."]`) on `bursar-network` (`max_ships: 55`), with `train-jumps-trader.json` strictly restricted to mobile wings `["4A.", "4B.", "5B."]` and legacy static `bursar-desk-*` routes retired (`max_ships: 0`) so zero Bursars abandon their desks [1][3].
- **Desk Hull:** `congregation` (T1 stationary commerce/refining platform, zero jump-fuel burn once stationed) [1].
- **Priority Hub Staffing & Claim Lock:** `bursar.py` claims stations atomically via `data/fleet/bursar_claims.json` with strictly **1 Bursar per station** (`max_per_station: 1`, `priority_max_per_station: 1`), `include_empty_neutral_stations: false` (while always keeping priority bases like Collective Resort Ridgecrest `a2cee88abdcb31db229c04315f0ef6e1` eligible even at `0` items), strong `log10(1 + depth) * 1500` liquidity weighting, and 31 `priority_stations` spanning Ridgecrest, our 10 core Empire vaults, and the top 20 deepest calm rival Exchanges (`arneb`, `lhs_1140`, `dheneb`, `zosma`, `40_eridani`, `gsc_0019`, `bd20_2457`, `gsc_0010`, `winterhold`, `cloverfield`, `crosshaven`, `gsc_0034`, `diphda`, `alpheratz`, `thuban`, `saiph`, `kornephoros`, `alzirr`, `lilii_borea`, `tiaki`) [4][5]. Strongholds and pirate-active stations (`pirate_avoid_hours: 6.0`) are automatically excluded [4].

### Market-Making Rules: Wholesale Buy Walls, Safe Sell Floors, and Zero-Fuel Crafting

Every 60 seconds (`poll_interval_s: 60`, 30-minute dispatch cycles), `bursar.py:plan_bursar_actions()` executes three tightly guarded commercial loops [4][5]:

1. **Disciplined Wholesale Buying (`buy.enabled: true`):** Each desk maintains up to `max_orders_per_station: 12` buy orders capped at `max_escrow_per_station: 250,000 cr`, `max_units_per_order: 200`, and `max_unit_price_ratio: 0.90` of fair value (`outbid_cr: 1`) [4]. Buying targets open `needs.tsv` bottlenecks plus 34 strategic inputs (`silver_ore`, `titanium_ore`, `plasma_gas`, `methane_ice`, `uranium_ore`, `lead_ore`, `fuel_cell`, `superconductor`, `power_cell`, `targeting_computer`, hospitality consumables, and rare Hunter drops) while strictly excluding fleet-abundant bulk goods (`iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`, `nitrogen_ice`, `water_ice`, `steel_plate`) [4]. Fleet-wide open buy caps are synchronized atomically via `data/fleet/bursar_buys.json` (`unwind_excess: true`) so desks never over-buy a bottleneck [4][5].
2. **Manipulation-Proof Liquidation (`sell.enabled: true`):** Desks list up to `max_orders_per_station: 48` sell orders from both personal storage and faction lockboxes (`sell_faction_storage: true`, `respect_reserve: true`), undercutting external asks by `1 cr` (`undercut_cr: 1`) while checking `same_station_cltv_buys` (`load_cltv_buy_orders` pilot map) so no Bursar ever hits another Collective pilot's buy order at the same station [4][5]. Prices are protected by `fair_price_map` (`market_guard.load_fair_price_map`), blocking sales below `min_price_ratio: 0.65` / `safe_sell_floor` (72 percent of oracle fair value) and stepping stale orders gently (`stale_step_minutes: 2`, `stale_step_pct: 0.10`) without crashing into scam bids [4][5][8].
3. **Opportunistic Zero-Fuel Station Crafting (`craft.enabled: true`):** Between order updates, `4C` desks run up to `max_runs_per_poll: 80` (`cycles_per_poll: 10`, `max_buy_spend_cr: 180,000`) zero-fuel commercial recipes clearing `min_margin_pct: 15%` and `min_profit_cr: 50 cr`, plus maintaining `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` `fuel_cell` inventory to protect fleet energy sovereignty [4].

### Cross-Division Synergy and Operational Overlap

`4C` is the commercial nervous system binding all 7 divisions together:
- **`4C` <-> `5A` Shared Footprint:** `4C` (25 ships) and `5A` (30 ships) jointly staff 55 permanent hubs on `bursar-network`, combining pure market-making desks (`4C`) with heavier industrial refinery desks (`5A` upgrading to `Conglomerate`) at zero fuel cost [1][3].
- **`4C` -> `4A/4B` Trade Haulers:** Every 60-second order-book refresh writes fresh `books` rows into `read.db`, exposing regional arbitrage spreads and deep buy-walls in real time for `4A` and `4B` [5][9].
- **Hunters (`2A/2B`) & Miners (`3A`-`3E`) -> `4C`:** Both `hunt_net.py` and `mine_net.py` grant proximity bonuses for dropping loot and ore at active `bursar_claims.json` hubs, turning field harvests directly into zero-fuel retail sales [10][11].
- **`4C` -> Treasury (`wallet.py`):** Accumulated retail sales cash across all 55 hubs is swept automatically in parallel by `wallet.py sweep --cash-only` directly into the Faction Treasury to fund uncapped T1-T5 hull commissioning [1][12].

Confidence in `4C`'s operating design is high: Trade is live-positive at `+132,467 cr/h` net profit (`292,380 cr/h` hourly gross income), `bursar_buys.json` + `load_cltv_buy_qty` eliminated both self-wash trading and external buy-wall blinding, and `exclude_buy_items` stopped cash leakage onto abundant bulk ores [4][6][8]. Because `4C` operates stationary `Congregation` desks, it requires zero heavy hull capex -- preserving treasury capital for combat (`1A/1B/1C/2A/2B`), extraction (`3A`-`3E`), and freighter (`4A/4B/6A`) tier climbs. Key metrics to monitor going forward are escrow capital turnover (ensuring the `250,000 cr` per-station escrow cap cycles rapidly into profitable fills rather than tying up dead cash on slow items) and station coverage parity as new player-owned Exchanges emerge across frontier regions.

Looking across the long-term empire horizon, `4C`'s strategic value scales automatically with the rest of the fleet. Every new Hunter loot drop, every rare `3A`/`3C`/`3D`/`3E` mineral load deposited at an active Bursar hub, and every new zero-fuel `5A` manufacturing line flows directly into `4C`'s 48-order retail liquidation shelves at zero incremental jump fuel [4][5][10]. Simultaneously, as The Collective constructs regional stations #2 through #5 across all five empire territories (Standing Goal 3), adding each owned station to `priority_stations` immediately seats dedicated double-staffed desks on our sovereign Exchanges with zero NPC rent [4]. Disciplined capital management stays simple and measurable: track active station count (`>= 50` hubs live), net escrow turnover (`<= 250k cr/station` cycling into profitable fills), zero self-wash crossings (`same_station_cltv_buys`), and steady upward growth in IFRS 15 Section 1 External Cash Revenue swept automatically into Treasury via `wallet.py sweep --cash-only` [4][6][12]. Paired with `5A`'s 30 station refiners, `4C`'s 25 Pure Bursar Desks secure real-time 60-second price truth across 55 hubs at zero recurring fuel cost -- directly fueling profitable decisions across every division of The Collective [3][4][5]. Disciplined wholesale bidding, strict bulk-ore exclusions, and safe-floor retail selling ensure `4C` compounds treasury cash steadily while shielding the entire fleet from open-market manipulation [4][8]. Maintaining 55 permanently staffed desks across empire capitals, Ridgecrest, and calm neutral hubs anchors both price truth and reliable cash flow across every market cycle [3][4][6].

## General Lessons

1. **Zero-fuel docked desks generate both direct spread income and fleet-wide intelligence.** Treating stationary Exchange traders merely as small-scale sellers undervalues them; their 60-second live order-book refresh is the data bedrock that makes every other division's valuation accurate [4][5].
2. **Enforce cross-desk anti-wash and buy-cap synchronization atomically.** A 55-desk market-making network must share a single synchronized open-buy ledger (`bursar_buys.json`) and check `same_station_cltv_buys` before every sell action; otherwise autonomous desks inevitably cross each other's orders and bleed 5 percent exchange tax [4][5].
3. **Never buy externally what your own fleet over-produces internally.** Explicit `exclude_buy_items` alignment with Mining (`iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`, `water_ice`, `nitrogen_ice`) and Logistics (`steel_plate`) keeps Bursar escrow focused exclusively on genuine bottlenecks and high-margin scarce goods [4].
4. **Combine patient retail sell orders with oracle-guarded floor protection.** Undercutting real asks by 1 cr above `safe_sell_floor` captures full retail margin at zero fuel, while blocking panic dumps into scam bids preserves the full value of Mining and Hunting production [4][8].
5. **Invest capex where hull stats scale output, not on stationary desks.** Keeping `4C` in affordable T1 `Congregation` desks avoids millions in wasted hull capex while delivering 100 percent of Exchange coverage, freeing treasury cash to accelerate T2-T5 combat and extraction upgrades [1].
6. **Use live pilot-level buy maps to stop self-crossing without blinding external demand.** Checking `same_station_cltv_buys` inside `bursar.py` blocks sister desks from hitting each other's bids, while `load_cltv_buy_qty` preserves visibility of giant external rival buy-walls sitting at the same hub [5][8].
7. **Avoid pirate-compromised and empty ghost neutral stations automatically.** Enforcing `exclude_strongholds: true`, `exclude_pirates: true`, `pirate_avoid_hours: 6.0`, `priority_max_per_station: 1`, and `include_empty_neutral_stations: false` (with owned priority bases always eligible) concentrates all 55 desks 1-per-station on liquid Empire and calm rival Exchanges (`arneb`, `lhs_1140`, `dheneb`, `zosma`, BUSY/IRLC/HEXC hubs) without losing unarmed `Congregation` desks or wasting seats on zero-depth ghost hubs [4].
8. **Sweep accumulated Bursar cash in parallel on a dedicated `--cash-only` lane.** Decoupling `wallet.py sweep --cash-only` from slow administrative queues guarantees daily retail revenue flows immediately into Treasury to fund zero-throttle hull upgrades [1][12].
