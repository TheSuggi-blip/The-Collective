# Ore Precision Mining Wing (3A) Operations, Scarce Belt Economics, and Dispatcher Logic

## Abstract

Wing `3A. Ore -- Precision` is The Collective's largest specialized extraction cohort, consisting of 38 Solarian Enterprise Utility Vessels (`EUV` prefix) engineered to harvest high-value scarce metals, rare minerals, and crystal deposits across the galaxy [1][2]. Operating under the unified `mine-network` AutoNet (`game/fleet/mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json`) with wing-specific scoring multipliers from `manifest_wings.py`, `3A` bridges the gap between bulk base-metal supply and high-margin rare-ore capture [2][3]. Every `3A` hull follows the Solarian precision mining ladder (`archimedes` -> `resonance_miner` -> `bonanza_king` -> `lithosphere` -> `tellurian`), capturing Solarian empire extraction bonuses and precision laser fitting (`fit-network`) [1][4]. Live market audits confirm that rare ores such as `silver_ore` (101 cr/u across 468,000+ units of rival buy-wall depth at `arneb`), `lead_ore` (120 cr/u), `uranium_ore` (600 cr/u), `energy_crystal` (40 cr/u), `darksteel_ore` (35 cr/u), `titanium_ore` (26 cr/u), and `palladium_ore` (20 cr/u) deliver 5x to 60x higher credit yield per cargo unit than common iron or copper [5][6]. To exploit these scarce belts without wasting fuel or starving tiny deposits, `3A` relies on manipulation-proof `market_guard.py` pricing, soft lawless preference (1.35x lawless vs 0.75x safe-zone with scarce exceptions), tiered 6/8/10-jump fuel radii, adaptive 1-to-3 ship belt caps, and a 0.2 percent scarce depletion floor [3][6]. The operational payoff is immediate: `3A` converts limited cargo holds and jump fuel into maximum Enterprise Net Worth per hour while feeding critical upstream minerals into Crafting (`5A/5B`) and Trade (`4A/4B/4C`). Directly advancing Standing Goals 1, 4, and 5, Wing `3A` converts 38 empire-native Solarian hulls into a continuous rare-metal profit engine that supplies both high-margin Bursar/Buy-Wall cash conversion and upstream electronic/alloy feedstocks for Collective Resort Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free manufacturing facilities [1][5][12]. Upgrading automatically with zero artificial per-pass throttles whenever treasury cash clears 2,000,000 cr, `3A` scales cargo capacity and laser throughput steadily from `Archimedes` through `Tellurian` while maintaining symmetric IFRS 8 intersegment handover parity inside `accounting.py` [1][10].

## Introduction

In SpaceMolt's mineral economy, not all asteroid belts are economically equal. Common ores (`iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`) sit in massive 50,000-to-250,000-unit deposits across policed core systems, often trading at 4 to 8 cr/u with severe fleet inventory gluts. By contrast, high-value metallic ores and exotic crystals (`silver_ore`, `titanium_ore`, `darksteel_ore`, `palladium_ore`, `platinum_ore`, `energy_crystal`, `silicon_ore`) exist primarily in thin, fiercely contested lawless belts (100 percent of `silver_ore` belts and 80 percent of `darksteel_ore` belts lie in lawless space) or rare policed pockets [5][6]. Top industrial rivals recognize this asymmetry: `[IRLC]` mines `+250,200 ore/h` across 275 ships (`AvgT 2.82`, 22 `Rock Eater` and precision miners), `[HEXC]` deploys `Lithosphere` T4 hulls within a tight 3-to-9-jump radius around 11 bases, and `[BUSY]` posts standing 468,000-unit buy-walls on `silver_ore` at 101 cr/u to vacuum up every unit extracted in the sector [5][7].

Wing `3A. Ore -- Precision` solves the specialization problem inside Mining. If all 130 miners used identical scoring weights, Solarian ore hulls would cluster indiscriminately on nearby low-value iron belts or fight over the same depleted silver vein while ignoring mid-tier titanium, darksteel, and crystal deposits needed by Logistics and Trade [3]. Simultaneously, naive price feeds expose precision miners to two opposite traps: 1-unit spoofed pump bids (such as a 701 cr x 6 spoof on `silicon_ore` when real bids sit at 7 cr) lure miners 10 jumps for phantom cash, while 2-cr trash scavenger walls poison naive volume-weighted averages and blind miners to genuine 40-to-101 cr scarce demand [6].

By assigning 38 Solarian hulls exclusively to `3A`, fitting them with precision mining lasers via `fit-network`, locking their scoring weights inside `mine_net.py:_wing_mine_multiplier`, and feeding every valuation through `market_guard.py`'s capped Cash-Weighted Median + LULD TWAP oracle, The Collective turns scarce-ore hunting into an automated, manipulation-resistant precision harvest [1][3][6].

A concrete unit-economic comparison explains why precision ore mining dominates bulk mining on return per jump. A standard mining run carrying 70 units of common `iron_ore` or `copper_ore` at 5 cr/u yields 350 cr gross -- barely covering a 3-jump round trip of fuel (~285 cr) and leaving near-zero net operating margin once hull wear and transit time are priced in [3][5]. By contrast, 70 units of `silver_ore` at 101 cr/u yields 7,070 cr, `energy_crystal` at 40 cr/u yields 2,800 cr, and `darksteel_ore` at 35 cr/u yields 2,450 cr -- delivering 7x to 20x greater gross revenue on the exact same fuel burn [5][6]. Rival benchmarks reinforce this asymmetry: `[BUSY]` maintains a massive standing 468,000-unit `silver_ore` buy-wall at `arneb` specifically to capture precision output from across the sector, while `[HEXC]` flies T4 `Lithosphere` miners within a tight 3-to-9-jump radius around 11 bases so station round-trips stay minimal [5][7]. Wing `3A` encodes that exact economic intuition into deterministic software.

## Findings

### Roster, Hull Ladder, and Wing Multiplier Mechanics

Verification of `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/fleet/mine_net.py` confirms the exact operational profile of `3A` [1][2][3]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 38 Solarian `EUV` hulls (`3A. Ore -- Precision`), representing 29.2 percent of the 130-ship Mining division [1][2].
- **Hull Progression Ladder:** `archimedes` (T1) -> `resonance_miner` (T2) -> `bonanza_king` (T3) -> `lithosphere` (T4) -> `tellurian` (T5) [1]. Upgrades execute automatically via `upgrades.py` with no artificial per-pass throttle, constrained solely by the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor and live shipyard listings (`read.db:ship_listings`) [1].
- **Tool & Wing Matching:** `mine_net.py:pilot_tools()` inspects live fitted modules (`mining_laser_*`) so `3A` ships only score solid ore and crystal belts (`resource_tool_kind(res) == "ore"`), while `_wing_mine_multiplier("3A.", res, tools)` boosts high-value precision metals, crystals, and rare non-radioactive ores over bulky base metals, separating `3A` naturally from `3B. Ore -- Bulk` (22 ships) and `3C. Deep-Core & Rad` (18 ships) [3].

### Scoring Architecture, Anti-Crowding Caps, and Depletion Thresholds

`mine_net.py:score_belt_candidates()` evaluates every scanned deposit in `read.db:fields` across five deterministic gates [3][8]:

1. **Four-Tier Priority Waterfall:** Belts are ranked in strict priority order: (1) `deficit` (open `needs.tsv` or reserve shortages up to `needs_max_priority: 3`), (2) `craft_demand` (active `craft-demand.json` inputs capped against glut), (3) `market` (external oracle-guarded bids `>= market_min_bid: 12 cr`, with `high_value_market_min_bid: 25 cr` bypassing full-reserve skips), and (4) `xp_fallback` (short-range `<= 4J` leveling belts, skipping hard-glut junk `> 200,000` held units) [3][8].
2. **Fuel-ROI Scoring & Hub Gravity:** Net profit per run is estimated as `(tier_mult * uval * 70 * liquidity_mult * saturation_mult * headroom_bonus) - round_trip_fuel`, divided by `round_trip_fuel * (1 + 0.35 * sys_claimed)` [3]. Because station round-trips (`st_hops`) burn fuel twice per load while approach (`hops`) is paid once, `st_hops` carries 2.7x higher fuel weight (`95 * st_hops` vs `35 * hops`) [3]. Owned zero-rent hubs (`ridgecrest`, `gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) and active lockbox stations earn a `1.04x` to `1.25x` Hub Gravity multiplier [3][8].
3. **Safe-Zone Avoided, Not Forbidden:** Live belt telemetry shows 100 percent of `silver_ore` (9 lawless / 0 policed) and 80 percent of `darksteel_ore` (12/3) lie in lawless space, whereas scarce `lead_ore` (1/6) and `palladium_ore` (0/4) appear heavily in policed space [5]. Accordingly, `policed_only` is disabled in favor of soft economic weighting: **1.35x lawless bonus** vs **0.75x safe-zone penalty**, with the penalty waived automatically for scarce ores (`>= 40 cr/u` or `< 3%` remaining) so `3A` never passes up 120 cr `lead_ore` [3][8].
4. **Tiered Range & Adaptive Belt Caps:** To prevent wasteful 10-jump fuel burns on medium ores while still capturing mega-value veins, `mine_net.py` enforces **6 jumps** normal (`< 60 cr/u`, `max_station_jumps: 5`), **8 jumps** high-value (`60-99 cr/u` or scarce/deficit), and **10 jumps** mega-value (`>= 100 cr/u` strictly: `uranium_ore`, `lead_ore`, `silver_ore`) [3][8]. Crowding is governed by `adaptive_belt_cap` (`max_per_belt: 3`, `units_per_ship_run: 400`), hard-capping scarce `< 3%` thin belts (such as 13-44 unit `silver_ore` veins) at **1 ship**, mid-size `1,000-9,999` unit belts at **2 ships**, and abundant `>= 10,000` unit belts at **3 ships** [3][8].
5. **Depletion Floors & Accounting Parity:** Ordinary belts stop at `min_left_pct: 5.0%` to preserve regeneration, while scarce ores (`>= 30 cr/u`, `>= 150k` real market cash, or open deficits) harvest down to **`scarce_min_left_pct: 0.2%`** (with a `>= 15` remaining unit floor for `>= 100 cr` mega-ores so 44-unit `silver_ore` pockets at 0.18 percent remain harvestable) [3][8]. Every `mine.py --deposit faction` handover writes symmetric `Mining -> Faction` entries to `handover.jsonl`, ensuring `3A` receives accurate IFRS 8 Section 5 intersegment production credit (`+195,830 cr/h` combined Mining 24-hour accrual) without double-counting Consolidated External Revenue [9][10].

### Cross-Division Synergy

`3A` forms the highest-margin upstream link in the fleet's value chain:
- **Intel (`7B` Deep-Core Surveyors) -> `3A`:** Fresh 4-hour scans across all 512 systems reveal newly regenerated `silver_ore`, `darksteel_ore`, and `energy_crystal` veins before rival miners arrive [5][11].
- **`3A` -> Trade (`4A/4B` Haulers & `4C` Bursars):** Precision ore deposited into faction lockboxes (`ridgecrest`, Empire hubs) is swept by `4B` Bulk Trade Haulers directly into deep external buy-walls (such as `[BUSY]`'s 101 cr `silver_ore` wall) or liquidated via `4C` Bursar sell orders [5][6].
- **`3A` -> Craft (`5A/5B`):** High-value metals (`titanium_ore`, `silicon_ore`, `darksteel_ore`, `energy_crystal`) supply zero-fuel station refiners making high-margin components (`superconductor`, `targeting_computer`, `power_cell`) [12].
- **Logistics (`6A`) -> `3A`:** Supply haulers keep owned hub fuel bunkers (`ridgecrest`, `gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) topped up so `3A` miners refuel and deposit rent-free within 0 to 5 jumps of lawless frontier belts [3][8].

## Conclusion

Wing `3A. Ore -- Precision` transforms 38 Solarian mining hulls into a laser-focused rare-mineral capture system. Rather than competing in low-margin volume wars on glutted iron and copper belts, `3A` allocates its cargo volume and jump fuel directly toward the steepest price-per-unit minerals in SpaceMolt, guided by manipulation-resistant external bids, tiered 6/8/10-jump distance limits, and size-adaptive 1/2/3-ship belt caps. Because every belt selection penalizes system-level crowding (`1 + 0.35 * sys_claimed`) and station round-trip burn, miners naturally fan out across 50+ systems instead of piling onto a single asteroid field.

Operational confidence in `3A`'s design is high: live 30-minute soak telemetry verified 100 percent of fresh miner claims targeting scarce high-value ores/gas/ice after unpoisoning `market_guard.py`, with fleet-wide Mining utilization at 129 of 130 ships active and `+195,830 cr/h` 24-hour accrual profit [9]. The main leverage point ahead is hull and module progression from T1 `Archimedes` through T3 `Bonanza King` and T4 `Lithosphere`, which multiplies extraction rate per tick and cargo capacity per trip without linearly increasing jump fuel. Key empirical triggers that would justify tuning `3A` parameters include sustained depletion of lawless `silver_ore` and `darksteel_ore` belts (warranting slightly lower `units_per_ship_run` or faster Intel `7B` rescan cadence) or hostile PvP interdiction in frontier corridors (warranting tighter `pirate_battle_avoid_hours` or escort pairing with `1A/1C`).

Across the T1 through T5 upgrade ladder, `3A`'s profitability compounds along two independent axes: extraction speed per tick and hold capacity per station round-trip [1][3]. Moving all 38 `3A` pilots from T1 `Archimedes` into T2 `Resonance Miner`, T3 `Bonanza King`, T4 `Lithosphere`, and T5 `Tellurian` multiplies single-sortie gross revenue by 3x to 6x while holding jump-fuel burn nearly flat per ton hauled [1][3]. Simultaneously, constructing stations #2 through #5 across all five empire regions and expanding to 8 corridor outposts (Standing Goal 3) will place rent-free faction lockboxes within 0 to 4 jumps of nearly every lawless `silver_ore`, `darksteel_ore`, and `energy_crystal` cluster in the galaxy -- triggering `mine_net.py`'s Hub Gravity bonus (`1.10x` to `1.25x`) and slashing `st_hops` fuel burn structurally [3][8]. Tracking live spread across unique systems (already lifted from 39 to 53 systems in live verification) and realized IFRS 8 Section 5 handover value per `3A` hull provides immediate, falsifiable proof of whether belt caps, scarce floors, or Intel `7B` refresh cadences need further sharpening [9][10]. Backed by manipulation-proof Cash-Weighted Median pricing, tiered 6/8/10-jump fuel radii, and adaptive 1/2/3-ship belt caps, Wing `3A` converts 38 Solarian precision hulls into one of The Collective's highest-margin cash generators [1][3][6].

## General Lessons

1. **Price oracle integrity governs physical fleet allocation.** In an autonomous economy, bad market pricing does not merely skew reports -- it physically flies dozens of ships into worthless space. Capped Cash-Weighted Median pricing with trash-bid removal and net-external `load_cltv_buy_qty` subtraction ensures miners respond only to real external cash [6].
2. **Scale crowding caps to deposit size, not a fixed fleet constant.** Allowing 3 ships on a 44-unit `silver_ore` pocket guarantees two wasted round trips, whereas capping scarce `< 3%` pockets at 1 ship while permitting 2-3 ships on 10,000-unit deposits maximizes fleet-wide yield per jump [3].
3. **Separate approach cost from round-trip station cost in spatial optimization.** Approach distance is paid once per belt session, whereas belt-to-station distance is paid twice on every cargo load; weighting `st_hops` heavily (`95` vs `35`) anchors precision miners naturally around owned zero-rent hubs and lockboxes [3].
4. **Use tiered depletion floors to compete on scarce deposits without killing renewable ones.** Holding common belts at a 5.0 percent floor protects long-term respawn, while dropping scarce high-cash belts to 0.2 percent (with a 15-unit minimum floor on `>= 100 cr` mega-ores) lets our precision miners capture thin frontier pockets that rigid floors leave for rivals [3][8].
5. **Credit upstream producers symmetrically without breaking GAAP consolidation.** Recording `Mining -> Faction` transfers in `handover.jsonl` makes `3A`'s true economic contribution visible in IFRS 8 intersegment reporting while eliminating 100 percent of internal transfers from Consolidated External Revenue [10].
6. **Penalize system-level clustering even when individual belt caps have room.** Multiplying round-trip fuel cost by `1 + 0.35 * sys_claimed` spreads 38 precision miners organically across 50+ systems rather than concentrating an entire unarmed division inside one attractive lawless sector vulnerable to rival gankers [3].
7. **Combine real buyer liquidity with fleet stock saturation inside belt scoring.** Multiplying belt yield by both multi-station buyer depth (`_liq_m`) and stock-to-demand decay (`_sat_m`) guarantees precision miners automatically pivot before flooding any narrow rare-mineral order book [3].
