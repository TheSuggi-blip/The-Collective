# Supply Chain Bulk Haulers Wing (6A) Operations, Bunker Sovereignty, and Industrial Distribution

## Abstract

Wing `6A. Supply Chain Bulk Haulers` is The Collective's 50-ship internal heavy transport backbone, manned exclusively by Nebula Logistic Support Vessels (`LSV` prefix) scaling along SpaceMolt's premier bulk freighter ladder (`dividend` -> `junk_convoy` -> `enterprise` -> `frankenhauler`) [1][2]. Operating under `haul-network` (`game/fleet/haul.py` and `game/haul-policy.json`) and strictly governed as a Service and Infrastructure Responsibility Center (never selling on open commercial markets), `6A` executes the physical distribution that keeps a 500-ship sovereign empire alive: (1) keeping fuel bunkers and repair stockpiles continuously filled across Collective Resort Ridgecrest and owned frontier outposts (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`); (2) consolidating raw ore, gas, and ice from regional lockboxes into central manufacturing hubs (`ridgecrest`, `node_beta`, Empire cores); (3) moving construction kits and structural materials (`steel_plate`, `circuit_board`, `reinforced_glass`) to close `needs.tsv` backlogs; and (4) provisioning hospitality consumables for owned stations [2][3][4]. Rival industrial superpowers `[IRLC]` (32 `Frankenhauler` freighters feeding 8 bases) and `[HEXC]` (`Frankenhauler` supply network across 11 bases) prove that territorial dominance scales strictly with internal bulk haulage capacity [5]. Equipped with `escort_proto.py` convoy safety hooks, hazmat hold checks, and hub-upkeep reserve protection (`_hub_upkeep_keep_floor`), `6A` turns scattered 5-empire extraction into one unified sovereign supply chain. Directly powering Standing Goals 2, 3, and 4, Wing `6A` transforms scattered 5-empire mining and crafting output into a single cohesive sovereign economy -- ensuring zero fuel starvation at lawless outposts, continuous hospitality provisioning at Collective Resort Ridgecrest's 13 active Resort/Quarters facilities (Station #1), steady feedstock delivery to our industrial crafting hubs (and future Foundry Station #3), and rapid closure of station construction kits [1][3][11]. Automated no-throttle hull commissioning (`max_auto_tier: 2` live) scales all 50 Nebula heavy freighters from `Dividend` through `Frankenhauler` above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor, protected by atomic job locking, hazmat hold checks, and `escort_proto.py` convoy hooks [1][3][8].

## Introduction

A multi-region industrial empire fails the moment its logistics arteries clog. Without automated internal haulage, 130 miners deposit raw ore at 16 regional lockboxes while 40 crafters sit idle waiting for feedstock at Collective Resort Ridgecrest; frontier outpost fuel bunkers (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) run dry and strand forward combat and mining wings; and construction kits stall indefinitely waiting for 10,000 `steel_plate` or `circuit_board` units sitting four jumps away [3][6]. Equally destructive is **incentive misalignment**: earlier fleet scorecards judged Logistics on raw wallet cash flow, creating perverse pressure to dump internal supplies onto NPC exchanges rather than fulfilling internal supply-chain SLAs [7].

Wing `6A. Supply Chain Bulk Haulers` (50 Nebula `LSV` hulls, 55.6 percent of the 90-ship Logistics division) solves both throughput and incentive purity. Dedicating 50 heavy Nebula freighters exclusively to internal industrial distribution -- completely separated from `4A/4B` commercial trading (`haul-network-trade`) -- guarantees that urgent outpost fuel deliveries, station upkeep consumables, lockbox consolidation, and construction kit backlogs never lose ship slots to opportunistic arbitrage [1][3][7]. Meanwhile, upgrading all 50 `6A` hulls from T1 `Dividend` through T2 `Junk Convoy`, T3 `Enterprise`, and T4 `Frankenhauler` delivers massive cargo holds that slash jump-fuel overhead per delivered ton of freight [1][5].

Because Logistics operates across all 5 empire regions and lawless sovereign outpost corridors (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`, `ridgecrest`), `6A` must also enforce strict survivability and reserve boundaries: refusing to drain an owned station below its operating upkeep floor (`_hub_upkeep_keep_floor`), verifying hazmat hold capability (`has_hazmat`) before moving radioactive or hazardous loads, avoiding active pirate systems (`pirate_avoid_hours`), and coordinating 3-to-5-ship armed convoys via `escort_proto.py` whenever moving critical loads through contested space [3][8].

In a 500-ship fleet (scaling eventually toward 1,000 ships under Standing Goal 2), internal freight volume grows faster than headcount because every additional mining tier, refinery facility, and regional station multiplies cross-hub material flows [1][6]. Consider the daily logistics load of The Collective's industrial map: 130 miners deposit across 16 whitelisted stations (`deposit_stations`), 40 crafters consume raw inputs at Collective Resort Ridgecrest and Empire workshops, 3 frontier outposts (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) plus Ridgecrest require continuous fuel cells, repair plates, circuit boards, and hospitality rations, and future builds for 4 more regional stations and 5 more corridor outposts will demand massive construction-kit convoys [3][6][10]. Rival `[NOIR]` (0 bases) and `[WEXL]` (0 bases) avoid internal haulage entirely by abandoning territorial sovereignty, whereas true superpower blocs `[IRLC]` (8 bases, 32 `Frankenhaulers`) and `[HEXC]` (11 bases, `Frankenhaulers`) invest heavily in heavy internal bulk haulage [5]. Allocating 50 Nebula `LSV` hulls (10 percent of total fleet) to `6A` gives The Collective sovereign logistics scale exceeding every rival in the galaxy [1].

## Findings

### Roster, Heavy Freighter Progression, and Responsibility-Center Governance

Exact verification of `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/fleet/hive.py` confirms `6A`'s structure and live posture [1][2][9]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 50 Nebula `LSV` hulls (`6A. Supply Chain Bulk Haulers`), representing 55.6 percent of the 90-ship Logistics division (alongside 28 `6B` Passenger Liners, 6 `6C` Salvage Tenders, and 6 `6C` Fleet Fuel Tankers) [1][2].
- **Nebula Heavy Freighter Ladder:** `dividend` (T1) -> `junk_convoy` (T2) -> `enterprise` (T3) -> `frankenhauler` (T4) [1]. All 50 hulls are active in `upgrades.py` with zero artificial per-pass throttle, bounded solely by the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor and live shipyard stock [1].
- **Strict Non-Selling Infrastructure Mandate:** Logistics is tracked as a Service/Infrastructure Responsibility Center measured by dynamic `Supply Chains & Fulfillment SLA` (`0 open backlog` once build kits close) rather than raw commercial cash sales [7]. Live 2026-11-27 telemetry shows Logistics operating 76 of 90 ships actively in space (`+214,129 cr/h` 24-hour purse velocity including mission rewards) [9].

### Core `haul.py` Logistics Lanes, Reserve Guards, and Bunker Resupply

Under `game/fleet/haul.py` and `game/haul-policy.json`, `6A` executes four automated supply-chain lanes [3][4]:

1. **Owned Station & Outpost Bunker Resupply:** Dedicated standing supply routes (`supply-bunker-atria-own-cells`, `supply-bunker-gsc-0044-own-cells`, `supply-repair-gsc-0044-boards`, `supply-repair-gsc-0044-plate`) plus dynamic `haul-network` bunker jobs automatically replenish owned outpost `fuel` (below 3,000-5,000 units), `circuit_board`, `steel_plate`, and hospitality upkeep consumables (`purified_water`, rations, beverages) using internally crafted `5A`/`5B` stock first before ever touching external supplies [3][4][10].
2. **Lockbox Consolidation & Central Depot Balancing:** `haul.py` tracks owned hub upkeep floors (`_hub_upkeep_keep_floor`) and `central_depot_targets` (`ridgecrest` for Resort hospitality upkeep + Empire industrial depots, expanding to Foundry Station #3 once built), pulling excess raw ore (`3A`-`3E`), refined alloys, and crafting inputs from peripheral lockboxes into active industrial hubs while keeping Ridgecrest's `13` active Resort/Quarters facilities stocked [3][11].
3. **Open `needs.tsv` Kit Fulfillment:** Whenever a new station, outpost, facility upgrade, or fleet fitting requirement opens an item row in `needs.tsv`, `6A` prioritizes hauling the required materials from faction storage directly to the target construction site until the need turns green [3][4].
4. **Convoy Safety & Atomic Job Deconfliction:** Active hauls lock `(origin, dest, item)` atomically via `active_claimed_keys()` so two freighters never race to fill the exact same outpost fuel bunker [3]. `haul.py` checks `has_hazmat` before assigning hazardous cargo, excludes stronghold and active pirate bases (`exclude_bases`), and integrates `escort_proto.py` (`convoy_fleet_created`, automatic fleet disband and `clear_escort_request` on completion) whenever armed Military convoy escort is required [3][8].

### Cross-Division Synergy

`6A` is the physical circulatory system linking every producing and consuming wing in the fleet:
- **Miners (`3A`-`3E`) -> `6A` -> Crafters (`5A`/`5B`):** `6A` lifts accumulated ore, gas, and ice from satellite lockboxes directly into Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free facilities and Empire refining hubs so zero-fuel crafting never starves [3][11].
- **Crafters (`5A`/`5B`) -> `6A` -> All Forward Wings (`1A/1B/1C`, `2A/2B`, `3A`-`3E`, `7A/7B`):** `6A` distributes internally refined `fuel_cell`, `armor_plate`, `repair_kit`, and station hospitality goods to owned outposts (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`, `ridgecrest`), enabling forward combat and mining wings to refuel and repair deep in lawless space at zero NPC rent [3][10].
- **`6A` -> Trade (`4B` Bulk Trade Haulers):** By consolidating scattered lockbox surpluses into high-traffic central hubs while strictly respecting hub upkeep floors, `6A` hands `4B` dense full-freighter loads ready for rapid liquidation into rival buy-walls [3].
- **`6A` <-> `6C` Fuel Tankers:** `6A` handles general multi-commodity bulk supply chains and station kits while `6C` Fleet Fuel Tankers specialize in deep-corridor fuel logistics (`dividend -> long_haul -> reserve -> plenum`) [1].

## Conclusion

Wing `6A. Supply Chain Bulk Haulers` is the 50-ship logistical spine that converts 500 independent ships into a coherent galactic empire. Locking 50 Nebula `LSV` hulls onto the `Dividend -> Junk Convoy -> Enterprise -> Frankenhauler` progression ladder and keeping them strictly dedicated to internal supply chains, outpost bunker fuel, construction kits, and lockbox consolidation guarantees sovereign infrastructure uptime without ever fighting `4A/4B` commercial traders for ship slots.

Confidence in `6A`'s architecture is high, validated both by live fleet execution (`76/90` Logistics active, Ridgecrest running 13 rent-free facilities) and top rival precedent (`[IRLC]`'s 32 `Frankenhaulers` and `[HEXC]`'s multi-base supply network) [5][9][11]. As The Collective progresses on Standing Goal 3 (fortifying 5 regional empire stations and 8 corridor outposts), `6A`'s workload will expand from single-corridor consolidation into five-region continental logistics. Prioritizing T2 `Junk Convoy`, T3 `Enterprise`, and T4 `Frankenhauler` hull upgrades across `6A` will multiply hauling tonnage per jump while holding fuel burn flat. The primary live diagnostic to watch is `needs.tsv` backlog age and owned outpost bunker minimums (`fuel < 3,000`): any persistent red backlog indicates either raw upstream extraction deficit (`3B`/`3E`) or `6A` cargo-capacity bottleneck requiring accelerated freighter tier upgrades.

Over the T2 through T4 progression cycle (`Junk Convoy -> Enterprise -> Frankenhauler`), `6A` delivers massive operating leverage across all five empire regions [1][5]. Each freighter tier jump expands cargo hold capacity dramatically, letting one `Enterprise` or `Frankenhauler` deliver in a single trip what previously required 5 to 10 T0/T1 runs -- cutting Logistics' metered fuel burn (`45,289 cr/h` live) per ton moved while accelerating consolidation into Ridgecrest and regional stations #2 through #5 [1][9]. Critically, as `5A`/`5B` internal `fuel_cell` production scales up at Ridgecrest, `6A`'s own-cell bunker supply routes (`supply-bunker-*-own-cells`) progressively replace external NPC fuel purchases across frontier outposts, compounding enterprise cash savings across every combat, mining, and recon wing [10]. Live SLA telemetry -- open `needs.tsv` backlog count, owned outpost fuel levels (`>= 3,000-5,000` units), and Ridgecrest lockbox feedstock uptime -- provides immediate, unambiguous verification that `6A` is keeping the entire empire supplied smoothly [3][7][11]. Ultimately, `6A`'s 50 Nebula freighters provide the physical scalability required to grow from 500 ships and 1 sovereign station to 1,000 ships, 5 regional empire stations, and 8 fortified corridor outposts without supply-chain fracture [1][6]. Keeping `6A` strictly dedicated to internal infrastructure SLAs while `4B` handles commercial bulk sales preserves clean operational focus across both logistics and commerce [3][7]. Sustained freighter progression guarantees logistics never bottlenecks galactic expansion [1][6].

## General Lessons

1. **Measure internal logistics on fulfillment SLAs and hub uptime, never retail sales.** Judging supply haulers on wallet revenue creates perverse incentives to sell off critical station fuel and construction stock; Responsibility-Center governance aligns `6A` strictly with zero open backlogs [7].
2. **Protect station upkeep floors mechanically before sweeping surplus.** Checking `_hub_upkeep_keep_floor` and `central_depot_targets` before every pickup prevents enthusiastic consolidators from starving an owned station of its own fuel or hospitality consumables [3].
3. **Prefer internally crafted supply chains over NPC purchases.** Routing `supply-bunker-*-own-cells` ahead of NPC fuel-cell purchase routes ensures `3E` ice and `5A` refining flow directly into owned outpost bunkers, structurally lowering fleet-wide operating burn [10].
4. **Match freighter hold volume to total fleet extraction output.** Fifty T0/T1 hulls cannot keep pace with 130 T3/T4 miners; upgrading `6A` alongside Mining into `Enterprise` and `Frankenhauler` hulls keeps consolidation ahead of extraction throughput [1][5].
5. **Deconflict bunker fills atomically across all 50 haulers.** Locking `(origin, dest, item)` in `active_claimed_keys()` prevents ten freighters from hauling 50,000 fuel cells to a single 5,000-unit outpost bunker [3].
6. **Enforce hazmat and pirate-zone filtering mechanically before freighter dispatch.** Checking `has_hazmat` before loading radioactive cargo and excluding stronghold or pirate-raided bases inside `haul.py` prevents catastrophic losses of fully loaded T3-T4 `Enterprise` and `Frankenhauler` transports [3].
7. **Clean up convoy fleets and escort requests atomically upon delivery completion.** Automatically running `disband` and `clear_escort_request` inside `haul.py`'s `finally` path ensures Military `1A/1C` escort ships return immediately to active hunting or defense duty rather than hanging orphaned in empty space [3][8].
8. **Reserve owned-station lockboxes strictly for accessible internal distribution.** Routing miners (`3A`-`3E`) into whitelisted lockbox hubs that `6A` services continuously eliminates stranded personal storage and maximizes working-capital velocity across all 500 hulls [3][11]. Maintaining strict non-selling Service-Center boundaries keeps 50 heavy freighters dedicated exclusively to empire uptime and rapid station expansion [3][7].
