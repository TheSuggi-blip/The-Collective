# Fleet and Defense Division Architecture, Task Forces, and Doctrine

## Abstract

The Fleet and Defense division is the strategic combat backbone of The Collective (`[CLTV]`), comprising 70 dedicated combat hulls organized across five operational wings: three combined-arms Task Forces (`1A-Prime` with 10 ships, `1A-Alpha` with 8 ships, and `1A-Beta` with 8 ships), the 20-ship `1B. Grand Battle Line`, and the 24-ship `1C. Outpost Sentinels` [1][2]. Unlike solo hunters that optimize for rapid per-hull credit generation, Fleet and Defense provides the coordinated strike capability, electronic interdiction, carrier projection, remote armor repair, and territorial deterrence required to hold sovereign stations, escort high-value convoys, and contest lawless space against rival blocs such as `[BUSY]`, `[ENDL]`, and `[IRLC]` [2][3]. Every ship in this division is birth-empire matched to permanent hull and combat bonuses across Voidborn, Crimson, and Solarian lineages, and mechanically locked via `manifest_wings.py` and `dispatch.py` to coordinated combat AutoNets (`hunt-network-packs`, `hunt-network`, `fit-network`, and `escort_proto.py`) [1][4]. Operationally, the division enforces scale-free threat gating, real-time ammo and firepower gauging (`gauges.py`), mandatory multi-ship pack composition, and fuel-optimal tiered engagement radii (6 jumps normal, 8 jumps high-value, and 10 jumps for apex prey) with lawless preference rather than safe-zone exclusion [5][6]. The implication for fleet architecture is decisive: without an integrated, empire-matched defense and strike wing that trains continuously on high-value apex wildlife during peacetime while staying ready for territorial defense, an industrial empire cannot protect its stations, lockboxes, or frontier extractors. From a capital-allocation and governance standpoint, Fleet and Defense bridges Standing Goals 1, 2, 3, and 5 by converting peacetime military training into self-funding crew mastery while securing Collective Resort Ridgecrest and our planned five regional stations and eight frontier outposts [1][2]. Every hull upgrade executes automatically without artificial concurrency throttles whenever treasury cash exceeds the 2,000,000 cr floor, prioritizing T1 through T5 shipyard stock across all five empires [1]. Combined with strict IFRS 15 external cash separation and IFRS 8 intersegment handover logging, the division prevents two classic multi-agent combat failure modes: under-fitted solo warships feeding NPC pirate raiders and idle peacetime garrisons draining fuel without building combat skill [5][7].

## Introduction

In SpaceMolt, raw industrial wealth without credible fleet combat power creates an asymmetric target for organized rival coalitions. Live telemetry across the top galactic blocs demonstrates that dominant factions pair deep industrial bases with large, well-tiered combat wings: `[BUSY]` operates 1,249 ships including 157 `Dialectic`, 43 `Axiomata`, and 21 `Opus Magna` combat hulls across 77 percent lawless space, while `[ENDL]` has logged 1,527 player-versus-player kills across 180 ships and 8 bases [3][7]. Conversely, pure industrial or trade blocs without hardened escort and battle-line formations cannot project force into lawless corridors where 74 to 100 percent of high-value scarce commodities and apex wildlife herds reside [6][7].

The Fleet and Defense division solves three coupled operational problems for The Collective. First, it establishes a standing combined-arms hierarchy that mirrors naval fleet doctrine: Command Flagships (`1A` CMD) coordinate formations and unlock fleet leadership bonuses; heavy DPS brawlers (`1A` DPS and `1B. Grand Battle Line`) deliver decisive alpha and sustained firepower; Voidborn Carriers project drone damage and tank incoming focus fire; Solarian Interdictors pin fast targets with warp disruption and webification; and Voidborn Medics sustain fleet armor and shield integrity through extended engagements [1][2]. Second, it prevents peacetime combat capital from sitting idle by routing Task Forces, Battle Line cruisers, and Outpost Sentinels into coordinated wildlife hunting packs (`hunt-network-packs`) and high-tier solo deterrence (`hunt-network`) that earn piloting, gunnery, and leadership experience while generating high-value biological loot for Bursar liquidation [4][5]. Third, it supplies on-demand multi-ship convoy and field escorts via `escort_proto.py` so unarmed haulers and deficit miners never enter contested lawless sectors alone [5][8].

Because every ship in The Collective is operated under one unified autonomous orchestration layer (`dispatch.py`, `runhost.py`, `upgrades.py`, `fit_net.py`, and `hunt_net.py`), Fleet and Defense must balance peacetime return on fuel against wartime survivability. Sending fragile starter ships into pirate systems or burning 10-jump round trips for low-value grazers destroys both hulls and working capital. Encoding exact wing roles, birth-empire hull ladders, power-ratio gates, and tiered engagement distances into declarative policy files (`hunt-policy.json`, `upgrade-policy.json`, `fit-policy.json`) converts naval doctrine into deterministic, 24/7 execution [1][4][5].

In SpaceMolt's real-time tick economy, combat outcomes are governed by strict physical constraints: weapon range bands, shield versus armor effective hit points, live ammunition magazines, jump-fuel burn scaling with hull tier, and multi-ship focus fire inside contested POIs [5][7]. A single warship -- even a T3 cruiser -- can be tackled and destroyed by coordinated NPC pirate packs or hostile player task forces if it lacks remote armor logistics and warp-disruption counter-tackle [5][8]. Comparing top rival postures sharpens this design requirement: `[ENDL]` accumulates 1,527 PvP kills across 180 ships by projecting compact, aggressive combat groups around 8 bases, `[BUSY]` overwhelms frontier space with 221 heavy combat hulls backed by massive mining wealth, whereas `[WEXL]` (154 ships, 133 `Cobble` mission hulls, 0 bases) and `[HIVE]` (200 ships, 0 combat) remain structurally vulnerable to territorial denial [3][7]. The Collective's 70-ship Fleet and Defense wing synthesizes the best of these lessons -- pairing combined-arms depth with continuous peacetime ROI.

## Findings

### Wing Structure, Birth Empires, and Hull Progression Ladders

Exact manifest verification across `game/fleet-500-manifest.tsv`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/upgrade-policy.json` establishes the 70-ship Fleet and Defense roster across five distinct sub-wings [1][2]:

- **`1A-Prime` (10 ships, `GSV` prefix):** 4 Voidborn Command Flagships, 3 Crimson Heavy DPS, 1 Voidborn Carrier, 1 Solarian Interdictor, and 1 Voidborn Medic [1][2].
- **`1A-Alpha` (8 ships, `GSV` prefix):** 3 Voidborn Command Flagships, 2 Crimson Heavy DPS, 1 Voidborn Carrier, 1 Solarian Interdictor, and 1 Voidborn Medic [1][2].
- **`1A-Beta` (8 ships, `GSV` prefix):** 3 Voidborn Command Flagships, 2 Crimson Heavy DPS, 1 Voidborn Carrier, 1 Solarian Interdictor, and 1 Voidborn Medic [1][2].
- **`1B. Grand Battle Line` (20 ships, `GSV` prefix):** 14 Crimson Heavy Assault/DPS ships and 6 Solarian Heavy Fire-Support/Tank hulls [1][2].
- **`1C. Outpost Sentinels` (24 ships, `GSV` prefix):** 12 Voidborn Tank/Carrier hulls, 6 Solarian Interdictors, and 6 Voidborn Medics permanently assigned to fortress corridors (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`, `ridgecrest`) [1][2].

All 70 ships follow strictly empire-matched T1 through T5 upgrade ladders defined in `upgrade-policy.json` with zero prestige-locked hulls and no artificial upgrade throttle beyond the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor and shipyard stock availability [1]:

- **Command Flagships (10 Voidborn):** `axiom` (T1) -> `theorem` (T2) -> `concordance` (T3) -> `consensus` (T4) -> `symposium` (T5) [1].
- **Heavy DPS & Battle Line (27 Crimson/Solarian):** `cleaver` (T1) -> `broadaxe` (T2) -> `dialectic` (T3) -> `crimson_devastator` (T4) -> `annihilator` (T5) [1].
- **Carriers & Heavy Tanks (15 Voidborn):** `axiom` (T1) -> `dualism` (T2) -> `gestalt` (T3) -> `superposition` / `voidborn_event_horizon` (T4) -> `overmind` / `apotheosis` (T5) [1].
- **Interdictors (9 Solarian):** `precept` (T1) -> `penumbra` (T2) -> `aporia` (T3) -> `paradox_engine` (T4) [1].
- **Fleet Medics (9 Voidborn):** `precept` (T1) -> `anamnesis` (T2) -> `remedy` (T3) -> `panacea` (T4) -> `pleroma` (T5) [1].

### Dispatcher Wiring, AutoNet Conditions, and Combat Gating

Fleet and Defense ships run continuously under `dispatch.py` and `runhost.py` through four coordinated lanes governed by `game/hunt-policy.json` and `game/fit-policy.json` [4][5]:

1. **Automated Fitting (`fit-network`):** Before combat deployment, `fit_net.py` reads authoritative module slot classes directly from `read.db:items.slot` (avoiding keyword misclassifications such as putting `remote_armor_repairer_ii` into weapon slots) and installs role-matched weapons, shields, armor plates, warp disruptors, and remote repairers using faction storage first before falling back to local market purchases within per-ship spend limits [4][9].
2. **Coordinated Strike Packs (`hunt-network-packs`):** Task Forces (`1A-Prime/Alpha/Beta`), Battle Line cruisers (`1B`), and Outpost Sentinels (`1C`) prioritize coordinated 3, 5, 7, 8, or 10-ship packs (`target_share: 0.40`, `max_forming_packs: 14`, `min_member_power: 350`, `command_flagship_min_power: 150`) [5]. Command Flagships never solo-graze when apex priority prey is open; they initiate packs to train fleet leadership while DPS and Medic ships rally within `rally_max_jumps` (12 jumps) and hold formation (`close_dist: 2`, `fight_ticks: 45`, `flee_hull_pct: 80`) [5].
3. **Scale-Free Threat & Rule F9 Firepower Gating:** `hunt_net.py` computes target threat as `sqrt(raw_ehp * edps) * 10 * role_mult` (grazer 1.0, predator 1.5, pirate 2.0, player/boss 2.5) and cross-checks `gauges.py` (`f9_gauge`), excluding any ammunition-fed weapon when the ship hold carries zero ammo [5]. Pack engagements require `min_power_ratio >= 1.8` on wildlife (`2.2` on pirates when pirate raiding is enabled) and a zero-fuel-waste ROI floor (`min_loot_val >= 750 cr` and `min_loot_per_ship >= 250 cr`) [5].
4. **On-Demand Escort Protocol (`escort_proto.py`):** When armed escorts are authorized, `escort_proto.py` dispatches minimum 3-ship combat formations (`min_escorts: 3`, `lawless_min_hp: 180`, 120-second lawless pre-entry rally wait) gated by live pirate threat ratio against `read.db:public_battles` and sightings [5][8]. During T0-T1 early hull buildup, escort `min_power` stays elevated to prevent under-tiered ships from feeding NPC pirate raiders [5].

### Safe-Zone Preference, Tiered Radius, and Cross-Division Synergy

Live market and rival benchmarking shows why Fleet and Defense uses tiered ranges and lawless preference rather than rigid boundaries. `[HEXC]` achieves top tier-per-hull efficiency (`AvgT 3.19`) operating at a median of 3 jumps and p90 of 9 jumps from its 11 hubs, while `[BUSY]` stages 77 percent of its fleet in lawless space around a 1-jump median and 12-jump p90 [3][7]. Accordingly, `hunt_net.py` avoids safe zones via a 0.65x safe-zone score penalty and 1.45x lawless preference without forbidding policed high-value hunts, and enforces fuel-optimal tiered ranges: 6 jumps for standard prey, 8 jumps for Tier-S targets (`>= 550 cr`), and 10 jumps strictly for apex megafauna packs (`>= 1,800 cr` such as `cloudwhale`, `molt_leviathan`, `prism_drifter`, and `cobalt_squid`) [5][6].

Fleet and Defense synergizes tightly with four sister divisions: Intel (`7A/7B`) refreshes `read.db:herds` and `public_battles` across all 512 systems so packs never fly blind; Logistics (`6A` Haulers and `6C` Tankers/Salvage) stocks fuel bunkers at owned hubs (`ridgecrest`, `gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) and recovers battlefield wrecks once `salvage-network` activates; Crafters (`5A/5B`) supply armor plates, repair kits, fuel cells, and weapon modules; and Trade (`4C` Bursars and `4A/4B` Haulers) converts harvested apex biological drops into treasury cash via anti-manipulation guarded Exchange sales [2][4][5].

## Conclusion

The Fleet and Defense division is engineered to serve two continuous missions without conflict: compounding combat experience and high-margin biological loot during peacetime while maintaining an immediately deployable 70-ship combined-arms force capable of station defense, convoy escort, and coalition warfare. Every architectural decision is anchored in empirical mechanics rather than cosmetic roleplay. Assigning Voidborn ships to Command, Carrier, and Medic roles, Crimson ships to Heavy DPS and Battle Line assault, and Solarian ships to Interdiction and Heavy Fire-Support locks permanent birth-empire modifiers into every tier from T1 through T5.

Three operational invariants govern the division's ongoing optimization and confidence level. First, pack ROI and survivability gates (`min_loot_per_ship >= 250 cr`, `flee_hull_pct: 80`, `f9_gauge` ammo checks, and tiered 6/8/10-jump fuel radii) must stay intact as ships graduate from T1 into T3 `Dialectic`, `Gestalt`, `Concordance`, `Aporia`, and `Remedy` hulls; relaxing those gates re-introduces the early-era failure mode where starter hulls burned fuel on 6-cr prey or died piecemeal to NPC pirates. Second, once average Task Force and Sentinel hulls reach T2-T3 with full shield/armor fitting, the escort `min_power` gate and `packs.pirates_enabled` flag can be lowered methodically to reclaim contested pirate belts and execute offensive fleet operations against rival strongholds. Third, this assessment rests on high confidence for live PvE pack execution and upgrade routing (verified directly against `hive.py`, `hunt_net.py`, and `upgrade-policy.json`) and moderate confidence for large-scale fleet-versus-fleet PvP doctrine until live T3+ Task Force engagements provide direct combat telemetry. Evidence that rival blocs shift heavily into cloaked interceptor raids or heavy capital fleets would require expanding Solarian Interdictor and Voidborn Medic ratios inside `1B` and `1C`.

Looking across the T1 through T5 progression horizon, Fleet and Defense follows a clear phased escalation curve. During the active T1-to-T2 commissioning wave (`Axiom -> Theorem`, `Cleaver -> Broadaxe`, `Precept -> Penumbra/Anamnesis`), the priority is zero-loss Piloting and Gunnery XP compounding on high-margin wildlife packs (`>= 750 cr` total and `>= 250 cr/ship`) near Bursar and Lockbox hubs [1][5]. Once average `1A` and `1C` hulls cross T2-T3 (`Concordance`, `Dialectic`, `Gestalt`, `Aporia`, `Remedy`), Fleet and Defense unlocks three transformative strategic capabilities: (1) full 3-to-5-ship armed convoy escorts via `escort_proto.py` enabling `3A`/`3C`/`6A` operations inside pirate-contested systems; (2) offensive NPC pirate stronghold and raider pack clearing (`packs.pirates_enabled: true`, `pirate_min_power_ratio: 2.2`); and (3) credible rapid-response territorial deterrence protecting stations #2 through #5 across all five empire regions [1][5][8]. Unit-economic discipline remains non-negotiable throughout: every jump tier (6J normal, 8J Tier-S, 10J apex) must earn back tier-scaled fuel burn plus repair amortization while building irreplaceable crew skill levels that rival mission-swarm factions lack [3][5].

## General Lessons

1. **Separate strategic deterrence from opportunistic raiding in fleet design.** Mixing command-and-control vessels, electronic interdictors, and repair ships into ad-hoc solo hunter loops wastes their unique multipliers. Dedicated combined-arms wings (`1A`, `1B`, `1C`) preserve role ratios so peacetime training builds the exact force composition needed for coalition warfare [1][2].
2. **Gate combat commitment on effective live capability, not nominal hull tier.** A T2 gunship with an empty ammunition magazine has zero effective DPS. Measuring live fitted firepower with verified cargo ammunition (`f9_gauge`) and comparing against scale-free enemy effective hit points prevents catastrophic losses across every tier from T0 through T5 [5].
3. **Prefer soft economic weighting over hard binary bans whenever rare exceptions exist.** Scoring lawless hunting grounds at 1.45x and policed zones at 0.65x captures the statistical superiority of lawless loot while preserving opportunistic access to profitable policed herds when frontier space is temporarily contested or cool [5][6].
4. **Tier travel distance strictly to expected payload value.** Burning a 10-jump round trip for ordinary prey turns combat into a negative-margin fuel sink, whereas reserving 8-to-10-jump reaches strictly for Tier-S (`>= 550 cr`) and apex (`>= 1,800 cr`) herds aligns fuel expenditure with marginal profit per jump [5][7].
5. **Maintain peacetime economic utility for military assets.** Idle warships depreciate in relative XP and skill level against active rivals like `[BUSY]` and `[IRLC]`. Routing combined-arms Task Forces into profitable apex wildlife hunts during peacetime turns defense overhead into self-funding crew mastery and cash generation [3][5].
6. **Preserve explicit support ratios before war breaks out.** Interdictors (`9` Solarian) and Fleet Medics (`9` Voidborn) appear less lucrative than raw DPS hulls on single-target spreadsheets, yet in multi-ship combat they determine whether high-value targets escape and whether T4-T5 flagships survive focus fire [1][2].
7. **Automate hull and module logistics so combat crews never stall on administrative bottlenecks.** Removing arbitrary per-pass upgrade throttles (`max_active: 500`, bounded only by the 2M treasury floor and shipyard stock) while fitting modules via authoritative `read.db:items.slot` keeps combat strength compounding continuously with zero manual babysitting [1][9].
