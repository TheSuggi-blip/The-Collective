# Ore Bulk Mining Wing (3B) Operations, Industrial Supply Base, and Anti-Glut Discipline

## Abstract

Wing `3B. Ore -- Bulk` is The Collective's 22-ship industrial feedstock backbone, composed of Solarian Enterprise Utility Vessels (`EUV` prefix) dedicated to high-tonnage extraction of structural base ores (`iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`, `silicon_ore`, `aluminum_ore`, and `titanium_ore`) whenever station construction, crafting lines, or faction reserves signal real demand [1][2]. Running on `mine-network` (`game/fleet/mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json`) with distinct `_wing_mine_multiplier` preferences, `3B` ensures that core building materials needed for sovereign stations, outposts, hulls, and zero-fuel station crafting never starve even while `3A` chases rare metals and `3C` hunts radioactive deposits [2][3]. Every `3B` hull progresses along the Solarian heavy-ore ladder (`archimedes` -> `resonance_miner` -> `bonanza_king` -> `lithosphere` -> `tellurian`), scaling cargo volume and multi-laser tonnage throughput to feed industrial facilities at Collective Resort Ridgecrest and future empire hubs [1][4]. Critically, `3B` is bound by strict anti-glut safeguards (`skip_full_reserves: true`, `hard_glut_skip_units: 200000`, `market_min_bid: 12`, and saturation decay in `score_belt_candidates`) so bulk miners immediately pivot into higher-value industrial or market ores once base-metal reserves hit target [3][5]. The result is an elastic industrial foundation: zero base-metal bottlenecks during rapid station expansion, and zero wasted fuel mining unsellable copper gluts during peacetime. Positioned directly under Standing Goals 3 and 4, Wing `3B` guarantees sovereign self-sufficiency across all five planned empire-region stations and eight fortified corridor outposts by keeping `iron_ore`, `silicon_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`, and `titanium_ore` flowing into lockboxes at internal extraction cost rather than inflated NPC ask prices [1][5][6]. Automated no-throttle hull commissioning scales all 22 Solarian bulk vessels from `Archimedes` to `Tellurian` above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor, while symmetric `Mining -> Faction` handover logging credits `3B`'s industrial tonnage accurately inside IFRS 8 Section 5 without double-counting external revenue [1][10].

## Introduction

Every sovereign space empire runs on bulk industrial feedstock. Constructing and maintaining five specialized regional stations (Resort Station #1 at Collective Resort Ridgecrest with `13` active hospitality/quarters facilities, Trade Station #2, Dedicated Foundry Station #3 at `Lanternwick`/`Avior`, Shipyard Station #4, and Fortress Station #5), eight corridor outposts, steel plates, circuit boards, hull frames, and repair kits requires hundreds of thousands of units of `iron_ore`, `silicon_ore`, `carbon_ore`, `copper_ore`, and `titanium_ore` [4][6]. Rival industrial giant `[IRLC]` demonstrates the sheer scale required to dominate galactic manufacturing: its 275-ship fleet extracts `+250,200 ore/h` across 8 sovereign bases, converting bulk mineral flow into `4.22M cr/h` wealth growth and dominant ship/module production [7].

Yet bulk mining carries the single most insidious economic trap in SpaceMolt: **inventory glut destruction**. Common ores sit right outside Empire capital stations in huge deposits, making them temptingly easy for naive mining bots to harvest endlessly. Without strict demand gating, bulk miners pile 300,000+ units of 4-cr `copper_ore` and `iron_ore` into faction storage -- burning continuous jump fuel, clogging storage bays, depressing local exchange bids to 2-3 cr, and generating negative real operating cash flow [3][5]. Earlier fleet telemetry proved how severe this distortion can be: raising depletion floors blindly or skipping saturation checks caused miners to either stall completely or flood lockboxes with unbid common ore [3][8].

Wing `3B. Ore -- Bulk` (22 ships, 16.9 percent of Mining) resolves this tension by pairing dedicated Solarian bulk tonnage capacity with demand-driven elasticity. When `needs.tsv`, `craft-demand.json`, or station construction projects open a real base-metal deficit (`tier == "deficit"` or `tier == "craft_demand"`), `3B` absorbs the bulk volume near our lockboxes and owned stations at minimal fuel burn (`<= 5-6` jumps) [3][5]. The moment faction reserves and crafting queues are full, `mine_net.py`'s saturation curve and `skip_full_reserves` automatically redirect `3B` into liquid mid-tier industrial ores (`silicon_ore`, `titanium_ore`, `aluminum_ore`, `palladium_ore`) rather than dumping worthless surplus into storage [3][5].

The physical throughput math of galactic station building clarifies why a dedicated 22-ship bulk ore wing is essential. Building a single sovereign station, equipping 13+ industrial facilities like Collective Resort Ridgecrest, and keeping continuous `steel_plate`, `circuit_board`, `reinforced_glass`, and `armor_plate` manufacturing lines running consumes enormous raw tonnage [4][6][11]. Buying that bulk feedstock on NPC markets drains treasury cash rapidly and hands margin straight to rival miners like `[IRLC]` (`+250.2k ore/h`) and `[ENDL]` (`+99.8k ore/h`) [7]. Conversely, dedicating too many hulls to bulk extraction -- or failing to gate bulk mining behind real `needs.tsv` and `craft-demand.json` ceilings -- turns 50+ ships into negative-margin iron dumpers that clog lockboxes and starve rare-mineral wings [3][5]. Calibrating `3B` at 22 Solarian vessels (16.9 percent of Mining) paired with strict demand-pull scoring hits the exact industrial sweet spot: fast deficit closure during construction waves and automatic market-metal pivoting during steady-state operations [1][3].

## Findings

### Wing Specification, Progression Ladder, and Dispatcher Conditions

Verified manifest and policy records (`game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, `game/routes/mine-network.json`) establish `3B`'s operational parameters [1][2][9]:

- **Fleet Size & Lineage:** 22 Solarian `EUV` hulls (`3B. Ore -- Bulk`), enjoying full Solarian native extraction bonuses [1][2].
- **Hull Ladder:** `archimedes` (T1) -> `resonance_miner` (T2) -> `bonanza_king` (T3) -> `lithosphere` (T4) -> `tellurian` (T5) [1]. All 22 hulls commission automatically through `upgrades.py` whenever cash sits above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor and shipyard stock is available [1].
- **Dispatcher Priority & Wing Multiplier:** `dispatch.py` routes `3B` through `fit-network` (ensuring optimal mining laser loadouts and cargo expansion) before assigning `mine-network` [9]. Inside `mine_net.py:_wing_mine_multiplier("3B.", res, tools)`, bulk structural ores (`iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`, `silicon_ore`, `titanium_ore`, `aluminum_ore`) receive priority weighting when demand is open, cleanly partitioning `3B` from `3A` (rare precision metals/crystals) and `3C` (radioactive/deep-core) [3].

### Demand Tiers, Anti-Glut Protection, and Belt Utilization Rules

`3B` obeys four tightly interlocking economic controls inside `mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json` [3][5]:

1. **Deficit & Craft-Demand Priority:** `mine_net.py` reads live open shortages from `read.db` / `needs.tsv` (`needs_max_priority: 3`) and active industrial manufacturing inputs from `game/craft-demand.json` [3][5]. When a station kit, facility build, or Logistics `steel_plate`/`circuit_board` chain needs `iron_ore` or `silicon_ore`, `tier == "deficit"` elevates those belts to top priority (`_TIER_RANK["deficit"]`), allowing up to an 8-jump reach if necessary while favoring belts within 0 to 5 jumps of lockbox hubs (`hub_gravity_bonus: 1.10x-1.25x`) [3][5].
2. **Hard Anti-Glut & Saturation Decay:** To prevent `3B` from mining unsellable bulk gluts, `mine_net.py` enforces three layered blocks: (a) `skip_full_reserves: true` skips any non-deficit resource sitting in `full_items` unless external bids exceed `high_value_market_min_bid` (25 cr); (b) `is_craft` caps craft-driven mining strictly below `max(1500, 0.85 * real_market_qty + min(craft_need, 3000))`; and (c) `hard_glut_skip_units: 200000` plus `skip_hard_glut_when_relocating: true` blocks XP-fallback mining of any common ore already held above 200,000 units, forcing adaptive relocation to profitable hubs instead [3][5].
3. **Optimal Belt Caps & Depletion Floor:** Bulk ore belts routinely contain 10,000 to 200,000+ units. Under `adaptive_belt_cap` (`max_per_belt: 3`, `units_per_ship_run: 400`), abundant `>= 10,000`-unit belts permit up to **3 concurrent miners**, mid-size `1,000-9,999`-unit belts permit **2 miners**, and depleted `< 3%` pockets drop to **1 miner** [3][5]. Bulk non-scarce belts enforce `min_left_pct: 5.0%`, leaving healthy regeneration mass while allowing full multi-run extraction before rotating via 30-minute cooldowns [3][5].
4. **Hub-Centric Zero-Rent Deposit Policy:** `3B` deposits strictly into whitelisted `deposit_stations` (`ridgecrest` rent-free flagship resort, Empire core hubs, and faction lockbox bases) under `deposit_mode: "faction"` (`allow_lawless_bursar_dropoffs: false`) [5]. This eliminated an earlier operational leak where miners stranded 63 loads of iron inside personal storage at third-party player stations with no Logistics pickup route [5]. Every deposit logs symmetric `Mining -> Faction` credit into `handover.jsonl` for IFRS 8 Section 5 attribution [10].

### Cross-Division Synergy and Operational Overlap

`3B` is tightly coupled with the industrial engine of The Collective:
- **`3B` <-> `3A` Overlap:** Whenever all base-metal deficits are green (`0` open `needs.tsv` ore shortages), `3B` ships naturally overlap onto `titanium_ore`, `silicon_ore`, and mid-tier market metals, ensuring zero idle bulk capacity [3].
- **`3B` -> Craft (`5A` Station Refiners & `5B` Field Workshops):** Bulk `iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`, and `silicon_ore` feed zero-fuel refining into `steel_plate`, `copper_wiring`, `circuit_board`, `reinforced_glass`, and `flex_polymer` -- cutting Logistics build costs by eliminating NPC market input purchases [11].
- **`3B` -> Logistics (`6A` Supply Chain Bulk Haulers):** `6A` consolidates lockbox ore from satellite stations into Collective Resort Ridgecrest (`a2cee88abdcb31db229c04315f0ef6e1`) and regional construction sites (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) [5][12].
- **`3B` -> Trade (`4C` Bursars):** Excess unreserved industrial ore above reserve ceilings is excluded from Bursar buy orders (`exclude_buy_items: iron_ore, copper_ore, carbon_ore`) and liquidated opportunistically only above `safe_sell_floor` (72 percent of oracle fair price) [13].

## Conclusion

Wing `3B. Ore -- Bulk` gives The Collective a self-regulating industrial supply engine sized accurately at 22 Solarian vessels (16.9 percent of Mining) -- large enough to feed simultaneous station expansion, outpost fortification, and zero-fuel crafting lines, yet small enough never to cannibalize rare-ore (`3A`), radioactive (`3C`), gas (`3D`), or ice (`3E`) throughput. The key architectural strength of `3B` is elasticity: driven by live `needs.tsv` shortages, `craft-demand.json`, and `skip_full_reserves`, the wing expands instantly into bulk base-metal extraction during construction surges and pivots smoothly into cash-positive industrial metals whenever bins fill.

Confidence in `3B`'s operating framework is high, verified against live `mine_net.py` guards, 129/130 active Mining utilization, and elimination of stranded third-party iron dropoffs [5][14]. As Collective Resort Ridgecrest completes its pure Resort buildout (Station #1) and The Collective constructs Trade Hub Station #2, Dedicated Industrial Foundry Station #3 (`Lanternwick`/`Avior`), Shipyard Station #4, Fortress Station #5, and 8 fortified outposts, `3B` demand for `iron_ore`, `silicon_ore`, and `titanium_ore` will grow steadily. Upgrading `3B` hulls into T2 `Resonance Miner`, T3 `Bonanza King`, and T4 `Lithosphere` (`max_auto_tier: 2` now active) will multiply ore tonnage per jump without expanding headcount. The main condition that would warrant re-tuning `3B` is sustained base-metal starvation during multi-station construction (signaled by persistent red `needs.tsv` iron/silicon deficits), which would justify temporarily widening `deficit` jump range or shifting idle `3A` capacity onto priority industrial veins.

Scaling `3B` across the T1 through T5 Solarian ladder (`Archimedes -> Resonance Miner -> Bonanza King -> Lithosphere -> Tellurian`) multiplies the collective bulk industrial capacity of The Collective without adding a single extra hull slot from our 500-ship cap [1][2]. Because bulk belts routinely contain 10,000 to 200,000+ units close to empire and owned hubs, larger cargo holds translate almost one-for-one into lower jump-fuel expenditure per thousand units of `iron_ore` and `silicon_ore` delivered to Collective Resort Ridgecrest and future regional stations [3][5]. Keeping `3B` restricted to `<= 6` jumps (`max_station_jumps: 5`) for normal ores and `<= 8` jumps for active construction deficits enforces hard unit-economic discipline -- preventing bulk ships from chasing distant 5-cr iron while leaving 10-jump mega-ore reaches exclusively to `3A` and `3C` [3][5]. Live verification metrics that confirm ongoing `3B` health are zero chronic red base-metal rows in `needs.tsv`, `hard_glut_skip_units` preventing >200k unbid copper/iron buildup, and high `5A`/`5B` industrial crafting utilization consuming `3B` lockbox stock continuously [5][11]. Anchored by demand-pull scoring, strict anti-glut skips, and lockbox-only depositing, Wing `3B` supplies the exact industrial tonnage required to fortify five regional stations and eight outposts without wasting fuel on surplus junk [3][5][6].

## General Lessons

1. **Make bulk extraction demand-pulled, never supply-pushed.** Unconstrained bulk mining is the fastest way to turn jump fuel into worthless warehouse clutter. Tying bulk wings strictly to `needs.tsv`, `craft-demand.json`, `skip_full_reserves`, and `hard_glut_skip_units` converts bulk mining from a cash sink into a responsive industrial utility [3][5].
2. **Never deposit industrial feedstock where Logistics cannot retrieve it.** Allowing miners to dump bulk ore into personal storage at unstaffed or foreign stations strands working capital permanently. Whitelisting lockbox-backed `deposit_stations` guarantees 100 percent of extracted tonnage remains accessible to `6A` Haulers and `5A` Refiners [5].
3. **Preserve 3-ship concurrency on abundant belts while protecting respawn floors.** Rich 10,000-to-200,000-unit bulk deposits absorb up to 3 concurrent miners cleanly without collision, while a 5.0 percent depletion floor preserves field regeneration over multi-day industrial campaigns [3][5].
4. **Coordinate buy-order exclusions with bulk mining output.** Explicitly listing `iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, and `carbon_ore` in `bursar-policy.json:exclude_buy_items` prevents Trade Bursars from spending treasury cash buying common ores that `3B` extracts internally at fuel cost [13].
5. **Size bulk wings for peak construction bursts, but rely on dynamic fallback during peacetime.** Keeping 22 ships in `3B` avoids both under-supply during station builds and idle hulls between builds because `mine_net.py` seamlessly redirects excess `3B` capacity to market-grade industrial ores [1][3].
6. **Prevent XP-fallback mining from becoming a stealth glut generator.** Enabling `skip_hard_glut_when_relocating: true` alongside `hard_glut_skip_units: 200000` forces miners in saturated starter systems to relocate adaptively toward profitable hubs instead of endlessly mining worthless 200k-glut copper [3][5].
7. **Audit live belt-slot survival before tightening global depletion floors.** Raising depletion floors blindly from 5 percent to 20 percent previously halved active in-plan slots on dual copper-iron belts; holding bulk belts at 5.0 percent preserves regeneration while keeping all 22 `3B` hulls productively employed [5][8].
8. **Separate bulk construction supply from commercial rare-ore hunting via wing multipliers.** Giving `3B` priority on open structural deficits frees `3A` and `3C` to stay locked on 35-to-600 cr rare minerals without industrial interruption [2][3].
