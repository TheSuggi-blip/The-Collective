# Deep-Core and Rad Mining Wing (3C) Operations, Exotic Extraction, and Hazard Protocol

## Abstract

Wing `3C. Deep-Core & Rad` is The Collective's 18-ship specialized high-value hazardous extraction unit, staffed by Solarian Enterprise Utility Vessels (`EUV` prefix) focused on the rarest, highest-priced radioactive and exotic deep-core minerals in SpaceMolt (`uranium_ore`, `radium_ore`, `lead_ore`, `silver_ore`, `palladium_ore`, `platinum_ore`, `dark_matter_residue`, and `quantum_fragments`) [1][2]. Operating under `mine-network` (`game/fleet/mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json`), `3C` diverges from standard ore wings at Tier 3 by branching onto the specialized `deep_resonance` hull (`archimedes` -> `resonance_miner` -> `deep_resonance` -> `lithosphere` -> `tellurian`) paired with deep-core lasers and radiation/hazmat protection fitted through `fit-network` [1][3]. Live galaxy-wide field and market audits reveal the extraordinary scarcity and value of `3C`'s target basket: `uranium_ore` commands 600 cr/u (only 1 known belt, 26 units remaining), `lead_ore` commands 120 cr/u (7 belts, 119-373 units), `silver_ore` commands 101 cr/u (9 lawless belts, 13-44 units per thin pocket), `radium_ore` commands 45 cr/u (1 belt, 26 units), and `dark_matter_residue` commands 40 cr/u [4][5]. Because these ultra-scarce deposits sit across both lawless frontiers and rare policed pockets with tiny unit counts, `3C` depends critically on three tuned mechanics: the 10-jump mega-value reach (`>= 100 cr/u`), the safe-zone scarce-ore exception ("avoid safe zones, never forbid"), a hard 1-ship cap on `< 3%` thin belts, and a 0.2 percent scarce depletion floor with a 15-unit minimum floor [5][6]. Advancing Standing Goals 1, 4, and 5, Wing `3C` targets the steepest value-density frontier in SpaceMolt -- capturing radioactive and exotic deposits worth up to 600 cr per unit (`uranium_ore`, `lead_ore`, `silver_ore`, `radium_ore`, `dark_matter_residue`) that bottleneck end-game T3-T5 manufacturing, reactors, and high-tier modules [1][4][7]. By uniting dedicated Solarian T3 `deep_resonance` hardware with whole-map `7B` surveyor intelligence, net-external `market_guard.py` pricing, and zero-throttle automatic hull commissioning above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor, `3C` secures strategic materials rivals overlook or over-crowd [1][5][8][9].

## Introduction

In SpaceMolt's end-game crafting and sovereign warfare economy, T3-T5 hulls, advanced reactors, shields, quantum modules, and high-tier munitions are bottlenecked not by common steel, but by radioactive isotopes, heavy shielding metals, and deep-core exotics [2][7]. Unlike common asteroid belts holding 100,000+ units, radioactive and deep-core deposits appear in tiny, scattered pockets across the 512-system map -- frequently containing only 13 to 400 remaining units at `< 1%` to `3%` remaining capacity [4]. A single unmonitored rival hauler or precision miner can strip an entire `uranium_ore` or `radium_ore` vein in one pass, locking out latecomers for hours until server regeneration ticks refill the deposit [4][5].

Standard mining configurations fail completely on deep-core and radioactive minerals for four distinct reasons. First, a rigid 5 percent or 15 percent depletion floor locks miners out of 100 percent of thin radioactive pockets (such as 26-unit `uranium_ore` or 44-unit `silver_ore` sitting below 1 percent remaining) [5]. Second, a naive `policed_only: true` or `lawless_only: true` binary filter cuts off half the radioactive supply: live `read.db:fields` data shows `silver_ore` (9 lawless / 0 policed) is strictly lawless, whereas `uranium_ore` (0/1), `radium_ore` (0/1), `lead_ore` (1/6), `dark_matter_residue` (0/2), and `palladium_ore` (0/4) currently spawn predominantly in policed pockets [4]. Third, sending 2 or 3 ships simultaneously to a 26-unit uranium pocket wastes 10 jumps of fuel on every redundant vessel [5]. Fourth, a blanket 6-jump range puts isolated 600-cr uranium and 120-cr lead veins completely out of reach, whereas an unrestricted 10-jump range on cheap ores ruins fleet fuel margins [5][6].

Wing `3C. Deep-Core & Rad` (18 ships, 13.8 percent of Mining) solves all four failure modes simultaneously. By dedicating a specialized Solarian wing to deep-core hulls (`deep_resonance`), pairing them with `7B. Deep-Core Surveyors` for whole-map vein discovery, and unlocking exact mega-ore range, cap, and depletion rules inside `mine_net.py`, The Collective captures the highest-value per-unit minerals in the galaxy before rivals can react [1][5][8].

Quantifying the exact scarcity profile of SpaceMolt's 512-system map highlights why `3C` operates under specialized rules distinct from both `3A` and `3B`. Across the entire charted galaxy in `read.db:fields` (2026-11-27), `uranium_ore` (600 cr/u) appears in only 1 known belt with 26 units remaining; `radium_ore` (45 cr/u) appears in 1 belt with 26 units remaining; `silver_ore` (101 cr/u) appears in 9 lawless pockets holding 13 to 44 units each; and `lead_ore` (120 cr/u) appears in 7 scattered pockets holding 119 to 373 units [4]. By comparison, rival industrial juggernauts like `[IRLC]` (`+250.2k ore/h`) and `[BUSY]` focus heavily on mass extraction around fixed bases, leaving scattered outer-rim and policed anomalies open to a fleet with whole-map 4-hour census freshness and precision single-ship dispatching [4][8]. Wing `3C` turns that exact informational and algorithmic edge into a persistent strategic monopoly over SpaceMolt's scarcest atomic feedstocks.

## Findings

### Roster, Specialized Deep-Core Ladder, and Wing Multiplier

Exact records in `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/fleet/mine_net.py` define `3C`'s configuration [1][2][5]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 18 Solarian `EUV` hulls (`3C. Deep-Core & Rad`), representing 13.8 percent of the 130-ship Mining division [1][2].
- **Distinct Hull Progression:** `archimedes` (T1) -> `resonance_miner` (T2) -> **`deep_resonance` (T3)** -> `lithosphere` (T4) -> `tellurian` (T5) [1]. Inserting `deep_resonance` at T3 equips `3C` with specialized deep-core extraction hardpoints and survivability tailored to exotic and radioactive asteroid environments [1][2].
- **Tool & Wing Specialization:** `fit-network` provisions specialized ore/deep-core lasers and shield/hazmat survivability modules [3], while `mine_net.py:_wing_mine_multiplier("3C.", res, tools)` applies elevated preference scores to radioactive and deep-core target resources (`uranium_ore`, `radium_ore`, `lead_ore`, `dark_matter_residue`, `palladium_ore`, `platinum_ore`, `silver_ore`, `darksteel_ore`) so `3C` leaves common ores to `3B` and focuses on ultra-scarce deposits [5].

### Empirical Scarce-Deposit Rules: Range, Caps, Floors, and Safe/Lawless Logic

Live inventory audits of `read.db:fields` cross-joined against `price_oracle.json` (2026-11-27) yield the exact empirical parameters governing `3C` inside `mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json` [4][5][6]:

- **Mega-Ore 10-Jump Exception:** Ordinary ores (`< 60 cr/u`) are capped at **6 jumps** (`max_station_jumps: 5`), and high-value ores (`60-99 cr/u` or scarce/deficit) reach **8 jumps**, whereas mega-value ores **`>= 100 cr/u`** (`uranium_ore` 600 cr, `lead_ore` 120 cr, `silver_ore` 101 cr) unlock the full **10-jump** reach [5][6]. At 101 to 600 cr/u, even a partial 40-unit load generates 4,040 to 24,000 cr -- comfortably paying 10-jump round-trip fuel (~950-1,500 cr) several times over [5].
- **Soft Safe-Zone Penalty with Scarce Exemption:** `mine_net.py` applies a **1.35x lawless bonus** and **0.75x safe-zone penalty** (`policed_only: false`), but automatically waives the safe-zone penalty for scarce resources (`>= 40 cr/u` or `< 3%` remaining) [5][6]. This allows `3C` to harvest lawless `silver_ore` and `darksteel_ore` aggressively without ever skipping policed `uranium_ore` (600 cr), `lead_ore` (120 cr), or `radium_ore` (45 cr) [4][5].
- **Strict 1-Ship Cap on Thin Scarce Veins:** Under `adaptive_belt_cap`, any scarce belt below 3 percent remaining capacity (e.g., 26u `uranium_ore`, 26u `radium_ore`, 13-44u `silver_ore`, 119-373u `lead_ore`) is hard-capped at **1 ship** [5][6]. Atomic locking in `mine_claims.json` prevents a second `3C` or `3A` miner from duplicating the jump, eliminating wasted transit fuel [5].
- **0.2% Scarce Floor + 15-Unit Mega-Ore Floor:** `effective_min_left_pct()` lowers the belt floor from 5.0 percent to **`0.2%`** for scarce high-cash ores (`>= 30 cr/u` or `>= 150,000 cr` real market cash) and admits `>= 100 cr/u` mega-ores down to **15 remaining units** even if percentage remaining dips slightly below 0.2 percent (capturing 44-unit `silver_ore` pockets at 0.18 percent cleanly) [5][6].
- **Oracle Protection Against Thin-Market Manipulation:** Because radioactive and exotic ores trade in low-volume books, `market_guard.py`'s `load_cltv_buy_qty` subtraction and `<= 3 cr` trash-bid filtering are vital: earlier boolean filtering and raw volume-weighted averages collapsed `radium_ore` (45 cr) and `silver_ore` (101 cr) to 2-cr trash valuations or masked `[BUSY]`'s 468k silver buy-wall [9]. Unpoisoning those prices immediately redirected live miners straight onto scarce veins [9].

### Cross-Division Synergy and Operational Overlap

- **Intel (`7B` Deep-Core Surveyors) <-> `3C`:** `3C` is the primary consumer of `7B`'s belt-scanning specialization inside `census-network` [8]. Because scarce veins often hold fewer than 100 units and regenerate sporadically across outer-rim and frontier systems, 4-hour whole-map census freshness directly determines `3C`'s hit rate [8].
- **`3C` <-> `3A` Overlap:** When all radioactive and mega-ore veins are on cooldown or claimed by a single `3C` vessel, remaining `3C` ships overlap seamlessly onto high-value `3A` precision metals (`titanium_ore`, `darksteel_ore`, `energy_crystal`) so zero deep-core capacity sits idle [5].
- **`3C` -> Craft (`5A/5B`) & Trade (`4A/4B/4C`):** Radioactive ores and `lead_ore` feed advanced reactor, hazmat, and shielding production lines as well as high-margin Bursar and Express Arbitrage sales; `6A` Bulk Haulers move lockbox reserves only with hazmat-rated holds when moving hazardous cargo [10][11].

## Conclusion

Wing `3C. Deep-Core & Rad` punches far above its 18-ship headcount by dominating the steepest value-per-unit segment of SpaceMolt's extraction economy. Where naive mining fleets either ignore 600-cr uranium because of rigid 5-percent floors and 6-jump caps, or hemorrhage fuel by dogpiling three ships onto a 26-unit radium vein, `3C` combines dedicated T3 `deep_resonance` progression with precision empirical controls: a 10-jump mega-ore reach (`>= 100 cr/u`), a hard 1-ship lock on `< 3%` pockets, a 0.2 percent / 15-unit scarce harvest floor, and safe-zone scarce exemptions.

Confidence in these settings is high because every threshold (`100 cr`, `0.2%`, `15u`, `1-ship cap`, safe/lawless split) is derived directly from live `read.db:fields` remaining-unit distributions and `price_oracle.json` bids rather than guesswork [4][9]. As The Collective commissions T3 `deep_resonance` and T4 `lithosphere` hulls and expands toward 5 regional stations across all 5 empire regions, `3C`'s average approach distance to frontier exotic veins will shrink further, converting even more of its gross ore value into net profit. The main indicator requiring future adjustment would be server patches introducing new deep-core or radioactive ores requiring specialized hazmat or strip-mining modules, which `fit-network` and `mine_net.py:resource_tool_kind()` can incorporate immediately via `read.db:items` inspection.

Looking forward across our T2-T5 fleet ramp, `3C`'s unique T3 branch onto `deep_resonance` (`Archimedes -> Resonance Miner -> Deep Resonance -> Lithosphere -> Tellurian`) creates a structural hardware advantage as The Collective moves into T3-T5 ship, shield, and reactor manufacturing [1][7]. Even more importantly, expanding from 1 sovereign station (`ridgecrest`) to 5 regional empire stations and 8 frontier outposts (Standing Goal 3) will scatter rent-free faction lockboxes across all four galactic quadrants -- shrinking `3C`'s average approach distance (`hops`) and return distance (`st_hops`) to isolated radioactive veins from 8-10 jumps down to 2-5 jumps [5][8]. That drop in transit time directly doubles first-mover capture rate whenever `7B` surveyors spot a freshly regenerated `uranium_ore`, `lead_ore`, or `silver_ore` pocket. Continuous monitoring of `mine_claims.json` hit rates on `>= 100 cr/u` ores and hazmat-verified `6A` transport from regional lockboxes will confirm that every harvested radioactive unit converts cleanly into top-tier modules or peak external cash [5][10]. By combining specialized `deep_resonance` hardware, 10-jump mega-ore reach (`>= 100 cr/u`), single-ship scarce belt locks, and 0.2 percent / 15-unit depletion floors, Wing `3C` guarantees The Collective captures every rare atomic vein that emerges across all 512 star systems [1][4][5][6]. Keeping `3C` tightly synchronized with whole-map `7B` deep-core survey scans and hazmat-verified `6A` consolidation guarantees rapid conversion of every rare pocket into top-tier modules or liquid cash [8][10].

## General Lessons

1. **Ultra-scarce resources require fundamentally different harvesting math than bulk commodities.** A 5-percent floor and 3-ship cap optimize 100,000-unit iron belts but completely break on 26-unit 600-cr uranium pockets. Scaling both the depletion floor (`0.2%` / `15u`) and concurrency cap (`1` ship) to actual deposit headroom eliminates both missed profits and redundant fuel burn [5].
2. **Never let a binary zone filter blind a specialist wing to rare anomalies.** Even when 80-100 percent of high-tier resources live in lawless space, rare high-value pockets (`uranium_ore`, `lead_ore`, `radium_ore`) can spawn in policed sectors. Preferential scoring with explicit scarce exceptions beats hard zone bans every time [4][5].
3. **Justify long jump distances strictly by value density per cargo unit.** A 10-jump round trip is destructive at 10 cr/u, marginal at 40 cr/u, and wildly profitable at 100-600 cr/u; tying jump caps to real oracle unit value (`6J / 8J / 10J`) aligns travel radius with unit economics automatically [5].
4. **Scarcity mining is only as fast as intelligence freshness.** Thin 20-to-50-unit veins vanish rapidly under rival competition; pairing `3C` directly with `7B` whole-map surveyor scans creates a real-time first-mover edge [8].
5. **Thin markets demand double-sided anti-manipulation protection.** Rare ore order books are equally vulnerable to 1-unit upward spoofs and 2-cr downward trash walls; Cash-Weighted Median + trash-bid filtering preserves true price discovery across scarce commodities [9].
6. **Never let stale unreachable-belt errors trap specialist miners in infinite loops.** `mine_net.py`'s per-pilot 6-hour unreachable-belt cooldown (`_mark_unreachable_belt`) ensures that if a deep-core vein sits behind a severed lane or temporary hazard, the `3C` ship pivots immediately to the next highest-value target instead of hammering one dead POI [5].
7. **Pair specialized extraction wings with hazmat-aware downstream logistics.** Mining radioactive ores into remote lockboxes creates value only when downstream haulers (`6A`/`4B`) verify `has_hazmat` capability and move exotic stockpiles safely into central refineries and exchanges [10].
