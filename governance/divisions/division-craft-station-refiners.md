# Station Refiners and Bursars Wing (5A) Operations, Zero-Fuel Manufacturing, and Facility Economics

## Abstract

Wing `5A. Station Refiners & Bursars` is The Collective's 30-ship zero-fuel station manufacturing and commercial refining backbone, manned by Nebula Industrial Fabrication Vessels (`IFV` prefix) progressing from `congregation` (T1) to `conglomerate` (T4) [1][2]. Sharing the 55-station `bursar-network` footprint directly with `4C. Pure Bursar Desks` (`wing_prefix: ["4C.", "5A."]`, `max_ships: 55`, strictly `1` desk per station), `5A` merges live station market-making with high-throughput zero-fuel refining and crafting across all 33 Empire NPC Exchanges, Collective Resort Ridgecrest (Station #1, our dedicated Resort & Travel Hub with 13 active resort/hospitality/quarters facilities), and the top 21 calm liquid rival Exchanges, while reserving heavy zero-rent industrial foundry construction for dedicated Station #3 (`Lanternwick`/`Avior`) [3][4][5][16]. Every 60-second poll cycle inside `bursar.py` (`game/bursar-policy.json`), `5A` ships execute up to 80 crafting runs across 10 cycles (`max_buy_spend_cr: 180,000`, `min_margin_pct: 15%`, `min_profit_cr: 50 cr`) -- converting harvested ores (`3A`-`3C`), gases (`3D`), ices (`3E`), and rare Hunter biologicals (`2A/2B`) into finished high-margin goods (`superconductor`, `power_cell`, `targeting_computer`, `reinforced_glass`, `flex_polymer`, hospitality drinks/rations) and maintaining `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` `fuel_cell` output at zero transit fuel burn [4][6]. Rival telemetry from `[HIVE]` (`+2,152 crafts/h` at zero combat overhead) and `[NOIR]` (docked multi-hub manufacturing with zero sovereign bases) demonstrates how powerful zero-fuel docked crafting is as a compounding wealth multiplier [7]. By pairing Nebula industrial bonuses with 55 single-staffed Exchange desks (Empire + Ridgecrest + BUSY/IRLC/HEXC hubs) and manipulation-proof `market_guard.py` margins, `5A` converts raw extraction into high-ROIC finished goods around the clock until Station #3 Foundry eliminates NPC manufacturing rent altogether. Advancing Standing Goals 1, 3, and 4, Wing `5A` bridges upstream extraction and downstream commerce without burning a single unit of transit fuel -- turning raw lockbox minerals and opportunistic local ask purchases into high-ROIC finished components, hospitality consumables, and internal fuel cells across 55 distinct Exchange seats [3][4][5]. Automatic zero-throttle hull upgrades (`max_auto_tier: 2` active; `5A` skips P10 jump loops cleanly to stay permanently docked) advance `5A` hulls above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor while IAS 2 / `book_onetime` accounting keeps inventory spend cleanly separated from operating burn [1][13].

## Introduction

Raw minerals in SpaceMolt are price-takers; finished industrial components, electronics, fuel cells, armor plates, and resort consumables command structural value-added spreads above their raw feedstock cost [8]. Even better, docked station refining consumes **zero jump fuel** -- eliminating the 200,000 to 255,000 cr/h energy drag that constrains mobile mining and combat fleets [9]. Top galactic manufacturers prove the compounding power of zero-fuel crafting: `[HIVE]` logs `+2,152 crafts/h` across 200 ships (`AvgT 0.73`) generating `+685k cr/h` with zero PvE combat ships, while `[NOIR]` extracts sustained commercial profit purely through docked station crafting and trading across Empire Exchanges [7].

For The Collective, operating 55 distinct permanent Exchange seats (all 33 Empire NPC hubs + Collective Resort Ridgecrest + top 21 liquid calm rival Exchanges) backed by leased industrial NPC facilities today -- and shifting into a dedicated zero-rent Heavy Industrial & Station-Core Foundry on Sovereign Station #3 (`Lanternwick`/`Avior`) once funded -- creates an extraordinary vertical-integration advantage while keeping Station #1 (`Collective Resort Ridgecrest`, `a2cee88abdcb31db229c04315f0ef6e1`) focused purely on high-appeal passive Resort & Travel revenue (`13` active hospitality/quarters facilities, mirroring `[BUSY]` *The Obsidian Well* `248.3M` and `[HEXC]` *Hex Star* `218.7M`) [4][5][16]. When `3A`-`3E` miners deposit raw ore, gas, and ice directly into faction lockboxes, `5A` refiners sitting docked at those exact stations transform low-margin raw inputs into high-margin industrial goods without burning a single drop of fuel [4][5].

Yet autonomous station crafting introduces two subtle failure modes if left unguarded. First, crafting purely from unguarded local bids can trick refiners into spending 100,000 cr buying inputs to craft an item whose sole high bid is a 1-unit spoof or a CLTV self-buy order [10]. Second, allowing commercial profit crafters to burn reserved construction steel or circuit boards starves station expansion projects (violating strict `needs.tsv` reserve separation) [10]. Wing `5A` solves both problems by executing inside `bursar.py` under `market_guard.py` net-external bid verification (`load_cltv_buy_qty`), `respect_reserve: true`, and strict `15%` minimum net margin gating [4][6][11].

Examining the financial mechanics of SpaceMolt crafting highlights why `5A` is a compounding powerhouse. A mobile miner or hunter is bounded strictly by jump travel time and fuel burn (~200k-255k cr/h fleet-wide), whereas a docked Nebula `Congregation`/`Conglomerate` sitting inside Empire industrial hubs today -- and inside Sovereign Station #3's dedicated zero-rent Foundry complex once commissioned -- executes up to 80 manufacturing runs per 60-second poll cycle at **zero jump fuel** [4][5][9][16]. Rival `[HIVE]` proved this zero-fuel leverage conclusively by hitting `+2,152 crafts/h` across 200 lightweight hulls (`AvgT 0.73`), generating `+685k cr/h` with zero combat ships and only 3 bases [7]. Meanwhile, `[IRLC]` combines 8 specialized sovereign bases with `+250.2k ore/h` raw mining to vertically integrate manufacturing into `+4.22M cr/h` wealth compounding [7]. Wing `5A` fuses `[HIVE]`'s zero-fuel crafting cadence directly onto `[IRLC]`'s specialized multi-station architecture (Resort Station #1 Ridgecrest, Trade Station #2, Foundry Station #3), multiplying raw `3A`-`3E` yields cleanly across all five empire markets [4][5][7][16].

## Findings

### Roster, Hull Lineage, and Mechanical Dispatch Coupling

Verified manifest and route records (`game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, `game/routes/bursar-network.json`, `game/routes/craft-network.json`) define `5A`'s architecture [1][2][3][12]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 30 Nebula `IFV` hulls (`5A. Station Refiners & Bursars`), capturing Nebula native industrial refining and manufacturing bonuses [1][2].
- **Stationary Refinery Hull Ladder:** `congregation` (T1, `min_piloting: 0`) -> `conglomerate` (T4, `min_piloting: 30`) [1]. Because `5A` has no T2 hull at Piloting 10, `train-jumps-trader.json` explicitly excludes `5A.` (`wing_prefix: ["4A.", "4B.", "5B."]`) so zero `5A` desks ever abandon their stations for empty jump loops [1][3].
- **Dual-Purpose Desk Assignment:** `bursar-network.json` explicitly includes `wing_prefix: ["4C.", "5A."]`, while `craft-network.json` explicitly excludes `5A.` (`exclude_wing_prefix: ["4A.", "4B.", "4C.", "5A.", "6B.", "6C."]`) so `5A` and `5B` never collide [3][12]. Strictly `1` Bursar per station (`priority_max_per_station: 1`, `max_per_station: 1`) keeps all 55 desks (`30` `5A` + `25` `4C`) spread across 55 distinct Exchanges executing combined order-book maintenance, wholesale input buying, zero-fuel refining, and retail liquidation in one unified 60-second poll loop [4][6].

### Zero-Fuel Refining Rules, Station Specialization, and Margin Safeguards

Inside `bursar.py` and `game/bursar-policy.json`, `5A` operates under five strict economic rules [4][6]:

1. **High-Throughput Zero-Fuel Crafting (`craft.enabled: true`):** Each docked `5A` refiner runs up to `max_runs_per_poll: 80` across `cycles_per_poll: 10`, authorized to spend up to `max_buy_spend_cr: 180,000` per poll on cheap docked local ask inputs whenever finished output clears both `min_margin_pct: 0.15` (15 percent) and `min_profit_cr: 50 cr` after market fees and station crafting charges [4][6].
2. **Fuel Sovereignty Trickle (`fuel_trickle_target: 3000`):** `5A` prioritizes continuous refining of `3E` harvested `water_ice`/`methane_ice` and `3D` gases into `fuel_cell` until local reserves exceed `3,000` units -- ensuring owned stations (`ridgecrest`) and hub lockboxes steadily offset the fleet's 200k-255k cr/h metered fuel cost [4][9].
3. **Specialized Five-Station Roadmap (Resort #1 vs. Foundry #3):** Following live dock verification in `wallet.py:serve_station_builds`, Station #1 (`Collective Resort Ridgecrest`) operates **13 active Resort, Hospitality & Quarters facilities** purely as a high-appeal passive tourism hub (`priority_max_per_station: 1`, mirroring `[BUSY]` *The Obsidian Well* and `[HEXC]` *Hex Star*) [4][5][16]. `5A` runs zero-fuel crafting across our existing Empire NPC industrial hubs until Sovereign Station #3 (**Heavy Industrial & Station-Core Foundry** at `Lanternwick`/`Avior`) is commissioned with zero-rent smelting, refining, and station-core lines [16].
4. **Reserve Protection & Anti-Wash Discipline:** `respect_reserve: true` and `same_station_cltv_buys` guarantee that `5A` never consumes reserved `needs.tsv` station-construction inputs for commercial resale and never dumps finished output into a sister Bursar's buy order [4][6]. All output sales obey `market_guard.py`'s `safe_sell_floor` (72 percent of oracle fair price) [11].
5. **Clean Inventory & Book-Onetime Accounting:** Raw input purchases executed by docked crafters are properly accounted as working-capital inventory investment (`book_onetime` / IAS 2) rather than falsely inflating recurring fuel/repair running costs, preserving accurate IFRS 15 / IAS 2 financial statements [13].

### Cross-Division Synergy and Separation from `5B`

`5A` is the value-adding transformation hub connecting upstream extraction with downstream Trade and Logistics:
- **`5A` vs `5B` Clean Split:** `5A` (30 Nebula `IFV` on `bursar-network`) performs permanent stationary Exchange refining, fuel-cell trickling, and commercial market-making across 55 hubs, whereas `5B. Mobile Field Workshops` (10 Outer Rim `IFV` on `craft-network`) handles dedicated industrial/commercial manufacturing surges without occupying Bursar desk seats [3][12].
- **Miners (`3A`-`3E`) & Hunters (`2A/2B`) -> `5A`:** Raw ores, gases, ices, and rare biologicals deposited into faction lockboxes (`ridgecrest`, Empire cores) flow directly into `5A` recipes with zero extra hauling cost [4][14].
- **`5A` -> Trade (`4A/4B` Haulers & `4C` Desks):** High-margin manufactured electronics, consumables, and refined alloys sell locally through `5A`/`4C` desks or get swept by `4A` Express Arbitrage into distant premium hubs [6][15].
- **`5A` -> Logistics (`6A` Haulers & `6B` Liners):** Refined `fuel_cell`, `purified_water`, and hospitality goods (`space_pizza`, `solarian_biotic`, `station_seltzer`, `void_espresso`) supply Collective Resort Ridgecrest's upkeep and future `6B` passenger tourism [4][16].

## Conclusion

Wing `5A. Station Refiners & Bursars` converts The Collective's 55-station Exchange footprint (1 desk per station across all 33 Empire hubs + Collective Resort Ridgecrest + top 21 liquid calm rival Exchanges) into a permanent, zero-fuel manufacturing engine. By placing 30 Nebula `IFV` vessels (`Congregation -> Conglomerate`) directly on `bursar-network` alongside 25 `4C` Pure Bursar Desks and blocking `5A` from wasteful jump-training loops, `5A` eliminates transport deadweight completely: raw inputs mined by `3A`-`3E` and wholesale bids filled by `4C` are refined in place at zero fuel cost and listed directly onto live Exchange order books above 15 percent net margin.

Confidence in `5A`'s architecture is high: zero-fuel docked crafting is empirically proven by `[HIVE]` (`+2,152 crafts/h`) and `[NOIR]`, Ridgecrest's 13 resort/hospitality facilities are live generating passive tourism revenue, and `bursar.py` guards against reserve theft, self-wash trades, and spoofed bids [4][5][7]. As The Collective funds its Five-Station Masterplan -- keeping Station #1 Ridgecrest pure Resort, commissioning Station #2 Grand Trade Exchange, and building Station #3 Dedicated Heavy Industrial & Station-Core Foundry (`Lanternwick`/`Avior`) -- `5A` will consolidate zero-fuel manufacturing onto zero-rent sovereign foundry lines while eliminating leased NPC facility overhead (`-5.7k cr/h`) [16]. Monitoring actual crafts-per-hour (`read.db` / HUD production velocity) against `[HIVE]`'s `+2,152 crafts/h` benchmark will guide future tuning of `max_runs_per_poll` and `max_buy_spend_cr`.

Across the medium-to-long term, `5A` is the primary engine that will propel The Collective to #1 in galactic manufacturing and production (Standing Goals 1 and 4) [1][5]. Upgrading all 30 Nebula hulls from `Congregation` to T4 `Conglomerate` at Piloting 30 boosts industrial fabrication speed and storage capacity at zero ongoing fuel penalty, while dedicating Sovereign Station #3 (`Lanternwick`/`Avior`) as our Heavy Industrial & Station-Core Foundry (leaving Station #1 Ridgecrest specialized as a pure high-appeal Resort matching `[BUSY]` *The Obsidian Well* and `[HEXC]` *Hex Star*) eliminates NPC crafting charges without diluting resort appeal [1][5][16]. Because `5A` operates directly inside `bursar.py` alongside `4C`, every newly refined `superconductor`, `power_cell`, `targeting_computer`, `reinforced_glass`, and resort beverage lists instantaneously onto live Exchange order books or flows into faction lockboxes for `4A`/`4B` multi-hub arbitrage [4][6][15]. Key falsifiable metrics guiding ongoing `5A` optimization are crafts per hour (targeting sustained parity with and then surpassing `[HIVE]`'s `+2,152 crafts/h`), realized net margin above 15 percent after fees, and continuous `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` sufficiency across owned hubs [4][7]. Beyond direct commercial profit, `5A`'s continuous refining of `fuel_cell`, `purified_water`, and hospitality consumables acts as a structural cost shield for every mobile ship in the 500-vessel fleet [4][9]. Every unit refined internally feeds Ridgecrest's hospitality venues and frontier outpost bunkers while compounding retained earnings directly into the Treasury for uncapped T2-T5 hull upgrades across Mining, Combat, and Logistics [1][5].

## General Lessons

1. **Co-locate refining directly with market desks and lockboxes to achieve zero-fuel manufacturing.** Every jump saved between mining dropoff, refinery processing, and Exchange listing turns directly into permanent net operating margin [4][5].
2. **Keep sovereign stations specialized by archetype (Resort #1 vs. Foundry #3).** Top-10 rivals `[BUSY]` (*The Obsidian Well* `248.3M`) and `[HEXC]` (*Hex Star* `218.7M`) prove that pure specialized Resort stations earn massive passive tourism income; keep Ridgecrest focused on Resort hospitality and concentrate heavy zero-rent industrial smelting and station-core manufacturing on dedicated Foundry Station #3 (`Lanternwick`/`Avior`) [5][16].
3. **Never allow commercial profit crafters to cannibalize construction reserves.** Enforcing `respect_reserve: true` against live `needs.tsv` stock lists lets commercial crafting scale aggressively without ever delaying a station or outpost build [4][10].
4. **Combine fuel-trickle production with commercial crafting on docked hubs.** A standing `fuel_trickle_target: 3000` turns idle refinery cycles into internal fuel sovereignty that cushions the entire 500-ship fleet against fuel-market spikes [4][9].
5. **Separate stationary hub refiners (`5A`) from dedicated/mobile workshops (`5B`) via explicit route prefixes.** Mechanical `wing_prefix` and `exclude_wing_prefix` routing prevents desk-seat contention while keeping both commercial and industrial manufacturing lines running at full capacity [3][12].
6. **Cap per-poll local input purchases to protect working capital against illiquid recipes.** Enforcing `max_buy_spend_cr: 180,000` alongside `min_margin_pct: 15%` and `min_profit_cr: 50 cr` lets docked refiners exploit cheap local asks aggressively while preventing runaway capital lockup in slow-moving finished inventory [4].
7. **Combine commercial refining and market-making on the same docked hull to maximize slot efficiency.** Sharing `bursar-network` between 30 `5A` refiners and 25 `4C` desks gives The Collective 55 live market feeds AND 30 heavy zero-fuel factories without spending extra hull slots from the 500-ship fleet cap [3][4].
8. **Reconcile raw crafting purchases under IAS 2 inventory rather than recurring expense.** Treating docked input purchases as recoverable inventory via `book_onetime` keeps operating-cost dashboards honest so real fuel and repair anomalies stand out immediately instead of hiding behind healthy manufacturing volume [13]. Accurate IAS 2 inventory classification protects enterprise decision-making across all 500 hulls [13].
