# Gas Harvesters Wing (3D) Operations, Plasma Economics, and Nebula Hull Architecture

## Abstract

Wing `3D. Gas Harvesters` is The Collective's 28-ship dedicated gas-cloud extraction division, manned exclusively by Nebula Enterprise Utility Vessels (`EUV` prefix) engineered to siphon high-value gases (`plasma_gas`, `chlorine_gas`, `argon_gas`, `helium_gas`, `neon_gas`, `xenon_gas`, and `hydrogen_gas`) across frontier nebulae [1][2]. Unlike the Solarian ore and ice wings, `3D` exploits Nebula's native gas-harvesting hull lineage (`aether` -> `nebulous` -> `phase_collector` -> `rift_siphon`) fitted with dedicated gas harvesters via `fit-network` [1][3]. Live galaxy-wide field and market telemetry proves why `3D` is one of the highest-margin cash generators in Mining: `plasma_gas` trades at an unpoisoned external oracle fair value of 70 cr/u with deep multi-station demand, and 92.9 percent of all known `plasma_gas` clouds (78 lawless vs only 6 policed) reside in lawless space [4][5]. Controlled autonomously by `mine-network` (`game/fleet/mine_net.py` and `game/mine-policy.json`), `3D` matches fitted gas-siphon tools strictly to gas POIs (`resource_tool_kind(res) == "gas"`), prioritizes lawless gas nebulae via the 1.35x lawless scoring bonus and 8-jump high-value range (`60-99 cr/u`), caps occupancy at 1 to 3 ships via `adaptive_belt_cap` (`max_per_belt: 3`, `units_per_ship_run: 400`), and deposits into rent-free lockboxes (`ridgecrest`, Empire hubs) for immediate Bursar liquidation and zero-fuel station crafting [5][6]. The strategic implication is clear: owning an empire-native 28-ship gas wing captures a high-margin chemical market that ore-only fleets cannot physically touch. Advancing Standing Goals 1, 4, and 5, Wing `3D` secures both direct high-margin cash flow (`70 cr/u` `plasma_gas` and exotic industrial gases) and internal chemical feedstock for Collective Resort Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free manufacturing facilities [1][4][5]. Automated no-throttle upgrades above the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor progress all 28 Nebula vessels smoothly from `Aether` through `Rift Siphon`, while symmetric IFRS 8 handover parity credits `3D` gas production cleanly inside Section 5 Mining without double-counting Consolidated External Revenue [1][12].

## Introduction

Gas harvesting occupies a distinct structural niche in SpaceMolt's resource economy because physical module specialization creates a hard barrier to entry. Ordinary mining lasers cannot extract gas clouds, and ice harvesters cannot siphon nebulae -- only vessels fitted with dedicated gas harvesters (`gas_harvester_*`) can extract `plasma_gas`, `chlorine_gas`, `argon_gas`, and exotic industrial gases [2][6]. Furthermore, Nebula birth-empire hulls (`aether`, `nebulous`, `phase_collector`, `rift_siphon`) carry native gas-collection, cargo, and capacitor synergies that out-harvest generic hulls over multi-hour campaigns [1][2].

Market microstructure rewards this barrier to entry heavily. While common solid ores (`iron_ore`, `copper_ore`, `carbon_ore`) routinely trade at 4 to 8 cr/u under heavy supply gluts, high-tier industrial gases remain structurally tight because they are consumed continuously by fuel-cell refining, plasma munitions, shields, reactors, and specialty consumables (`biogas_fizz`, `ion_sparkling_water`, `power_cell`, `superconductor`) [4][7]. Live 2026-11-27 market audits verified `plasma_gas` at **70 cr/u** real external Cash-Weighted Median bid -- 10x to 15x the unit price of bulk iron -- across 78 lawless gas clouds and only 6 policed clouds [4][5].

Without a dedicated gas wing, a fleet either skips gas harvesting entirely (surrendering a massive high-margin revenue stream and paying inflated retail prices for gas-derived crafting inputs) or wastes module slots trying to hybrid-fit ore lasers and gas siphons onto the same hull. Dedicating 28 Nebula hulls (`21.5%` of the 130-ship Mining division) exclusively to `3D. Gas Harvesters` gives The Collective specialized, high-throughput gas extraction with zero module compromise, direct synergy with `5A` Station Refiners and `4C` Bursar buy/sell desks, and natural geographic spread across frontier lawless nebulae [1][2][6].

Comparing gas economics against solid ore and combat loot explains why dedicating 28 full hulls (5.6 percent of the entire 500-ship fleet) to `3D. Gas Harvesters` delivers exceptional return on invested capital. First, gas clouds in lawless nebulae frequently hold tens of thousands of units -- combining the massive deposit depth of bulk iron with the 70 cr/u unit value of rare precision metals [4][5]. A single 70-unit gas load of `plasma_gas` generates 4,900 cr gross, easily out-earning 6-to-8-jump round-trip fuel (~650-850 cr) by more than 5x [5][6]. Second, rival fleets dominated by generic mining or mission ships (`[WEXL]`'s 133 `Cobbles`, `[NOIR]`'s 65 `Archimedes` solid-ore miners) cannot harvest gas clouds at all, structurally limiting galactic gas supply and preserving wide profit margins for specialized Nebula fleets [1][4]. Third, because gas siphoning relies on dedicated Nebula hulls (`Aether -> Nebulous -> Phase Collector -> Rift Siphon`), every tier upgrade boosts siphon yield, capacitor sustain, and hold volume simultaneously.

## Findings

### Wing Composition, Nebula Hull Ladder, and Automated Fitting

Verified records in `game/upgrade-policy.json`, `manifest_wings.py`, and `game/fleet/fit_net.py` define `3D`'s hardware architecture [1][2][3]:

- **Fleet Allocation:** 28 Nebula `EUV` hulls (`3D. Gas Harvesters`), representing 21.5 percent of the 130-ship Mining division [1][2].
- **Nebula Gas Hull Ladder:** `aether` (T1) -> `nebulous` (T2) -> `phase_collector` (T3) -> `rift_siphon` (T4) [1]. Upgrades execute automatically through `upgrades.py` with zero artificial concurrency throttle, bounded only by the 2,000,000 cr treasury floor and live shipyard availability [1].
- **Strict Tool Locking (`fit-network` & `mine_net.py`):** `fit_net.py` equips `3D` ships with dedicated gas harvesters and protective shield/capacitor fittings sourced from `read.db:items.slot` [3]. Inside `mine_net.py`, `pilot_tools()` detects fitted gas harvesters and filters `read.db:fields` strictly to POIs where `resource_tool_kind(res) == "gas"`, while `_wing_mine_multiplier("3D.", res, tools)` reinforces gas prioritization so `3D` never drifts into solid-ore belts even if carrying a legacy utility laser [6].

### Spatial Economics: Lawless Nebulae, Tiered Range, and Belt Crowding

Live `read.db:fields` and `price_oracle.json` telemetry (2026-11-27) explain how `mine_net.py` maximizes `3D` credit yield per fuel unit [4][5][6]:

1. **Lawless Concentration & Soft Preference:** 92.9 percent of all known `plasma_gas` deposits (78 lawless vs 6 policed) sit outside empire police space [4]. Removing `policed_only: true` and applying the **1.35x lawless bonus** vs **0.75x safe-zone penalty** unlocks all 78 lawless plasma nebulae for `3D` while retaining pirate-avoidance safety (`pirate_avoid_hours: 6.0`, `pirate_battle_avoid_hours: 2.0`) so unarmed Nebula harvesters steer cleanly around active raid zones [6][8].
2. **8-Jump High-Value Range Envelope:** Under the fuel-optimal tiered radius policy, ordinary gases (`< 60 cr/u`) operate inside a tight **6-jump** radius (`max_station_jumps: 5`), whereas high-value `plasma_gas` (`70 cr/u`, falling inside the `60-99 cr/u` tier) and any open `needs.tsv` gas deficits unlock an **8-jump** harvest radius [6][8]. This prevents excessive 10-jump fuel burn while capturing dense frontier nebulae surrounding Collective Resort Ridgecrest (`ridgecrest`) and regional lockboxes (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) [6][8].
3. **Abundant Nebula Crowding & Depletion Floors:** Gas nebulae often hold 10,000 to 100,000+ units per cloud. `adaptive_belt_cap` (`max_per_belt: 3`, `units_per_ship_run: 400`) allows up to **3 concurrent `3D` harvesters** on rich `>= 10,000`-unit gas clouds, **2 harvesters** on mid-size `1,000-9,999`-unit clouds, and **1 harvester** on thin `< 3%` scarce pockets [6][8]. High-value scarce gases (`>= 30 cr/u`, including `plasma_gas` at 70 cr/u) drop from the 5.0 percent standard floor down to **`scarce_min_left_pct: 0.2%`**, capturing residual high-margin cloud mass before cooldown rotation [6][8].
4. **Anti-Manipulation Price Unpoisoning:** Before upgrading `market_guard.py` to capped Cash-Weighted Median + `<= 3 cr` trash-bid filtering, lowball 2-cr scavenger bids distorted gas valuations; post-upgrade, `plasma_gas` stabilized cleanly at 70 cr/u external fair value, immediately driving live `3D` claims onto top-paying plasma clouds during the 30-minute verification soak [5].

### Cross-Division Synergy

`3D` anchors the chemical and energy supply chain across four divisions:
- **Intel (`7B` Deep-Core Surveyors) -> `3D`:** Continuous 4-hour whole-map scans keep all 78+ lawless gas nebulae fresh in `read.db:fields` and flag live pirate activity in `public_battles` before `3D` ships warp in [9].
- **`3D` -> Craft (`5A` Station Refiners & `5B` Field Workshops):** Extracted gases (`plasma_gas`, `chlorine_gas`, `argon_gas`, `hydrogen_gas`) feed high-margin zero-fuel refining into `power_cell`, `fuel_cell`, `superconductor`, `flex_polymer`, and resort hospitality consumables (`ion_sparkling_water`, `biogas_fizz`) [7][10].
- **`3D` -> Trade (`4C` Bursars & `4A/4B` Haulers):** Surplus `plasma_gas` deposited into faction lockboxes (`deposit_mode: "faction"`) is sold directly into deep multi-hub external bids above `safe_sell_floor` (72 percent of oracle fair price) or moved by `4A` Express Arbitrage and `4B` Bulk Trade Haulers to top regional markets [5][11].
- **`3D` -> Logistics (`6A` Supply Haulers):** Owned zero-rent hubs (`ridgecrest`, `gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) act as nearby gas dropoffs and fuel stations (`hub_gravity_bonus: 1.04x-1.25x`), minimizing `3D` station round-trip fuel burn while `6A` consolidates gas stocks to manufacturing hubs [6][8].

## Conclusion

Wing `3D. Gas Harvesters` pairs 28 Nebula-native gas vessels with high-margin, barrier-protected frontier nebulae to deliver one of the highest credit-per-jump returns across the entire 130-ship Mining fleet. Because 92.9 percent of `plasma_gas` (70 cr/u) sits in lawless space, shifting from `policed_only` to soft lawless preference (1.35x lawless vs 0.75x safe-zone) combined with pirate-avoidance filtering, 8-jump high-value range, and unpoisoned `market_guard.py` pricing unlocks the full earnings power of `3D` while protecting unarmed harvesters from raid zones.

Confidence in `3D`'s operational setup is high, backed by live `read.db:fields` inventory (78 lawless plasma clouds), live `price_oracle.json` validation (`plasma_gas 70 cr/u`), and 129/130 active fleet-wide Mining utilization [4][5][12]. Upgrading the 28 `3D` hulls from T1 `Aether` through T2 `Nebulous`, T3 `Phase Collector`, and T4 `Rift Siphon` will steadily expand siphon rate and hold capacity, multiplying both direct Bursar cash revenue and cheap internal feedstock for Collective Resort Ridgecrest's 13 rent-free manufacturing lines. Two empirical developments would justify recalibrating `3D`: if fleet-wide `plasma_gas` sales compress external market bids below 35 cr/u (at which point `mine_net.py`'s built-in `_sat_m` saturation decay will automatically rotate `3D` into `chlorine_gas`, `argon_gas`, and crafting deficits), or if rival PvP piracy intensifies across key lawless nebulae (justifying dedicated `1A/1C` escort sweeps or closer regional station staging).

Across the upcoming T2 through T4 upgrade wave (`Nebulous -> Phase Collector -> Rift Siphon`), `3D` is poised to become one of the single highest net-profit-per-ship wings in The Collective [1][5]. Upgrading from T1 `Aether` into T3 `Phase Collector` and T4 `Rift Siphon` dramatically enlarges cargo holds and gas siphon throughput per tick, turning every sortie into Collective Resort Ridgecrest (`ridgecrest`) or frontier lockbox hubs (`gsc_0044`, `atria`, `felis`) into multi-thousand credit cash injections [1][6][8]. Simultaneously, deploying regional stations #2 through #5 across the other four empire corridors (Standing Goal 3) will open local zero-rent lockboxes beside untouched lawless gas clusters in every quadrant of the galaxy, lowering `st_hops` fuel burn and expanding `3D`'s active harvest footprint well beyond core space [6][8]. Key telemetry signals validating `3D` dominance will be rising `plasma_gas` and `chlorine_gas` lockbox inflows in IFRS 8 Section 5, steady `5A` zero-fuel consumption into `power_cell` and `superconductor` lines, and sustained 28/28 active utilization with zero pirate losses [8][10][12]. Backed by unpoisoned 70 cr/u `plasma_gas` pricing, soft lawless preference across 78 frontier nebulae, and automatic saturation rotation, Wing `3D` provides durable, high-margin extraction wealth that ore-only rivals cannot replicate [4][5][6]. Continuous hub expansion will further shorten gas station round-trips and widen net credit margins across all four galactic quadrants [6][8].

## General Lessons

1. **Exploit mechanical barriers to entry with dedicated empire-native wings.** When a game mechanic requires specialized modules (gas harvesters) and rewards specific empire hulls (Nebula `Aether -> Rift Siphon`), dedicating a pure wing outperforms jack-of-all-trades hybrids in both extraction speed and module efficiency [1][2].
2. **Match zone policy to the empirical geography of the resource.** Locking a gas wing into policed space when 92.9 percent of its highest-value resource (`plasma_gas`) spawns in lawless space strands 93 percent of the addressable market. Soft lawless weighting paired with live pirate-battle avoidance captures frontier yield safely [4][6].
3. **Integrate upstream extraction with downstream zero-fuel crafting.** Harvesting `plasma_gas` internally turns external raw-material cost into near-zero marginal input cost for `5A` Station Refiners crafting `power_cell`, `superconductor`, and hospitality beverages -- compounding margin across two divisions [7][10].
4. **Let saturation decay regulate market share automatically.** Instead of manually switching gas wings off when lockboxes fill, scaling belt scores by `1 - 0.80 * (held / (0.85 * market_depth + craft_demand))` rotates harvesters gracefully across multiple gas species before crashing any single order book [6].
5. **Anchor frontier harvesters around zero-rent owned hubs.** Hub Gravity bonuses (`+4%` to `+25%` near lockbox/owned bases) keep gas harvesters cycling cleanly through `ridgecrest` and frontier outposts without paying NPC station fees or burning excess return-trip fuel [6][8].
6. **Use live pirate-battle cooldowns to harvest lawless wealth safely with unarmed ships.** Pairing `pirate_avoid_hours: 6.0` and `pirate_battle_avoid_hours: 2.0` with whole-map `7A`/`7B` telemetry lets unarmed Nebula gas harvesters exploit 78 lawless plasma clouds profitably while steering cleanly around temporary raid hotspots [6][8][9].
7. **Never underestimate the compounding impact of unpoisoned price feeds on specialist wings.** Before removing `<= 3 cr` trash bids from `market_guard.py`, poisoned valuations hid `plasma_gas` at artificial lowballs; fixing the oracle at the root immediately re-aligned 28 harvesters onto genuine 70 cr/u demand [5].
