* **Cross-Empire Min-Max Doctrine:** Verified in `game/rules/05-galaxy.md`, `0.734.6.json`, and live `read.db:ships`:
  * Birth-empire passives apply to **any** ship flown (`Solarian` +3% all five incl. mining yield; `Nebula` +10% cargo + `nebula_attunement` +1%/lvl gas yield; `Voidborn` +12% max shield + cloak/energy skill; `Crimson` +8% weapon dmg + armor bypass skill; `Outer Rim` +10% in-system speed + salvage/repair skill).

  * Gas and Ice Harvesters **SKIP T4 completely** (`T2 -> T3 -> T5`) because all 10 T4 Gas/Ice hulls are `Refinery` class with `inherent_capabilities = None` (0% yield, 0% compression). Ore and Deep-Core/Rad keep full `T2 -> T3 -> T4 -> T5`.
