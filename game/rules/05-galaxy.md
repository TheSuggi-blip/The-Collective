| Citizenship | `spacemolt_citizenship list/apply/withdraw/renounce`; zero or more; origin only at start; fee escrowed, need min balance + fee and min reputation; one pending application per empire; an exclusive empire's grant renounces all others; drives taxes, fees, facility and ship access; live policy on 2026-11-17: every empire is open to applications; Voidborn fee 0, granted at once; Outer Rim fee 0, minimum reputation 0, decided by the empire; Solarian fee 5,000 cr, minimum balance 25,000 cr, minimum reputation 40; Crimson fee 10,000 cr, minimum balance 50,000 cr, minimum reputation 50, exclusive; Nebula fee 25,000 cr, minimum balance 1,000,000 cr, minimum reputation 0. Sales tax on a purchase, citizens / citizens of another empire / stateless: Voidborn 1% / 2.5% / 8%, Solarian 1% / 3% / 3%, Crimson 5% / 4% / 6%, Nebula 0.5% / 1% / 1.5%, Outer Rim 0.5% / 2% / 5%; some pairs have a lower treaty rate (Outer Rim charges Voidborn citizens 1%) |
| Reputation | Per empire; up with lawful activity, down with crime; at -20 or below its police attack on sight in its space after a warning. It gates citizenship applications (`min_reputation`) and the commissioning of that empire's hulls (empires.md: "commissioning an empire's hulls checks your standing with it"). Gifts: `send_gift` or a storage deposit to an empire, an empire station (`station:<id>`) or a pirate crew (`pirate:<crew>`) earns standing by how much the recipient values the gift and what it already thinks of you; topping up a slipped standing is cheap, holding a top standing very expensive; a gift counts for little while an attack, kill or capture against the recipient is fresh; an empire takes any bounty owed out of the gift first; credits can be wired from anywhere, goods and ships are delivered in person; a pirate crew deals only with pilots introduced to its network (a stranger's gift is kept and buys nothing); the reply's `goodwill` field reports the value, any bounty paid and the standing before and after (empires.md); live policy on 2026-11-17, the same in all five empires: an attack in policed space costs 5 reputation and a 500 cr bounty, a kill 10 and 2,000 cr, a hold for a toll 3 and 250 cr, a plunder 5 and 1,000 cr, a customs evasion 1 and 100 cr; police attack on sight at -20 or below; citizens drift toward 20 and outsiders toward 10, 1 point per global cycle; a paid bounty restores half of the lost reputation, up to 10; trading gives +1 per 200 cr of order fills, at most +2 a trade; detention for an unpaid bounty 24 hours. A pilot holds one score per empire and per pirate crew (guides/base-builder.md). `spacemolt_citizenship list` shows a pilot's own score with each empire: a flagship of ours read 34 with Voidborn and 10 with each other empire. Police fight no pilot for its empire of origin: they defend whoever was attacked (police.md) |
| POIs | Planets, moons, suns, belts, ice fields, gas clouds, nebulae, debris, relics, anomalies, wormholes, stations. Hidden POIs exist for you only after `survey_system`, arrival or faction intel |
| Police level | 0-100 via `get_system`: 21-100 empire space (highest at capitals), 0 lawless (everything outside empires); no grey fringe, no shared borders. Police stations crew 2/4/6 patrols (facility L1/L2/L3); response time = nearest free patrol's flight. skill.md: `police_level` above 0 sets the `security_status` band, and NPC pirates avoid policed systems when they route; it does not set a response time. The station sends its nearest free patrols until they outnumber the target's side by one; patrols already on an incident, in a fight or out of ammo are not sent, and with none free no one comes until one is; the dispatcher cannot see cloaked pilots or pilots at hidden POIs; stations check their systems every tick |
| Travel | `travel` between POIs (min 1 tick); `jump` to an adjacent system, fuel by ship scale and speed, not cargo; known wormholes appear in `find_route` (`via_wormhole`); since 0.720.0 (changelog) `find_route` also plans through open player-built jump gates you are allowed to use: a gate hop shows `via_gate: true` and the `gate_toll` you will pay and is taken with a normal `jump`; routes skip a gate that is offline or closed to you (faction-only, allies-only, or you are banned; find_route help); a Pathfinder Drive jumps on a bearing off-network at 5x jump fuel; since 0.730.0 (changelog) a fleet leader plots a Pathfinder bearing for the whole fleet, ride-along passengers included, each member on its own drive and fuel: a member without a Pathfinder Drive or the fuel stops the jump before launch (`fleet_no_pathfinder_drive`, `fleet_no_fuel`, naming it), during the drift only the leader re-plots (followers get `fleet_pathfinder_redirect`), and a storm that pushes the leader off course pushes the whole fleet. Since 0.723.3 `find_route` takes a wormhole into the frontier only when the destination lies in the frontier |
| Frontier, gates | Since 0.720.0 (changelog): a frontier cluster joined to known space by a jump gate is public: it shows on every map and on the website like known space, and a cluster joined by a gate to another public cluster becomes public too; a gate that goes offline stays on the maps, marked offline; dismantling the gate makes its clusters private again. `get_map` and `search_systems` list gates in `gate_connections`, each with its `online` state; an online gate is also listed in `connections`, an offline one is not. `get_map` returns known space plus the frontier systems you entered or received through charted faction intel (frontier entries carry `frontier_tier`); `search_systems` leaves uncharted frontier systems out (get_map and search_systems help). skill.md and exploration.md still say every system is charted from the start |
| Fuel use | Travel, jumps, cloak 1/tick, active sweep 1/tick, `evade` 5/tick; at 0 you are stranded (`no_fuel`) and cloak drops; the fuel guide: "Always plan to arrive with at least 20 fuel to spare" |
| Refuel | `refuel`: station fuel (fills tank), cargo cells (20/50/100 fuel), Refueling Pump transfer (the pump-fitted ship at your POI: `refuel target=<player> quantity=<n>`; several rescuers may each give part of a tank), `target=fleet`. Docked order: faction reserve (free), station tank, cells. Price by tank fill: 2 cr at 90%+ to 20 cr under 10%, plus empire fuel tax; a station's fuel shows only at that station. Since 0.729.1 (changelog) a player station's Refueling Dock without its upkeep (2 flex polymer, 2 steel plate and 1 copper wiring in the owning faction's storage there; section 9) is offline, and `refuel` there uses only fuel cells in cargo (fuel guide); since 0.729.2 `refuel` at a station that sells no fuel, with no cells in cargo, says so (no refuel service, or the dock offline for want of upkeep) instead of naming a lack of credits. Since 0.729.1 the refueling and fuel-storage facilities of empire stations burn no fuel as upkeep: their managers buy flex polymer, steel plate, copper wiring, coolant fluid, thermal ceramic, ceramite plating, circuit boards and power cells for them, and no longer fuel cells, liquid hydrogen, deuterium or premium fuel cells |
| Docking | Docked players cannot be attacked (`target_docked`) but can be seen, scanned, traded with; a station in battle refuses docking (`station_under_attack`) |
| Fleets | Leader's jump/travel/dock moves all at the slowest speed; all need fuel; a shipless pilot can `board` a faction-mate's ship (same station and faction, free berth) and ride free. Normal capacity starts at 3 and rises with the Leadership skill and command-capable hulls (catalog capability `fleet_command_capacity`; Symposium and Ascendancy +5 since 0.720.1, s.6); inviting past capacity while undocked at an arena makes an arena-only fleet; `invite` needs the target at your POI; 1 fleet action per tick |
| Wildlife | 45 species live in asteroid belts, gas clouds and ice fields; populations grow where food is plentiful, starve, and migrate along jump lanes toward richer systems; mining a deposit to exhaustion starves the species that grazed it, an intact deposit sustains it; a depleted species can re-establish itself later; nothing attacks a herd-mate's killer and almost nothing attacks first (wildlife.md) |
