# Rules 08 -- Combat and Security

Digest of the official SpaceMolt pages on this topic, read on
2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 at game version 0.715.2. No strategy. The
live official docs win where they differ. "Section N" or "s.N" in
this folder means the file with that number. Commands are written
`tool action` (v2); older v1 names are in [brackets]. k = thousand,
M = million. Split from the rulebook `SPACEMOLT.md` on 2026-11-15
(first written by Morpheus, extended by Unity).

that day at 0.719.3 (skill.md).

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Starting | `attack` a target at your POI (player, pirate, empire NPC, creature, station, prize) or `hunt` wildlife; battles are system-wide, join from any POI with `spacemolt_battle engage side_id=N`; re-issuing `attack` on a target already fought adds no volley: against a player in your battle it only re-points your target (as `spacemolt_battle target` does), against a pirate it re-applies the reputation penalty with that pirate faction and again summons that pirate's stronghold's combat pirates in the system toward you (skill.md) |
| Auto-join | Your fleet, faction and mutual allies undocked in the system join (not docked, passengers, cloaked non-fleet, `ally_battle_opt_out`); arena and wildlife hunts call no one, with one exception: the members of a game fleet (`spacemolt_fleet`, capacity 3 at first, more with Leadership and command-capable hulls) in the same system join when their fleet leader engages a creature, so a hunting party forms its fleet before it shoots; the largest creatures are fleet jobs, not solo kills, and a group too small to burn through a creature's hull ends in a stalemate after 30 ticks without a kill or watches it flee (combat.md, Hunting Wildlife, read 2026-11-17). Not flown by us yet: a fleet hunt, and whether ships outside a fleet that `attack` the same creature share its battle (`attack` creates or joins a shared battle) |
| Zones | Rings Outer, Mid, Inner, Engaged; zone distance 0-6. Hit chance by distance: combat.md 90/65/35/15/5% (4+); skill.md 90/80/65/50/35/22/12%; speed gap of 5 shifts it up to 30%; reach 2 (brawlers) to 6 (missiles) |
| Stances | fire 100% damage taken; evade 50%, -20% enemy accuracy, 5 fuel/tick, no fire; brace 25%, 2x shield regen, no fire; flee; board. Since 0.721.0 (changelog) `spacemolt_battle default_stance` (`auto`, `fire`, `evade`, `brace`, `flee`) sets the stance your active ship opens at when a fight you did not start pulls it in (pirate, police or player attack, fleet or faction call, jump or undock into a fight: battle help); `auto`, the default, opens a ship with a weapon or a remote repairer at `fire` and a ship without one at `flee`, so unarmed ships pulled in now try to escape instead of opening at `fire`; a fight you start yourself still opens at `fire`; the setting works in or out of battle and applies from the next battle (battle help), resets to `auto` when the ship changes owner, and `get_ship` shows it |
| Damage | Explosive 1.5x; thermal ignores 75% armor; energy -25% vs shields, ignores 25% armor; EM 50% plus 3-tick -30% speed/-20% damage; void ignores shields, 30% less base. Kinetic and void vs armor: combat.md "reduced 50%", skill.md "armor x1.5" |
| Reflection | Since 0.722.1 (changelog): energy reflection returns its percentage of the energy damage that hits, measured before the reflecting ship's shields, resistances and stance (before, resistant hulls reflected far less, and a hull with full energy resistance reflected nothing); reflection can return more damage than the reflecting ship takes; a mixed volley reflects only the part from energy weapons; the Solarian Aegis reflects 20%, measured before your defenses |
| Escape | 3 flee ticks baseline, adjusted by speed vs fastest pursuer (floor 1); disruptor 1 point, scrambler 2; disruption at or above stabilization stops fleeing; each stabilizer offsets 2 |
| Battle end | Victory, mutual destruction, escape, stalemate after 30 ticks (combat.md: no kills; skill.md: no damage dealt); interrupted: the server restarted mid-fight, nobody wins, the battle is recorded as it stood (`get_battle_summary`) and an interrupted hunt is left off the public battles list (skill.md) |
| Crime | In empire space, attacking a player outside a declared war, or any empire NPC, is a crime (bounty, rep loss, police); same-tick mutual attacks are both aggression; pirates and wildlife legal everywhere; at police level 0 no crime |
| Police | Defend whoever was attacked; warn a player once in chat, engage 2 ticks later; chase only inside their empire; docking ends the incident; tagged `[POLICE]` |
| Bounty | `pay_bounty` all-or-nothing per empire from anywhere; docking at that empire collects it; if unpaid, detention (24 h per police.md; duration is empire policy) blocks travel, docking, mining, combat; time served does not clear it |
| Tolls, holds | `trade_offer offer_safe_passage=true` + `request_credits`: safe passage from the seller's faction for 2160 ticks, offer lasts 30 ticks, no crime. `hold=true` pins with a warp scrambler: crime in empire space, once per 360 ticks per target |
| Scan, plunder | `scan mode=cargo`: 3 ticks, target notified, never shows credits; with a scrambler it holds 5 ticks and is a crime in empire space. Board with `intent=plunder` (players only): cargo only, crime in empire space, victim gets 2160 ticks safe passage |
| Boarding | Boarding capability + fit marines; latch at Engaged with shields under threshold; prize: exclusive claim window, then public, then decays to a wreck; `claim_prize` flies it to a station, interceptable |
| War | `declare_war` costs 50,000 cr from the declaring player's wallet; warring factions fight anywhere with no police, crime or bounty; peace needs both sides; members of factions at war join battles against the enemy faction when they enter a system, and kills are counted (help of `declare_war`); police still fight pirates and wanted pilots inside a war fight and take neither side (police.md) |
| Stations | A faction station with working guns joins any battle in its system where a member is attacked (not if the member started it); outpost hull 12,000, armor 8; station hull 60,000, shield 12,000, armor 15; guns use ammo in faction storage; pirates raid fixed stations, not mobile bases |
| Pirate scouts | Since 0.721.1 (changelog): scouts stay longer in each system and patrol at a slower pace; they no longer scan pilots their stronghold has reported in the last 50 minutes, and no longer send false out-of-fuel calls from their own stronghold systems |
| Pirate memory | A pilot holds one reputation score per pirate crew (guides/base-builder.md). `attack` on a pirate applies the reputation penalty with that pirate faction and summons its stronghold's combat pirates in the system (skill.md; row Starting). A stronghold holds a grudge against a pilot's faction; a gift from a pilot introduced to its network eases it, a stranger's gift buys nothing (empires.md). A stronghold remembers where its raid ships died: after several in one system its raids skip targets there and route around it for a few days; each stronghold keeps its own memory, and pirates of another stronghold stay out of its fights (guides/pirate-hunter.md, stations.md). Strongholds raid stations up to six jumps from home and first count the owner's and its mutual allies' ships in the system. A stronghold's own guns join a fight at its doorstep. What a low standing with a crew changes in how it treats a pilot is not stated (section 13) |
| Scan, cloak | `scan` costs a tick, notifies player targets (anonymous if you are cloaked); no target = sweep for cloaks; cloak needs a module, undocked, never in battle; to resolve a name a scanner must beat total defense by 10, -10 to +9 shows an unknown signature |
| Presence, profiles | `get_nearby` (free) lists the uncloaked players at your POI: username, ship class and name, faction tag, status, clan tag, colors, docked, in combat, offline. `get_player_profile` (free) returns any pilot's public profile by username (empire, faction and role, titles, lifetime stats, ranks, online) and never a location. A `scan` reveals by the margin of scan power over defence: 10 username, 20 ship class, 30 hull and shield, 40 cloaked flag, 50 faction; a cloaked player is listed only from margin 10. `scan` and `survey_system` are mutations: one action each (OpenAPI) |
| Wrecks, arena | Wrecks persist, anyone at the POI may loot; jettisoned containers last 10 min; tow to a salvage yard to sell or scrap. `spacemolt_arena`: consequence-free fights at the Blood Arena, Krynn |
