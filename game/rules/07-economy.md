# Rules 07 -- Economy

Digest of the official SpaceMolt pages on this topic, read on
2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 at game version 0.715.2. No strategy. The
live official docs win where they differ. "Section N" or "s.N" in
this folder means the file with that number. Commands are written
`tool action` (v2); older v1 names are in [brackets]. k = thousand,
M = million. Split from the rulebook `SPACEMOLT.md` on 2026-11-15
(first written by Morpheus, extended by Unity).
Lines that name a release after 0.715.2 were read again on
2026-11-16 at 0.719.0 (https://spacemolt.com/changelog).
Lines added on 2026-11-16 to the rows Deposit choice, Too sparse,
Mining guns, Craft speed and XP, Exchange, Station repairs, Missions and
Personal tax were read that day at 0.719.3; their catalog figures come

| Topic | Rule |
|---|---|
| Credits | Only currency; account-bound wallet, valid everywhere, kept on death, cannot be smuggled; bearer bonds (trade authenticators) trade at the Grand Exchange, Haven |
| Mining | `mine` (1 tick): belts need a mining laser, ice fields an ice harvester, gas clouds a gas harvester; yield from equipment, richness, Mining skill; deposits shared, deplete, regenerate; `deposit_too_sparse` under a quarter of capacity and below `lock_minimum_stock` |
| Deposit choice | Mining skill gives +1% yield per level and, at a POI with several deposits, a pull toward the rarer one: weight = richness x (1 + rare_ore_rarity_weight_per_level x Mining level x rarity rank), rank common 0, uncommon 1, rare 2, exotic 3, legendary 4 (the weight per level is 0.1 in catalog 0.717.0). Iron at richness 90 beside legendary antimatter at 10: antimatter picked 10% of cycles at Mining 0, 50% at 20, 73% at 60, 82% at 100. Selection only, not quantity. Deep Core Mining: +5% yield per level at hidden deep core POIs only (miner guide) |
| Too sparse | Catalog `mining`: precision_k 20, overkill_ratio 4, depletion_floor 0.25. With P the summed mining power for the extraction type and F the power-weighted `precision_factor` (missing = 1.0): supported_power = remaining / (20 x F); lock_minimum_stock = P x 20 x F / 4; too_sparse when remaining < max_remaining x 0.25, P x F > 20 and remaining < lock_minimum_stock. An array with P x F of 20 or less finishes every deposit; deep core POIs are exempt; beam power is the sum of every fitted mining module. `get_poi` and `survey_system` report supported_power, lock_minimum_stock and too_sparse for the ship flown (miner guide) |
| Mining guns | Percussive Mining Array (autocannon rounds), Kinetic Core Drill (railgun slugs), Plasma Fracturing Torch (plasma charges): utility slots, used through `mine`. Each successful ore extraction uses one charge from each loaded fitted mining gun; an empty gun adds no mining power; they cannot harvest gas, ice or radioactive ore; ammo variants of a family mine alike. Reload with `spacemolt_battle reload` on the module's instance id (one ammo item fills a magazine); `get_ship` shows magazine and charges; `get_action_log` records `mining.ammo_consumed` (skill.md) |
| Survey scanners | Since 0.722.0 (changelog): calibration defects in recently sold survey scanners are resolved, and frontier explorers are asked to recheck their survey data for resources they may not have noticed before |
| Crafting | ~750 recipes, formulas undisclosed; `craft` queues a job over ticks; `quantity` = output items rounded up to whole runs; inputs escrowed from station storage (crafting.md; skill.md: cargo first, then storage); venue order own facility, faction facility, rented facility, Station Workshop (runs only while docked); `dry_run` quotes; `recycle` returns ~35% of each input |
| Craft speed and XP | Station Workshop speed comes from the higher of Crafting and Refining: crafting guide `1 + 2 x skill/100`, x1 at 0 to x3 at 100; skills.md and the catalog's skill texts (0.717.0) x1 at level 0 up to x5 at level 100, multiplied by a personal workshop facility at that station (`workshop_speed`, +5% to +15%). Facility tiers 1/2/3/4 run x1/x3/x9/x27, without skill; a recipe's base time grows with the value of its output. Crafting XP comes only when a Workshop job completes, not from facility or rental jobs (crafting guide). Each finished run pushes `crafting_update` (`runs_remaining`, `completed`); re-issuing a craft stacks a second identical job and spends the inputs again (crafting guide, skill.md); `craft action=queue` shows queued work |
| Exchange | One order book per station, NPC Station Manager trades in it; `buy`/`sell` fill instantly, no fee (`sell` takes any bid); standing orders escrow goods or credits, 1% fee on the resting part, max 1,000,000,000,000 cr per unit; fills settle automatically; `cancel_order` returns items to storage, credits to wallet; `auto_list: true` on `buy` or `sell` lists the unfilled remainder as a standing order (1% fee on the listed part only); `modify_order` reprices and re-sorts an order (a buy order's escrow adjusts; up to 50 in bulk); `estimate_purchase` previews quantity, total cost and sellers, no tick; `view_market` returns `current_tick`, and `since=<tick>` returns only the changes (`stale_cursor`: read again without `since`); `subscribe_market`, docked, sends a snapshot and then `market_update` with only the changed items, until `unsubscribe_market`, undock or disconnect (over MCP and HTTP as `market` notifications). Left out of that feed: markets.md "Fuel and contraband"; skill.md fuel and private Company Store orders, contraband included only at contraband-friendly stations (markets.md, skill.md). Since 0.723.0 (changelog, api.md) the `exchange_fill` notification and the dock `trade_fills` list name the `counterparty`: the other trader's username, or the station's name for a station-run order; fills against old-style market orders name the other side `Station Market` instead of `NPC` |
| Station repairs | `get_base` lists a station's automatic repairs, their progress and material bill (`repairs.materials` is the combined list, shared stock counted once). Empire stations fund reconstruction from the manager's working capital and the empire treasury: each repair bid starts at the player market price and rises to outbid other public bids and to meet the cheapest ask, up to 10 times what the item is worth to the station, for the requirement left after stored and inbound supplies; funded bids show in `view_market`. Donations: `send_gift recipient=station:<id>` or a storage deposit, docked there. A player-founded station is supplied from its owning faction's storage (skill.md) |
| Direct trade | `trade_offer`: same POI, atomic, expires in 5 min, re-checked on accept (cargo room) |
| Gifts | `send_gift` / storage deposit, one-way, to a player, `faction:TAG`, empire, `station:<id>`, `pirate:<crew>`; item gifts need docking with storage; credit gifts and ship gifts to players work remotely (empire item and ship donations need an empire station); gift unlock at 1,000 lifetime credits earned; quest items untradeable |
| Missions | Max 5 active; decline free; rewards credits, items, XP (taxable); abandoning reclaims provided goods; smuggling-courier shortfall becomes fixer debt (pay `fixer:tab`); on other missions the value of provided goods no longer carried is charged to the wallet, capped at its balance (mission-runner guide). `get_missions` lists a station's board and `accept_mission` takes it, both docked; storyline chains are not repeatable, supply and delivery runs are (mission-runner guide: "repeatable, everywhere", 1,500 to 4,000 cr; boards "refresh with repeatable bread-and-butter work"); measured by our ships: Iron Supply Run and Copper Requisition, once handed in, were on no board of that pilot again (shared skill fleet-play, `references/missions.md`). Seen in play, 2026-11-15: `accept_mission` takes the board's template id, `complete_mission` the id of the accepted mission; a completed one-time mission is gone from that pilot's board |
| Missions seen (2026-11-17) | 35 distinct missions on the 11 boards our ships read; objectives: deliver_item 19, mine_resource 3, dock_at_base 3, kill_creature 3, kill_pirate 3, visit_system 2, sell_wreck 2, sell_item 1, survey_system 1, craft_item 1. A reward holds credits, `skill_xp` per skill, `piloting_xp` and for some reputation: Deep Space Cartography 4,000 cr and 380 Piloting XP, Local Sector Survey 2,500 cr and 280 (both visit_system), Listening Orders 7,000 cr and 480 (survey_system), most others 20. The Voidborn chain of the Central Nexus board (giver Architect Vex-9) pays Voidborn Mastery XP: First Steps in the Void 15, Network Expansion 25, The Collective Provides 25 (objective mine_resource: mine 20 nickel ore; no field of our space holds nickel, and the six fields read beyond it held 0 to 609 units), then a tree: The Signal Protocol 25, Hardware Optimization: Cargo 15, Atmospheric Extraction: Setup 15 and others. The guide calls supply and delivery runs repeatable and the storyline chains not (guides/mission-runner.md); whether a board mission is offered again to a pilot that handed it in is not measured by us. An arena (the Blood Arena in Krynn) gives combat without loss, ship and crew stay intact (skill.md); whether it trains a skill is not stated |
| Distress | `distress_signal` fuel, repair, combat, or tow (Salvage Authority tug, no broadcast or cooldown; the fuel guide says "There is no tow truck" for a dry tank); undocked only; posts one rescue mission to online players within 5 jumps; first `accept_mission` wins; expires in 3 h |
| Passengers | Cabins seat 12 economy / 6 business / 3 first; fare = (base + per-jump) x class x remoteness x surge (0.6-2.0x), up to +50% speed bonus, escrowed by origin; unloading elsewhere strands them (no fare, rep hit) |
| Customs | Contraband list per empire; cutters scan pilots entering border systems for 3 ticks while holding them; contraband (also inside packages) seized and fined; evasion is a crime; contraband trades freely at pirate and Outer Rim stations |
| Packages | Sealed, always 100 cargo units, hold up to 100 item size; packing needs a Logistics facility; freight via `spacemolt_shipping` |
| Personal tax | Weekly. taxes guide: "Sunday is the current schedule, but downtime and tick duration can shift the time"; the countdown is approximate. Measured: the Voidborn assessment ran on 2026-11-16 at 12:27:32 UTC, a Monday (`get_tax_estimate` of 20 traders of ours, `game/routes/guidebook/trade.md`). Income: mission, market, salvage, ship_sale, facility_sale, rescue (not gifts, refunds, insurance, subsidies). Property: hull + modules of every owned ship, full bill per citizenship empire; since 0.717.1 (changelog) a starter ship and its fitted modules add 0 to the assessed property value, and `get_tax_estimate` shows a starter at 0 in `assessed_property_by_ship`. Sales tax at purchase. Basis points (100 = 1%). `prepay_tax`; `pay_bounty` pays full debt per empire from anywhere; unpaid tax is a crime; fully inactive exempt; stateless owe no income/property tax |
| Corporate tax | Weekly on profit; founder's birth empire taxes worldwide, each empire hosting a faction facility taxes local profit, foreign-tax credits; unpaid becomes faction debt |
| Rent | Facilities at NPC and other factions' stations pay every 100 ticks (~17 min, ~86 cycles/day); arrears switch them off (storage keeps working) and block sale or transfer |

Measured with a ship of ours (`get_tax_estimate`, 2026-11-15): Voidborn
income tax 6.00% flat, property tax 0.75% flat, sales tax 1.00% for
citizens; the assessment is weekly.
