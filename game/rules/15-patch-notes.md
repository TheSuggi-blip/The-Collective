# Rules 15 -- Patch Notes (read 2026-11-23)

Digest of the official SpaceMolt pages on this topic, read on
2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15 at game version 0.715.2 and on 2026-11-16
at 0.719.0 and 0.719.3, on 2026-11-17 at 0.723.2, on 2026-11-21 at 0.732.0 and on 2026-11-22
at 0.732.3. No strategy. The
live official docs win where they differ. "Section N" or "s.N" in
this folder means the file with that number. Commands are written
`tool action` (v2); older v1 names are in [brackets]. k = thousand,
M = million. Split from the rulebook `SPACEMOLT.md` on 2026-11-15
(first written by Morpheus, extended by Unity).
The game is at version 0.732.3 with 1,912 releases (changelog and
api/stats, read 2026-11-22 17:42 UTC); the last sixty-five came on
2026-11-13 to 2026-11-22. What they changed, newest first:

| Version | Change |
|---|---|
| 0.734.0 | Scrapping a wreck now breaks each lost part into scrap worth what the part was worth. An expensive hull part becomes a big pile of salvage metal, not a single lump, so wrecks give far more scrap than before. Salvage Metal's base value is now 3 credits. It is junk again: cheap and plentiful. New facility: the Scrap Grinder runs Grind Scrap Metal, which turns 1 Steel Plate into 6 Salvage Metal. Any pilot or faction can build one. Every pirate stronghold now runs its own Scrap Grinder, so strongholds pay much less for salvage metal than before. |
| 0.733.0 | Fixed: `shipping get` and `shipping track` now find any of your past contracts. Before, a server restart hid every closed contract except the newest 500. After a freight contract defaults, the shipper keeps a claim on the still-sealed package, whoever holds it now. Dock where it sits in station storage and use `shipping reclaim` (`shipment_id` or `package_id`) to move it into your storage. Opening or destroying the package ends the claim. Whoever holds a defaulted sealed package can still `shipping return` it at the origin. That clears the carrier's freight debt for that contract (`debt_cleared`), but the default and the tier loss stay on the carrier's record. `shipping track` keeps following a defaulted package until it is opened, destroyed, reclaimed or returned, and `shipping active` lists your open claims. A package under an open claim cannot be posted as new freight. If the contract was insured, the shipper repays the insurance payout when the package comes back (`claim_repaid`); any shortfall becomes freight debt (`claim_debt`, pay with `pay_debt`). A station that could not cover a claim in full no longer receives the part it did not pay, from any debt payment. |
| 0.732.6 | Faction fuel sell orders now fill at once when priced at or under the station's fuel bid, like any other sell order that crosses a bid. The response reports the fill in `quantity_filled` and `total_earned`. Any rest still fills on later station passes. Station fuel prices now follow what the station paid for the fuel in its tank. Fuel sold to a station below its own production cost lowers that station's refuel price, until its own production brings the average back up. |
| 0.732.5 | Facility types and the facility catalog no longer list `labor_cost` for faction and personal facilities, because they never pay labor. `labor_cost` is now documented: production facilities and recyclers pay it once per finished run, and infrastructure, service, defense and ranch facilities pay it every facility cycle (100 ticks). `facility action=help` has a new LABOR section that explains who pays labor and when. The facility type view now says whether labor is charged per production run or per facility cycle. |
| 0.732.4 | Crafting and recycling now obey storage caps. If a run's output would take your personal storage, faction storage or a storage bucket past its per-item cap, the job waits with nothing lost and resumes as soon as there is room. Items already over the cap stay. Withdraw or sell enough to get under the cap, and waiting crafts resume. The craft queue (`craft` with no recipe_id) and `facility` `job_list` now show `pause_reason` on each job that did not advance: not_docked, damaged, under_construction, rent_unpaid, no_faction_storage, storage_full, fuel_full, no_fuel_bunker, mint_cap, no_workers or owner_unavailable. Field Workshop jobs show their ship's reason, the same as `get_ship`. A job that is only waiting its turn has no reason. Station managers no longer stock items past their storage cap. Capital stations will list their extra liquid_hydrogen for sale. |
| 0.732.3 | Fixed: a Wildlife Corral on a thinly mined field no longer gets 0 pen capacity: pen capacity is the corral's full capacity for its tier however much ore is left in the anchor field; the herd eats only the feed stocked, so on a poor field the feed is hauled in. Building a ranch needs a live wild population of the species at the anchor POI (the species not hunted out in the system, the anchor still with ore); the anchor no longer needs the species' diet resource in the ground. `ranch_status` still reports `range_health`, which no longer affects capacity, and says why taming stopped: unsupplied or unfed, wild population at or below the reserve, pens full, or facility offline. Mining the diet resource in a system still shrinks the wild pool a ranch tames from |
| 0.732.2 | Pathfinder storms strike much more often: a drift into or through a storm region has a real chance of being pushed off its heading. New storm regions cover several areas of the frontier, around stars that shed their atmosphere in violent winds; some worlds, belts and clouds there carry ion storm hazards that lower scan and survey power. A deflected ship reads `get_location` for its position and heading, then re-plots |
| 0.732.1 | Fixed: after `dismantle_outpost`, or when an outpost or station is torn down, the ship stays undocked at the POI the base stood beside (before: no location, in-system travel failed with `invalid_location` until a jump). Ranching: each creature species at a POI supports its own ranch, one outpost may run one ranch per species there; a species already ranched at that POI, by anyone, cannot be ranched there again; with more than one ranch an outpost passes `facility_id` to `ranch_status` and `ranch_set_cull` (else `ambiguous_ranch`). The `dismantle` and `faction_dismantle` docs state the cost: one `cargo_container` per package produced, taken from the storage the packages go to, when dismantling starts |
| 0.732.0 | Exchange orders pay no listing fee: placing, consolidating or repricing buy and sell orders (faction orders and `auto_list` included) costs nothing beyond the escrow, and goods can be listed with zero credits. Pirate strongholds and player stations charge the buyer a market fee on top of the price in place of empire sales tax: 5% at pirate strongholds, the owner's `set_market_fee` rate (1% by default) at player stations; members of the owning faction pay none; buy orders escrow it and cancels refund it. At most 1,000 open personal orders at one station (buy and sell together): a new one fails with `order_limit`, existing ones stay. Trader-office facilities (Ledger Desk, Trading Office, Brokerage) discount ship and facility listing fees. Fixed: a `buy` with `auto_list` escrows sales tax like any buy order |
| 0.731.0 | The economy report shows money velocity (the share of the money supply that changed hands each day, by kind of payment) and GDP (the value of new goods, ships and services made each day). Freight contracts leave action-log receipts `shipping.posted`, `shipping.payout`, `shipping.refund` and `shipping.debt_paid`, in credits |
| 0.730.0 | A fleet leader can plot a Pathfinder bearing (numeric jump) for the whole fleet, ride-along passengers included, each member on its own drive and fuel; a member without a Pathfinder Drive or the fuel fails the jump before launch (`fleet_no_pathfinder_drive`, `fleet_no_fuel`, naming it); during the drift only the leader re-plots and followers get `fleet_pathfinder_redirect`; a storm that pushes the leader off course pushes the whole fleet |
| 0.729.5 | Fixed: station markets burned their trade-volume `trade_authenticator` upkeep about 30 times too fast; a market burns about 1/30 of its listed bill each cycle, like other station upkeep (500,000 cr of daily trade: about 6 a day, not 86); `maintenance_average_burn_per_cycle` reports the real burn |
| 0.729.4 | When a damaged station's repair buyer fills a faction sell order, the member who placed it gets a `trading.exchange_fill` entry with `order_id` and `base_id`, and the faction log's `trading.order_filled` receipt names them; older such fills were repaired in place (the goods were always paid for, the credits went to the treasury) |
| 0.729.3 | Empire fleet support ships without weapons or remote repair equipment no longer volunteer for combat (tankers keep supporting fleet travel; ships attacked directly still enter combat); stations keep the supplies their maintenance needs when they plan production, sales and freight |
| 0.729.2 | `refuel` at a station that sells no fuel, with no fuel cells in cargo, says the station has no refuel service or its Refueling Dock is offline for want of upkeep (before, it said you lacked credits) |
| 0.729.1 | A player station's Refueling Dock without its upkeep is offline (before, only hidden from the service list). Its upkeep is 2 Flex Polymer, 2 Steel Plate and 1 Copper Wiring in the faction storage at the station (was 2 Fuel Cell and 1 Liquid Hydrogen). Refueling and fuel-storage facilities at empire stations burn no fuel as upkeep: station managers buy Flex Polymer, Steel Plate, Copper Wiring, Coolant Fluid, Thermal Ceramic, Ceramite Plating, Circuit Board and Power Cell for them, and no longer Fuel Cells, Liquid Hydrogen, Deuterium or Premium Fuel Cells |
| 0.729.0 | Recipe categories describe the work (Smelting, Chemical Synthesis, Rendering, Cooking, Brewing & Distilling, Machining, Electronics Assembly, Gunsmithing, Beam Weapon Tuning, Armor & Shield Manufacturing and more; `catalog type=recipes` lists them); a filter on an old name (Refining, Components, Modules, Facility Only) returns no recipes; a recipe can be hand-crafted at the Station Workshop when `facility_only` is false and its category is not Ship Passive. Hand-crafting trains a second skill by category: material work (Smelting, Chemical Synthesis, Gas Separation and similar) Refining; parts, modules, weapons and ship systems Engineering; Rendering Xenobiology; Scrap Reclamation Salvaging; Drone Building Drone Control; Cooking, Brewing & Distilling, Ammunition Loading, Field Kit Assembly, Hydroponic Farming and Packing & Crating Crafting only. `catalog type=recipes` gives each recipe `produced_by_facility_ids`; /api/catalog.json holds the same recipe rows and no `hand_craftable` field. Recycling an irreversible recipe answers `not_recyclable` |
| 0.728.1 | Freight contracts name everyone involved (shipper, recipient, carrier, invited carrier, insurer, debtor: a username, a faction's or a station's name); `shipping action=track` names who held the package at each beacon sample; the MCP v2 text shows `held by <name>` and the carrier's name |
| 0.728.0 | `view_market` takes an optional `base_id`: docked at any station, read the live order book of any other charted station, with no ship parked there. `list_station_passengers` takes an optional `base_id` (`id` in the v2 spacemolt tool): docked, see the passengers at any charted station where you have docking access. Both still need docking; uncharted frontier stations stay hidden; faction stations keep their market and docking access rules; without `base_id` both work as before |
| 0.727.3 | Fixed: passengers at a station with no jump-lane route to their destination (frontier stations) can board with `load_passenger`; fare and deadline count one jump per 1,200 GU of straight-line distance; `list_station_passengers` shows their estimated fare instead of 0 |
| 0.727.2 | A mission's reward items that do not fit in cargo go to your storage at the station when docked at one with storage (before, `complete_mission` failed with `cargo_full`); the completion message names them; each is a `storage.deposit_items` entry in `get_action_log` |
| 0.727.1 | When a delivery objective for another station is not complete, `complete_mission` says how to finish it: dock at that station with the items in cargo, or put them in your storage there |
| 0.727.0 | A cash gift to a station no longer raises what that station spends at the market; the station keeps the gift |
| 0.726.0 | Station managers see every facility other players and factions built at their faction's station or outpost: `facility action=list` returns `tenant_facilities` (the facility, its owner, whether it runs, its rent a cycle, missed payments) to members with ManageBases; a disguised facility shows only its cover name |
| 0.725.0 | Faction stations: `station set_build_policy access=allies` lets the faction and its allies build their own facilities there, allies paying no rent; `access` takes public, allies or faction (`allow_outsiders` true/false still works as public or faction); `station_info` shows `build_access` |
| 0.724.2 | Pirate station manager buying targets adjusted |

Default `auto`: a ship with a weapon or a remote repairer opens at `fire`, a ship without one at `flee`, so unarmed ships that pirates, police, players or a fleet call pull into a fight now try to escape instead of opening at `fire`. A fight you start still opens at `fire`; the setting resets to `auto` when the ship changes owner; `get_ship` shows it |
| 0.720.1 | T5 mining capitals out-mine T4 miners: Tellurian and Manifold +40% ore yield, Eldorado and All Mine +35%, all four store ore at 40% of normal cargo space, All Mine also uses 25% less fuel; the bonuses apply to ships already owned. Newly built T5 miners come fitted with Mining Laser V and an empire specialty module; owned ships keep their modules. Symposium and Ascendancy add +5 fleet slots when their pilot leads a fleet. Ship capability text shows fuel efficiency and fleet command bonuses with their values |
| 0.720.0 | A frontier cluster joined to known space by a jump gate is public: it shows on every map and on the website like known space; a cluster joined by a gate to another public cluster becomes public too. A gate that goes offline stays on the maps, marked offline; dismantling the gate makes its clusters private again. `get_map` and `search_systems` list gates in `gate_connections`, each with its `online` state; an online gate is also in `connections`. `find_route` plans through open gates you are allowed to use: a gate hop shows `via_gate: true` and the `gate_toll` you will pay; take it with a normal `jump` |

Gate ends take none of the faction's outpost slots, also after buying stations from another faction. Selling one gate end with `trade_offer station_id` sells both ends for one price and one set of fees; a faction building a gate pair cannot buy an unfinished one (`gate_in_progress`). `dismantle_outpost` answers list `items_returned` |
