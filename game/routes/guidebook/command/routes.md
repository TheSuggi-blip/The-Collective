# Command guidebook -- Routes (routes.md)

### Standing Plan
Status: proven. Updated: 2026-11-21. Evidence: the plan file (tool plan, show); fill.py status command; hive.py standing Unity.

Turn of 2026-11-21 21:30 UTC (orders ENT-057). The six flagships take
the list First (the missions paid once), then their own routes: each a
list of far hunts of grazers (one ship a herd, `max_ships` 1), the
soma sales and carries where a bid stands, then the shared hunts and
missions, then the steel crafts from own stores; `park` last. They
hunt for Combat and Piloting and lead groups when Military flies one
(rule F10); Leadership and Tactics rise only by leading, which no
route flies yet.

Prime Directive is the faction's leader and the sweep's leader: the
sweep's gifts collect in its wallet and it deposits into the
treasury between its runs. A deposit is refused while it fights
(`in_battle`), so its hunts delay the deposit by a pass or two, no
more. What would change the plan: a group hunt that kills (a
flagship leads it), a command hull at the next tier, or a hunt list
whose herds read below what the route needs (`carries`).
