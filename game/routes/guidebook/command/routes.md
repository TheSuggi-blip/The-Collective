### Standing Plan
Status: proven. Updated: 2026-11-22. Evidence: the plan file (tool plan, show); fill.py status command; hive.py standing Unity; workclass.py sales.

Turn of 2026-11-22 01:30 UTC (orders ENT-061). The six flagships take
the list First (the missions paid once), then their own routes: a test
the filler adds in front (three ships at most, rule F37), then far
hunts of grazers (one ship a herd, `max_ships` 1), the soma sales and
carries where a bid stands, the shared hunts and missions, the steel
crafts from own stores; `store-hold` and `park` last. A flagship that
stands at a board with a mission it has not flown gets that mission
first. They hunt for Combat and Piloting and lead groups when Military
flies one (rule F10); Leadership and Tactics rise only by leading.

Prime Directive is the faction's leader and the sweep's leader: the
sweep's gifts collect in its wallet and it deposits between its runs.
The group's wallets stand near 30,000 since it paid the Atria kit. Ten
of its plan rows still sell and seven craft: under strict classes (P5)
they become gifts or leave the plan. What would change the plan: a
group hunt that kills, a command hull at the next tier, or herds that
read below what a route needs (`carries`).
