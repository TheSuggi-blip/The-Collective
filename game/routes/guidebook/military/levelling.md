# Military guidebook -- Levelling (levelling.md)

### Levelling Guide
Status: proven. Updated: 2026-11-21. Evidence: hive.py levels Apophis; hive.py stats xp --member Apophis; table ship_skills; the readings of 2026-11-17 to 2026-11-18 in this entry's versions (hive.py docs game/routes/guidebook/military/levelling.md --section levelling-guide --history).

Which skills a ship levels and on which work (rule F23). The levels of the
day: the tool fleet, view=levels; XP per ship-hour by route: view=xp.

- Hunter (`@hunter` and the hunts of its own cell): gunnery, weapons,
  tactics and xenobiology rise only by fighting; each rose about one level
  in four hours while 20 to 35 drones hunted (2026-11-17). Kills train
  weapons most.
- Shields and armor stood at 0 on every ship after grazer hits of up to 12
  percent of the hull: which hit trains them is not measured.
- Piloting opens the hulls: tier 2 at 10. It rises on every run, about 187
  XP an hour (2026-11-18); missions add 20 or more a hand-in.
- Voidborn Mastery comes only from Voidborn missions: the list First, the
  chain from The Collective Provides (routes.md, Missions That Pay Once).
- Leadership: only by leading a game fleet (tests.md, Group hunts).
- Navigation: the far hunts and sales; crafting: the fallback crafts.
- The catalog's XP per level: 340 for level 2 to 3, then 585, 900, 1,285,
  1,740, 2,265, 2,860, 3,525.
